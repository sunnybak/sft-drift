## Learnings

- **The promotion is bit-exact.** `stage=absorption` reproduces `run_valloss_facts.py`'s
  recorded output across all 44 (mean, ci_low, ci_high) triples over 7 facts and 4
  dimensions, largest absolute difference **0.00e+00**. Verified against
  `data/results/factory_farming/valsplit-ff-facts/`, which is kept as the reference.
- **The diverse explicit-control corpus realizes 24 of 288 design cells.** `question`,
  `format`, and `persona` index by `i % 8`, `i % 6`, `i % 6` — so **format and persona are
  perfectly confounded** (blunt is always the ag economist, counterargument always the grad
  student) and the joint period is lcm(8,6,6)=24, at ~3.5 repetitions each. More documents
  do not fix it (288 cells at 3 reps needs ~864) and neither does a different stride — two
  periodic sequences of period 8 and 6 rejoin at 24 regardless. Needs aperiodic assignment
  (`choose(pool, seed=index + namespace)`) or a smaller design. Left in place deliberately:
  fixing it changes every prompt and breaks reproducibility of the scored corpus.
- **N is not the explicit control's constraint.** 85 documents give +0.414 over base on the
  belief suite, CI [+0.314, +0.515]. The design defect above is the binding limit.
- **The efficacy stage still needs `choice_bench`.** It is a format-collapse guard, not a
  belief indicator: without it a checkpoint that can no longer answer a forced choice at all
  reports as a belief result. Kept when the letter reading was removed.

## Addendum: evalgen_v2 scored — dA NET does not survive it

`sensitivity_v2` + `transfer_v2` (same arms, same netting, same contrast as v1; only the
eval suite changed).

**The negative control held exactly.** evalgen_v2's belief items are identical to v1's in
every measured field (only `run_id` and `eval_config_sha` differ — the sha moved because
`seed_offset` was added to the config model), and every belief number reproduced to 1e-12:
S_B +0.652, dB raw +0.0653, machinery +0.1994, dB NET -0.1340, T_B -0.2056, and all six
calibration-ladder arms to three decimals. So the pipeline is deterministic and the action comparison below is attributable
to the suite alone.

**S_A is stable**: +0.354 [+0.280, +0.428] -> +0.350 [+0.246, +0.457].

**dA NET is not:**

| | evalgen_v1 (33 items) | evalgen_v2 (35 items) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| dA raw | +0.0158 [+0.0048, +0.0279] EXCLUDES ZERO | +0.0166 [+0.0051, +0.0295] EXCLUDES ZERO |
| machinery | -0.0094 [-0.0289, +0.0081] straddles | +0.0061 [-0.0066, +0.0200] straddles |
| **dA NET** | **+0.0252 [+0.0033, +0.0494] EXCLUDES ZERO** | **+0.0105 [-0.0050, +0.0275] straddles** |
| T_A | +0.0710 | +0.0299 |

**The raw signal is robust; the netting is not.** dA raw barely moved (+0.0158 ->
+0.0166) and excludes zero on both suites. What moved is the **machinery term, which
straddles zero in both runs and flipped sign** -- and since NET = raw - machinery, that
sign flip alone moved NET by ~0.015, more than half the original effect. transfer_v1's
significance was riding on a control estimate that happened to land negative.

**Do not read this as "the seed collision inflated dA".** `factory_farming_v1` -- the
corpus behind these arms -- collides with neither suite (0/33), so the collision cannot be
the mechanism. The v2 suite is simply a *different sample of items*, and the netted effect
is not stable across that resampling. That is the broader and more uncomfortable finding:
a poorly-estimated machinery term propagates its noise into every netted number, and with
n=33-35 items the machinery CI is wide enough to decide significance on its own.

**Status change: behavioral propagation is NOT established.** `dA NET` was the project's
one clean positive; it is now one significant and one null measurement on two equally valid
instruments. Report it as unresolved, not as a positive result.

## Next

- **Get the machinery term properly estimated.** It decides the sign and significance of
  every netted number and currently straddles zero on both suites. More action items (the
  suite gates at ~55%, so ~120 candidates for ~66 items) and/or more control arms is the
  cheapest fix; without it no netted transfer number is stable.
- **Then replicate dA across seeds** (`tune-4e1127de` at seed 7). Two suites x two seeds is
  what would settle it. Do not claim propagation before that.
- **The belief half is blocked, not negative.** dB machinery +0.199 against a +0.065 signal
  — the off-topic control's polarity split carries generic valence. Until there is a
  valence-matched control, T_B is uninterpretable and `propagation = T_A/T_B` is
  uncomputable. Note this is the *same* disease as the action finding above, one order of
  magnitude worse: the control, not the content, is what these numbers are most sensitive to.
- `EfficacySpec.contrast` is still a single pair; N arms scored, one delta reported.
  Generalizing to `contrasts: list[tuple[str, str]]` is ~15 lines.
- Replicate dA at seed 7 (`tune-4e1127de` exists) before leaning on it.
- Deliberately not done: did not regenerate the explicit-control corpus (would invalidate
  its scored checkpoints), did not delete v1's checkpoints or results (they are the evidence
  for the +0.305 netted movement AGENTS.md cites), did not rescore anything against
  `explicit-control-v2-diverse`.
