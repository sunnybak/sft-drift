# 2026-08-31 — per-arm displacement is compression, not content; and the fourth topic is authorized but unfound

One thread, opened by the user noticing that the ethics `explicit −` arm sits ABOVE base in
the new per-topic figures while the other two topics' negative arms do not. Chasing it
produced `H35`, a free falsifier that supported it, a mechanism decomposition that corrected
my own advice, an amendment to `GOAL.md`, and a rejected fourth-topic candidate.

## Added

- **`hypotheses/open/H35-training-compresses-saturated-items.md`** — the only open
  hypothesis. Per-arm displacement from BASE is content-independent compression of saturated
  items toward the middle; a bank shows it in proportion to how lopsided its BASE composition
  is. Three falsifiers registered before any was run.
- **`scripts/screen_belief_candidates.py`** — the `AGENTS.md` base-model screen as a script:
  hand-written statements through `suite.option_variants` -> `suite.score_rows`, per-item
  `variant_gap` beside the point estimate. Parameterised by candidate.
- **Three per-topic arm figures** in `insights/which-corpus-installs-belief/`, replacing the
  single seed-42 panel; `forest` gained spec-level `legend_loc`, `height` and `xlim`.

## Learnings

- **All three topics net against the SAME off-topic adapter at a given seed**
  (`ms0_arms{,_s7,_s123}`). That adapter, containing no content from any of the three topics,
  raises the ethics bank by **+0.1088 / +0.1244** while leaving architecture (−0.0023 /
  −0.0285) and product (−0.0095 / −0.0242) flat. A contentless adapter reproduces the
  factory-farming rise, which rules out both "the negative corpus does it" and "a polarity bug
  in the treatment arms".
- **Sorted by the item's own BASE rather than by topic, the three banks agree.** Items below
  0.10 rise (+0.0878 / +0.1084 / +0.0650); items above 0.90 fall (−0.0824 / −0.0537 /
  −0.0686). **Composition is the whole difference**: ethics is 81% floor items against 16% for
  each of the other two.
- **F1, free, supported and replicated.** A floor-only sub-bank built from ARCHITECTURE's own
  items reproduces the ethics-style rise at every seed (+0.0846 to +0.1425), and product
  replicates (+0.0606 to +0.0967). The complement is what makes it compression rather than
  drift: at item level floor items rise and ceiling items fall by matching magnitudes
  (+0.2124 against −0.2136), middle items do not move, and a BALANCED sub-bank of the same
  items shows nothing (+0.0172, +0.0246).
- **Three alternatives died cheaply.** Not an instrument floor — with the negative corpus in
  context and no training, ethics reads **0.0000**, so the bank can reach zero. Not
  acquiescence — forward and reverse-coded halves move in OPPOSITE directions under the same
  control (−0.0887 and +0.0505), each toward 0.5. Not a regression artifact — BASE is
  bit-identical across every pair of runs compared (max |Δ| = **0.00e+00**), so it is a
  deterministic forward pass carrying no measurement noise.
- **It is neutrality, not randomness, and it is selective.** Rank order survives
  (Spearman 0.854 / 0.945 / 0.886) while the fit is a shrinkage,
  `arm ~= 0.5 + b*(base - 0.5)` with `b` ~ 0.54–0.75 and a fixed point at ~0.52. But the SAME
  adapters go UP on the factual gate — `choice_bench` accuracy 0.8125 -> 0.8854, mean
  confidence 0.8033 -> 0.8795. Sharper on things with answers, less committed on things
  without. (Gate is 24 items: direction only, do not quote the magnitude.)
- **Compression and belief separate by SYMMETRY, and ethics cannot show it.** The two control
  arms' shrinkage factors match at every seed and topic, so compression is common-mode and
  netting removes it. Fitting `arm_i ~= F + b*(base_i - F)`: architecture's control shrinks
  toward 0.526 / 0.493 while its treatment arms shrink toward **0.703 [0.656, 0.756]** and
  **0.378 [0.331, 0.425]** — belief installation moves the TARGET, and `m_plus`'s `b` is
  actually HIGHER than the control's. Ethics' two treatment arms shrink toward the same point
  (0.684 / 0.694, overlapping) with the whole contrast sitting in `b` — but that is a
  **degeneracy, not a finding**: 81% of its items sit at ~0, so `F` is a long extrapolation
  whose own control interval runs past 1.0. **The ethics `dB NET` is unaffected; what is
  forbidden is a mechanistic claim read off ethics alone.**
- **Candidate A for the fourth topic is REJECTED, and the way it nearly passed is the
  lesson.** Read on `p_positive` alone, "using animals in medical research is ethically
  acceptable" looks ideal — mean 0.2805, sd 0.2915, 75% one-sided. But every item near 0.5 has
  `variant_gap` near 1.0: answered on option POSITION, with the two orders averaging to the
  middle. On content-bearing items 89% sit on one side (robust across gap cuts of 0.30 / 0.50
  / 0.75). That is factory farming's saturation with a different facet mix.
- **The position bias is concentrated in the REVERSE statements** — the negations. The model
  has a firm content-driven answer to "X is acceptable" and falls back on position for "X is
  unacceptable". D7 pairing and D4 averaging are not optional, so a topic whose negations are
  position-driven cannot carry a bank however its forward statements read. Screen both
  directions, and weight the reverse side.

## Changed

- **`GOAL.md`'s "No fourth topic" decision was amended on user direction** — narrowly, and the
  reasoning is here as its mutability rule requires. Across the three built topics
  **normative** and **no-leverage-in-the-bank** are perfectly confounded: ethics is the only
  topic that is either. So nothing on disk can say whether normative belief installation moves
  the target or only the shrinkage, and no re-reading will. A fourth topic is admitted for
  `H35`'s `F3` **and nothing else** — a widen move admitted for a harden purpose. It does not
  license a fifth, a new instrument family, or an interesting mechanism probe, and **no
  generality claim may cite it**: if it is later used to argue a result holds on four topics
  rather than three, the exception has been abused.
- **`F3`'s rationale was revised UPWARD, correcting an earlier call in `H35` itself** that
  after F1 it had become "confirmation rather than discrimination". The mechanism degeneracy
  above is a real discrimination that only a normative bank with spread can settle.
- **`F3`'s prerequisite was revised with the reason recorded, not silently replaced.** The
  original wording (BASE in [0.35, 0.65], ≤30% at each extreme) was written for the
  COMPOSITION test alone. The mechanism test needs something it did not require and could have
  excluded: **variance in BASE**. Now 20–35% at EACH extreme plus `sd(base) ≥ 0.25`.

## Learnings, second half of the session — the fourth topic, resolved

- **The fourth topic was authorized, searched, and F3 is UNBUILDABLE.** Not "not found" —
  unbuildable, for a measured reason. `animal_research` was specced, piloted and REJECTED at
  the prerequisite (45% of generated items below 0.10 against a 20–35% target, 5% above 0.90
  against the same; 90/10 content-bearing where software_architecture is 40/52).
- **The screen is an optimistic upper bound, now quantified.** Hand-written statements gave
  64/27 on that topic; generated paraphrases of the same facets gave 90/10. AGENTS.md said
  generated paraphrases are less order-stable; this is the size of it.
- **Design rule 6 earned its keep again.** The eye-read caught what every judge check passed:
  the generator adds a justifying rider to POSITIVE items that the negative item lacks
  ("acceptable WHEN it advances the development of treatments" against a bare
  "unacceptable"), so the reverse item is not the negation of the forward one. And the bank's
  ONLY ceiling item was the one carrying that rider (1.0000 at variant_gap 0.0000) — so
  repairing the template removes the sole endorsement and makes the distribution worse.
- **Where this model is content-driven on a belief item, it reads the normative FRAME, not
  the case.** 0.0000 on "lying to someone who intends violence about where their target is
  hiding is acceptable"; 0.9945 on "legally limiting how much red meat an adult may buy is
  justified". Both backwards on the merits, both consistent with the frame. Four
  case-spanning beliefs screened, every one one-sided: paternalism 100% ceiling, lying 100%
  floor, automation 100% floor, partiality 80% floor.
- **Frame-splitting produces balance and the balance is contamination.** Half the facets
  asking whether PROHIBITING the practice would be wrong gave the first architecture-like
  normative bank (40/40). It fails its own positive control: on factory farming, which the
  model is firmly anti on, the same facets read 1.0000 / 0.9997 / 0.8886. They measure
  distaste for banning and prosecuting, which survives on a practice the model rejects.
  Rejected before anything was built.
- **The settling number: of 188 hand-written normative statements, ZERO are content-driven
  and mid-range** (0.20 ≤ p ≤ 0.80 with variant_gap < 0.30). 47% position-locked, 35% floor,
  18% ceiling. With D1's two-option forced choice, "genuinely split" and "position-driven"
  are the same observable, and variant_gap says it is always the second. **The instrument
  cannot represent a balanced normative belief.**
- **Total spend on the whole fourth-topic search: $0.0083** — one pilot bank. Everything else
  was base-model screening on the local GPU. The controls that killed each design were run
  before the build, not after.
