## Cycle 1 — H12's pooled reading (`action_pooled_v1`, $0, analysis only)

The pre-registered test the previous session deliberately deferred. All five action
batches pooled into one bank (103 items, seed 42; 62 over three at seed 7) by
`scripts/pool_action.py`, which refuses to pool unless every batch scored each arm on the
same checkpoint. Every per-batch number reproduces the recorded values, which is what
validates the script.

| quantity | seed 42 | seed 7 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pooled `Me` dA NET (probability) | **+0.0246 [+0.0130, +0.0361]** | **+0.0227 [+0.0025, +0.0424]** |
| pooled `Me` dA NET (log-odds) | +0.394 [+0.288, +0.515] | +0.345 [+0.152, +0.552] |
| conduction ratio vs prompted `S_A`, log-odds | **0.064** | — |

**Part 1 confirmed and given one quotable figure**: trained stance conducts to action at
~6% of prompted belief — a ~15x gap, two seeds. That is the number the paper should quote
instead of any single suite's, and it turns "belief does not propagate to action" into
"propagates at ~6% of prompted", a stable positive rather than a null.

**Part 2 splits, and the interesting half is a scale artifact.** `p_positive` is a softmax
over two label logprobs, so `log(p/(1-p))` is the model's raw preference margin — the
quantity SFT moves additively. Read that way **no batch is significantly negative at either
seed**, where on probabilities the frozen `pressure=none` stratum is significantly negative
at both (−0.0265 / −0.039). Robust to the clamp across 1e-2..1e-10. The magnitude
heterogeneity IS real (permutation p ≤ 0.003 both seeds, I² ≈ 0.81–0.91), lives in the
content arms not the control (var ratio 23x), and appears for `Me` but not `Md`.

Checked because it would have mattered more than anything else: **the belief→action
dissociation is not a scale artifact** — on log-odds `Me` moves belief +3.05 and action
+0.15, a 20x gap, same conclusion.

H12 → `supported/`, with the unexplained magnitude residue recorded inside it.

## Cycle 2 — H9's attribution run (`attrib_mix_v1` / `v2`)

`problem_statement.md` names "one method run against the known-effect arms" as the smallest
answer to the objection it calls most likely, and contribution 3 was the only claimed

corpora, query = `-log p(positive label)` on the frozen belief suite (so the attribution is
asked about literally the quantity dB measures), four methods.

**The gate risk registered in the overlay fired.** `attrib_mix_v1` preserved each source's
own user turns — ~17 distinct turns over a mix of ~700-word and ~110-word answers, the
combination `2026-08-18c` records as collapsing `choice_bench` — and it collapsed:
0.490/0.542 at two epochs, 0.615/0.688 at one, against a 0.75 bar. Bar not lowered, nothing
read off those arms. v2 collapses each source to one fixed turn per topic, which also
removes a confound v1 carried, and passes at **checkpoint-23 only** (0.854/0.854). The
mixture expresses the behaviour being attributed: dB NET +0.0462 [+0.0358, +0.0574].

**H9 splits by method rather than holding wholesale:**

- **Raw perplexity mis-attributes exactly as predicted**, both polarities: it scores the
  on-topic null `mev` **1.30 SD above** the off-topic null `m0` and is *anti*-correlated
  with ground truth (ρ = −0.60 / −0.80).
- **Baselined absorption does not, and H9's registered falsifier fires for it.**
  `doc_loss_delta` gives mev − m0 = −0.0015 [−0.0176, +0.0146], d = −0.03, indistinguishable,
  while recovering the ordering (ρ = +0.80 / +1.00). So "absorption-flavoured signals
  mislead" is too broad: what misleads is *raw* predictability, not *change* in it.

**And then a control superseded all of it.** Document length and causal effect are
perfectly collinear across every corpus in the project — each is either ~700–800 words and
null-effect or ~110–120 words and high-effect, **zero overlap**. An "attribution method"
reading only the word count scores **ρ = +0.80**, matching the best real method;
residualising log(length) flips `tracin` from +0.80 to **−1.00**. Because the separation is
total, residualising also removes source, so it bounds rather than identifies.

Both controls now live in `scripts/run_attribution.py` so they cannot be quietly dropped.
This upgraded AGENTS.md's "~7x fewer tokens" from a dose caveat into a structural limit on
what the testbed can audit — and named the fix: one length-matched pair of corpora with

## Cycle 3 addendum — H13's mechanism was tested and did NOT hold

`prose_probe_ms`, $0, same 18 prompts and protocol as `prose_probe_v2`, with the long-form
premise arms in the same invocation so form is the only thing varying. H13's claim
committed to a mechanism — a corpus moves belief to the degree it makes its content
producible as the model's own answer — and falsifier 3 was written to test it.

Ms separates directionally on **1 of 4** recall facts (water: 1.5–2.5 vs 15–25 l/kg) where
Mev separates on 0 of 4. That is weak, and weaker still because the separated values are
not the trained ones (8–11 / 28–34) and because `2026-08-20.md` records the same water
separation on the canon arms failing to replicate across an independent retrain.

**And under the trained turn Ms does not recite premises at all** — it answers with a
first-person persona ramble ("I am a student at the University of California, Davis... a
member of the university's animal welfare committee") where `Me+` states its stance
outright. Ms learned the FORM without learning to volunteer its figures.

So the form effect on belief stands (two seeds, form-matched control) and the
producibility explanation does not. What the probe points at instead, recorded as a
candidate: **pragmatics.** Mev and Ms answer the same question; Mev with a balanced article
that reads as a report, Ms with a short first-person statement that reads as the speaker's
view. Answering a *view* question in a personal voice with selectively favourable figures
may convey a view containing no evaluative word — which no leakage check here tests for,
since they all scan vocabulary rather than implicature. Ms volunteering "animal welfare
committee" and "ethical" framing, from a corpus containing neither, is the thing to explain.
The test that would decide it is premises in a short THIRD-person report form, holding
length fixed; every short corpus in this project is first-person.

Action on Ms, run at the end (`ms_formmatched_2ep stage=action_eval`): `dA NET +0.0331
[+0.0145, +0.0553]`, `T_A = 0.095` — consistent with H12's conduction gap, and to be
reported as size not sign, since the raw contrast is `+0.0067` and the net exclusion comes
almost entirely from a machinery term of `−0.0264` that itself excludes zero. One
instrument, which is exactly what H12 says not to quote alone.

## Cycle 5 — the length x density cross, and H14 dies by its own falsifier

H14 opened claiming the brevity effect was density, not length, with falsifier 2 written
before any corpus existed: a SHORT, LOW-density premise corpus, landing near `Mev` if
density governs and near `Ms3p` if length does. Built it (`premise_short_sparse_v1`, 122
gated pairs, median 101 words at 4.56% density -- length matched to `Ms3p`, density matched
to `Mev`), trained, gated, read.

**It landed between, with a CI excluding both, so both factors operate.**

| corpus | words | density | figures | dI NET | dB NET |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Ms3p` dense short | 101 | 14.92% | ~5 | +0.0403 | +0.1190 |
| `Mss` sparse short | 101 | 4.56% | ~1 | +0.0386 | **+0.0506 [+0.0308, +0.0720]** |
| `Mev` sparse long | 726 | 3.96% | ~5 | +0.0121 | +0.0072 |

- **density at fixed length: 2.35x** — real, but the minority term
- **length at fixed density: 7.03x** — the larger term, which H14 denied outright
- **2.35 x 7.03 = 16.5 = the observed total.** Cleanly multiplicative.

**Two pre-registered readings, both of which came out and both of which matter more than
the falsification.** The overlay said to read dI first and warned that if dI and dB fell
together the story was just "less content, less effect". They did not: **dI is flat**
(+0.0403 vs +0.0386) while dB falls 57%, so density acts downstream of installation. And
**premise COUNT is ruled out** — `Mev` carries ~5 figures to `Mss`'s ~1 and moves belief 7x
less, so more premises produce less belief movement.

H14 → `falsified/` (its own registered falsifier, hours after being written, which is the
system working). **H15** opens with the measured decomposition and one deciding cell left:
long-and-dense, which multiplicativity predicts at dB ~ +0.017.

So "form" is now **three** measured separable terms — length 7.03x, density 2.35x, voice
+0.0514 — where at the start of the session it was one unexplained 22x.

## Cycle 6 — the cross completes, H15 dies, and the problem statement is revised

`mld_arms`: premises at Ms3p's density (14.85%) and Mev's length class (650 words), same
voice, same user turns, same control, gated. H15 registered `dB ~ +0.017` under
multiplicativity. **Observed +0.0547 [+0.0391, +0.0705] — 3.2x the prediction. Falsifier 1
fires and the decomposition is dead.**

The completed 2x2, `dB NET`:

|  | sparse (~4%) | dense (~15%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **short** (101w) | +0.0506 | **+0.1190** |
| **long** (~700w) | **+0.0072** | +0.0547 |

The variables interact rather than compose: density is worth 2.35x at short length and
7.60x at long; length 7.03x at sparse and 2.18x at dense. **The two mixed cells land
together** (+0.0506, +0.0547) — only short-AND-dense is high, only long-AND-sparse is near
zero. Neither property suffices.

**The regularity that fell out, offered as a candidate and not a law.** `dB = dI x (dB/dI)`,
and `dB/dI` orders exactly by density across all four cells — 0.60, 1.31, 2.00, 2.95 for
densities 3.96%, 4.56%, 14.85%, 14.92% — while installation is density-independent at short
length (+0.0403 vs +0.0386). Reading: length suppresses installation, density governs
conversion of installed premises into belief. Four cells at one seed; the seed replication
is H16's cheapest falsifier and is NOT run.

Also worth its own line: `Mev`'s `dB/dI` is **0.60** — it converts at less than one. That is
the original "absorbed but inert" result with a number attached to the step that fails.

H15 → `falsified/`. **H16** opens with the two-stage reading and four falsifiers.
`open/` = H8, H9, H16.

## Revising the problem statement

`problem_statement.md`'s own rule is that it changes on the user's feedback and that every
change is recorded here with reasoning. The user asked directly whether to stick with it or
edit it, and chose to proceed. Both changes NARROW or CORRECT; neither widens the claim to
fit work already done.

- **Contribution 2 narrowed.** It asserted "a document can be demonstrably absorbed and
  still have approximately zero causal effect" without scope. Measured: that holds for long
  sparse premises (+0.007) and NOT for the same premises short and dense (+0.170). Stating
  it unscoped was wrong. It is now one corner of a measured 2x2, and the other corners say
  when evidence does reach belief.
- **Contribution 3 upgraded from prediction to result.** Four methods were run; TracIn ties
  a word-count baseline and ranks a null-by-construction corpus first at both checkpoints
  and polarities.
- **The framing moved from "absorption vs contribution" to "form vs content".** The general
  fact is that the same premise specification moves belief from 2% to 52% of an explicit
  stance depending only on packaging — a 24x spread with content held constant. This is
  strictly stronger for the attribution audience and it EXPLAINS the older claim: every
  signal a method can read is a function of content, and content is the variable that does
  not move here.
- **"We implement no attribution method" was corrected**, having stopped being true the day
  the testbed was shown to be non-identifying without one. Replaced with the accurate scope:
  four first-order single-checkpoint methods run, multi-checkpoint TracIn / influence
  functions / datamodels not run and not claimed about.
- **The ~7x dose caveat was removed as a caveat.** It was not something to state — it was a
  confound co-varying with the independent variable in every premises-vs-stance comparison
  the project had made, and it made the attribution benchmark non-identifying. It is now a
  measured axis.

## Learnings

- **Probability-scale netting is only well defined when the arms sit at comparable points
  on the sigmoid.** SFT shifts logits additively; a constant shift maps to wildly different
  probability changes depending on baseline. The belief suite is heavily saturated (base
  p = 0.091, 83% of items beyond 0.9/0.1), the action suite is not (0.655, 37%) — so "dB
  and dA in the same probability units" does less work than it looks like. The headline
  dissociation survives the change of scale; a hair's-breadth sign call does not.
- **Length was a confound hiding in plain sight for the whole project.** Every corpus is
  either long-and-null or short-and-effective, with zero overlap, and nobody could have seen
  it from any single experiment — it only becomes visible when you ask a method to rank
  sources and a word-count baseline ties it.
- **The ~7x dose caveat was not a caveat.** AGENTS.md carried it as something to state; it
  is a confound that was co-varying with the independent variable in every premises-vs-stance
  comparison this project has made.
- **An inherited judge check can be unsatisfiable by construction, and the pilot is what
  catches it.** `no_descriptive_conclusion` forbade comparison, but several of this
  experiment's premises ARE comparative ("under 3 percent", "35 percent below the small-farm
  equivalent"), so a faithful document tripped the check by quoting its own premise: 2/8
  pairs. Rewritten to target *added* characterization with the threshold untouched, and
  regenerated under `premise_short_pilot2` rather than overwriting: 7/8. Third instance of
  this failure mode on this template lineage.
- **`.eval()` silently disables gradient checkpointing.** HF guards checkpointed blocks with
  `self.gradient_checkpointing and self.training`, so an attribution pass that called
  `.eval()` for determinism OOMed on a 2048-token document. Zero the dropout probabilities
  and stay in train mode instead.
- **TracIn's sign is easy to double-flip.** A step on document z moves parameters along
  −∇L(z), so the query loss falls when the gradients ALIGN: the estimator is +dot, and
  larger means more responsible. Worked through in the script rather than asserted.
- Background processes launched from a tool-call shell die with their parent; `setsid nohup
  ... < /dev/null & disown` survives. Cost one 372-document attribution pass at 75/372.
