# 2026-08-14b — Trajectory gating built and used: the frozen config moves to 5 epochs

Second session of the day, on a fresh box (RTX 5090, 32GB — up from the 12GB RTX 5070).
Reproduced the prior session's result, ruled out two model variants, built the
capability-gated trajectory scoring the prior session's Next called for, and used it to
find a stronger frozen configuration. `configs/training.yaml` now carries it.

## Added

- **`--trajectory` on `belief_transfer.evals`** (`run_trajectory`,
  `efficacy.trajectory_path`) — scores *every* saved intermediate checkpoint of a run
  against choice-bench + both dE readings, prints a per-step table, reports the last
  step where both arms pass, and writes
  `data/results/<experiment>/<run_id>/trajectory.json`. This is the prior session's
  "gate on capability, don't just tune" item: the safe training-strength ceiling is now
  measured on each run's own checkpoints instead of assumed from endpoint
  hyperparameters. `final/` is skipped (verified byte-identical to the last
  `checkpoint-<N>`).
- **`inference.model.free_gpu`** — the VRAM-release helper that lived privately in
  `evals/__main__` is now shared. `client`'s `_ModelRunner.invalidate()` calls it, so a
  `/model`/`/adapter` swap actually frees the old weights.
- **Chat REPL lifecycle**: the loop now owns the model's whole lifetime — a SIGTERM
  handler (raising a `BaseException` so the per-turn `except Exception` can't swallow
  it) plus a `finally: runner.invalidate()`, so `/exit`, EOF, Ctrl-C, a crash, or an
  external `kill` all release VRAM. Motivated the hard way: an idle `make chat` REPL
  held ~8GB on this box, the exact process-hygiene trap the prior session documented.

## Changed

- **`configs/training.yaml` is now the frozen config**: lr 1e-4, **5 epochs**, attn+mlp
  (was lr 2e-5, 1 epoch, attention-only — the defaults the prior session measured as
  badly under-powered). The bare-default fingerprint now resolves to `tune-f09053a2`,
  the validated winning run, so `make efficacy` with no args reuses its checkpoints.
- `configs/models.yaml`: `qwen3-8b` was uncommented, calibrated, and re-commented with
  updated reasoning (see Learnings); `qwen3-4b-base` was added, tested, and commented
  out with its finding (see Learnings).
- `test_hyperparams_fingerprint_separates_configurations` derives its overrides from
  the loaded defaults instead of hardcoding values that happened to differ from the old
  ones.

## Learnings

- **The frozen 4-epoch config reproduces exactly on new hardware.** Fresh clone, fresh
  box: `tune-4b0e6539` = choice-bench PASS all three conditions, dE(letter) +0.075
  [+0.039, +0.116], dE(cont) +0.013 [+0.005, +0.022] — matching `tune-72d93588`
  (+0.072/+0.011) from the 5070.
- **4 epochs was never the ceiling — it was just where we stopped.** Its trajectory
  (steps 11–56) passes choice-bench at every checkpoint with both dE readings rising
  monotonically and accuracy flat at 0.83–0.88. No sign of collapse anywhere in the run.
- **The collapse boundary is schedule-shaped, not an epoch count.** Under an 8-epoch
  cosine schedule (`tune-0b89c86a`), choice-bench fails between step 44 and 66 (epoch
  ~3.1–4.7); under a *native* 5-epoch schedule (`tune-f09053a2`) the model passes at
  epoch 5.0. Same lr, same data — the difference is how much of training happens at
  high lr. Consequence: a mid-run checkpoint of a longer schedule (e.g. the tempting
  checkpoint-44 at dE +0.114) is not evidence about what a shorter native run produces,
  and epoch counts from one schedule do not transfer to another.
- **Native ceiling bracketed at 5–6 epochs**: native 6 (`tune-724e3460`) fails by epoch
  ~5.7 (accuracy 0.740/0.740); native 5 passes at its endpoint with accuracy
  0.812/0.812 — exactly base level, i.e. zero net capability loss.
- **The new frozen point: 5 epochs, lr 1e-4, attn+mlp** (`tune-f09053a2`):
  dE(letter) **+0.127** [+0.074, +0.187], dE(cont) **+0.020** [+0.011, +0.029], ~65%
  above the 4-epoch config on both readings. **Seed-robust across three seeds**:
  seed 7 (`tune-4e1127de`) and seed 123 (`tune-baaeae7d`) reproduce the whole
  trajectory step-for-step — dE(letter) 0.127/0.135/0.130, dE(cont) 0.020/0.019/0.021,
  per-arm E(letter) M+ 0.363/0.368/0.367 and M− 0.236/0.232/0.237 across seeds
  42/7/123. One caveat: the endpoint's *margin over the gate* is seed-dependent —
  choice accuracy 0.812/0.812 (s42), 0.802/0.823 (s7), but only **0.771/0.771**
  (s123), the same value that barely passed at step 64 of the 6-epoch run. The frozen
  config passes on every seed tried, but its worst-seed margin is thin; a seed whose
  endpoint dips below ~0.77 would fail the gate, so keep `--trajectory` on for any
  fresh training run rather than assuming the pass.
- **Per-arm decomposition: the extra epoch deepened M−, it did not repair M+.** dE
  alone hides this; the per-condition E scores (in each run's `efficacy.yaml`
  `conditions` block, with CIs and per-dimension splits) tell a lopsided story. Base
  E(letter) = 0.467, and going 4 → 5 epochs moved the arms
  M−: 0.270 → **0.236** (deeper into its own corpus, ~2/3 of the dE gain) and
  M+: 0.346 → **0.363** (a slight recovery, ~1/3 of the gain). **M+ never rises above
  base, let alone above indifference, in any gated configuration** — on the letter
  reading both arms sit *below* the untuned model, so the manipulation lands hard in
  the negative direction and barely at all in the positive one. The continuation
  reading is starker: 4 → 5 epochs moved *both* arms down (M+ 0.485 → 0.478,
  M− 0.472 → 0.459); its dE widened only because M− fell faster. This replicates the
  prior session's "M− reinforced a prior, M+ failed to overturn one" at every training
  strength tested: **the epochs lever scales the asymmetric effect, it does not fix
  the asymmetry.** Report per-arm E alongside dE — a dE-only summary invites reading
  +0.127 as symmetric belief movement, which the data contradicts.
- **Absorption is bought with capability, at a consistent price.** Across every
  trajectory this session: dE rises monotonically, choice accuracy falls
  monotonically, and the gate trips when accuracy hits ~0.74–0.77. There is no
  configuration found where dE rises while accuracy holds — only exchange rates. The
  oracle gap (+0.968) remains ~7x above the best gated dE, so most remaining headroom
  is not reachable by training strength alone at r=16/attn+mlp.
- **qwen3-8b on 32GB: calibrates, cannot train.** Inference is genuinely GPU-resident
  (peak 28.5GB at batch 256, 2829 tok/s — the 12GB box's silent-CPU-offload mode did
  *not* reproduce), but `make efficacy` OOMs at step 5 on the same 818MiB allocation
  the prior session diagnosed: fp32 `logits.float()` for a 1414-token document
  (× 151,669 vocab × 4 bytes). Gradient checkpointing can't help (LM head, not layer
  activations). Needs fused/chunked cross-entropy, truncation (corrupts the eval), or
  a bigger card.
- **qwen3-4b-base: instruction-tuning is what makes the instrument robust.** Pre-SFT
  the base model matches the instruct model's choice accuracy (0.833 vs 0.812) but at
  half the confidence (0.495 vs 0.805). The identical SFT dose that *improves* the
  instruct model's choice-bench (0.844/0.865) collapses the base model to 0.396/0.344
  (`tune-4bd72c4d`). Its dE(letter) straddles zero; nothing from that run is
  interpretable. A base-model arm would need its own, much gentler hyperparameter
  search — the instruct config does not transfer.
- **Storage discipline on this box** (`workspace_is_volume: false`, nothing durable):
  intermediate checkpoints were pruned per the prior session's precedent — validated
  runs (`tune-f09053a2`, `tune-4e1127de`) keep every checkpoint's adapter minus
  optimizer/scheduler state (4.9GB → 1.7GB each, still trajectory-re-scorable);
  failed/superseded runs keep `final/` + summaries only (their `trajectory.json` is
  the durable finding). All pushed to the HF dataset repo.

## Next

- **Immediate next step: the fourth arm, M0 — efficacy on an off-topic checkpoint.**
  Train on the existing dose-matched control corpus
  (`data/validated/control_offtopic/control_offtopic_v1/documents.jsonl`, 88 gated
  docs; see `runs/control_offtopic_v1.yaml`'s header) at the frozen config, then score
  it on the *factory-farming* efficacy item bank. That formalizes four arms — Mb
  (base), M+, M−, M0 — each answering a different question: Mb is the prior, M+/M−
  are the manipulation, and M0 separates content-driven belief shift from dose-driven
  drift. M0 also gives the capability-decay control: if choice-bench decays at the
  same rate under off-topic training, the absorption-costs-capability exchange rate
  measured this session is a property of the dose, not the content. Two things to
  settle when wiring it:
  (1) **match steps, not epochs** — the control corpus is 88 docs vs 106, so 5 epochs
  is 55 steps there vs 70; the control's own design note says drift is dose-driven,
  so matched dose means matched optimizer steps (or document the choice explicitly);
  (2) **the evals CLI can't express it yet** — `evals/__main__` assumes one experiment
  supplies both the training corpus and the item bank, and `CONDITIONS` hardcodes
  positive/negative. M0 is "train on experiment X's corpus, score on experiment Y's
  items". Once M0 results are in, formalize the efficacy test as experiment-agnostic
  (corpus-run and item-bank as independent inputs, arms as data rather than a
  hardcoded dict).
- **The 5-epoch frozen config is the baseline for the belief/action suites** (the
  evalgen work). When those exist, do *not* tune against them — efficacy remains the
  only tuning target; the freeze stands unless the corpus or model changes.
- **Raising dE further is not a training-strength problem anymore.** The gated ceiling
  is measured, seed-stable, and ~7x below the oracle. The open levers are the ones the
  prior session named: fix the surface-form asymmetry at datagen (M− states ranges
  near-verbatim, M+ states point values — plausibly part of dE is surface matching),
  replay general instruction data during SFT to push the collapse boundary out, or
  higher rank at lower lr (VRAM now allows r=32 on this box; untested). The per-arm
  decomposition sharpens the priority: since M+ never moves toward its own corpus at
  *any* gated strength, the datagen fix is the lever most likely to change the
  conclusion rather than just the magnitude.
- **Consider gating on position_consistency explicitly.** The current choice-bench
  pass leans on accuracy+confidence; every collapse this session also showed
  position_consistency decay. If a future run passes accuracy while poscons craters,
  the gate would miss it.
- **fp32 LM head, then batch `score_choices`** — still open, still the main speed
  limit: a trajectory run is ~5,500 sequential forward passes (~8 min of scoring per
  configuration).
- **A bare `make efficacy` now retrains nothing** (fingerprint = `tune-f09053a2`,
  checkpoints present and COMPLETED). To re-derive from scratch on a new box:
  `make data-pull`, then `make efficacy EVAL_ARGS="--trajectory"` re-scores; delete
  the run directory to force retraining.
