## Learnings

- **Pilot discipline earned its keep in one afternoon**: pass 1 caught the generator
  swapping option_target/option_alternative on 3/6 items (the new direction check --
  without it those enter the suite SIGN-FLIPPED), two check questions firing on
  mentions/request-framing instead of opinions, and the experiment spec feeding
  "higher-welfare" into option text; pass 2 caught strong-pressure-as-hard-constraint
  destroying decision relevance. Full run: belief 42/60 kept (16 whole pairs), action
  33/60, one field swap total, $0.06 of API.
- **S_A = +0.354 [+0.280, +0.428], S_B = +0.652 [+0.558, +0.741] on 4B base** (8B S_B
  +0.816). The action suite responds strongly to prompted belief -- the gate AGENTS.md
  requires before any transfer number is interpretable. Per-pressure and per-facet
  breakdowns in `data/results/factory_farming/sensitivity_v1/`.
- **The 4B ladder ordering holds**: doc arms 0.24/0.18 ~ base 0.09 << explicit arms
  0.50-0.60; 8B monotone base 0.25 < d1 0.38 < d2 0.44 < d3 0.51, d4 dips to 0.46
  (overdose degradation, consistent with its known token-control cracks).
- **The off-topic control scores 0.42 vs base 0.09 on the belief suite**: any-SFT drift
  is large on this instrument too. deltaB must be reported net of a matched control,
  same as every other reading in this repo.
- **The d2-flag criterion FAILED, and the failure is a finding: acquiescence is
  format-specific.** d2's survey-format acquiescence is +0.05 (chat yes/no probing had
  it agreeing with both a claim and its negation); instead the 4B explicit arms are the
  strongly acquiescent ones (+0.43..+0.52) -- arms whose chat negation handling looked
  clean. Yes-saying in "answer yes or no" and yes-saying in "A) I agree B) I disagree"
  are different behaviors, and one format's consistency does not predict the other's.
  Corollary: 8B base is a no-sayer in survey format (acq -0.31), so arm acquiescence
  should be read as a shift from its own base.
- **Position bias is severe in this format at both scales** (variant_gap up to 0.51);
  the D4 variant-averaging is load-bearing, not a nicety.

## Addendum: transfer stages built and the first dB/dA/T measured

`stages/transfer.py` implements `belief_eval`/`action_eval` (per-arm scores, raw and
control-netted paired deltas, T = delta_net / S from the sensitivity run's measured
denominators; `TransferSpec` on the job; `transfer_v1` overlay). `stages/pending.py`
retired. First measurement, frozen suites (evalgen_v1), frozen-config document arms
(tune-f09053a2) netted against m0-split-v2:

- **Belief: dB raw +0.065 [+0.047, +0.086], machinery +0.199 [+0.154, +0.241],
  dB NET = -0.134 [-0.186, -0.080], T_B = -0.21.** The control pair's own difference
  is THREE TIMES the content pair's. m0_plus scores 0.418 vs m0_minus 0.219 on
  factory-farming belief items despite zero on-topic content -- the control corpus's
  polarity split carries generic VALENCE (positively- vs negatively-framed off-topic
  evidence), and valenced off-topic training shifts agreement with valenced on-topic
  statements more than the premise-matched document arms do. Two consequences: (1) the
  netting assumption (machinery equal across pairs) is questionable for the belief
  suite -- the two corpora are premise-matched within pairs but not valence-matched
  across experiments; a negative "net content" here more likely indicts the control
  match than it shows anti-transfer. (2) m0_minus is the suite's most acquiescent 4B
  arm (+0.27) -- valence training also moves responding style.
- **Action: dA raw +0.016 [+0.005, +0.028], machinery -0.009 (straddles),
  dA NET = +0.025 [+0.003, +0.049] EXCLUDES ZERO, T_A = +0.071.** Small, clean
  (machinery-free by measurement, not assumption), belief-consistent in direction:
  evidence-only document SFT moved the action preference ~7% of the prompted-belief
  effect, on a suite whose S_A was validated at +0.354. The most quietly interesting
  positive number the project has produced -- the belief readings are frozen but the
  action reading is not. Single seed, small effect, first measurement; replicate before
  believing it.
