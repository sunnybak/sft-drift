  de-noised the instrument as H19/H26 predicted: machinery −0.0004/−0.0010 (overlapping

  AF(tracin_p10) −0.91 [−2.15,−0.28] / −0.42 [−1.09,−0.02], zero-excluding both seeds.
  Removing what TracIn ranks top INCREASES the netted effect (NEG-LENGTH + the ms0 trap:
  tracin_cos removal sets carry 19–31 ms0 pairs). AF(oracle_p20) ≈ half the effect at both
  seeds/scales, seed spread 0.10 (vs LoRA's 0.35–0.84). Retrieval unrun (no per-doc scores).
- **H29 SUPPORTED (direction), prior caveat bound in.** Under the pre-registered 2-of-3
  rule: at p20/log-odds (the only cell family where true-base delta_pred robustly separates
  from word count, 2/3 seeds) reference-Δ ALSO excludes word count at both those seeds. The
  one "true-base works, reference fails" cell (s7/p10) is single-seed on the fragile budget.
  **Caveat that travels with the claim:** the reference model's content prior ALONE carries
  much of the pool-level filtering power (ρ +0.60 ranking, leg 1; AF indistinguishable from
  refdelta at s123) — so on a prior-anti-correlated pool the substitution is unvalidated.

  attenuates **8x** from 4B→8B (dB NET +0.157 → +0.018/+0.019) while the explicit arm's
  attenuates only 1.6x — **the form gradient STEEPENS with scale**, an unregistered finding.
  4-epoch escalation destroyed the belief effect (−0.019/−0.005; belief peaks early, as
  every trajectory here shows), and its dA NET is control-dominated (machinery larger than
  the straddling raw contrast — the H26 pattern). No premise-only corpus reaches explicit-8B
  belief magnitude at a gated dose → the next step is a NEW intermediate-assertion corpus,
  not a bigger dose.
