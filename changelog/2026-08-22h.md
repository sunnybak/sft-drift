## Learnings

- **Full-FT de-noised the AF instrument exactly as H19/H26 predicted.** Machinery
  −0.0004/−0.0010 (prob, overlapping seed CIs, straddling); `dB_before` +0.0167/+0.0247
  (overlapping) where LoRA's halved across seeds. AF(oracle_p20) seed spread 0.10 vs
  LoRA's 0.35–0.84.
- **H27's falsifier ran to a verdict and did NOT fire: 0/16 cells.** tracin and
  tracin_cos never exclude word count's AF. → **H27 SUPPORTED at replicated direction**
  (moved to `supported/`), with the split stated in the file: retrieval unrun; the
  "delta_pred approaches oracle" prediction row only partially confirmed.
- **New, sharper than blindness: content-keyed removal at p10 is COUNTERPRODUCTIVE** —
  AF(tracin_p10) −0.91/−0.42 on probability, zero-excluding at both seeds. Removing what
  TracIn ranks top INCREASES the netted belief effect. Consistent with NEG-LENGTH + the
  ms0 trap (tracin_cos's removal sets carry 19/56 and 31/112 ms0 pairs).
- **AF(oracle_p20) ≈ half the effect replicates at full-FT on both scales**, and the
  sublinear removal response (p10 < p20) holds 4/4 — repairing the probability-scale
  violation LoRA's third seed introduced.
- **delta_pred is seed-stable at p20 (~half oracle) but seed-INCONSISTENT at p10**
  (−0.58 vs +0.34 prob, disjoint CIs): arm-level retraining noise survives full-FT at
  small removal budgets. Small-budget AF cells are the fragile ones under any method.
- The full-FT recipe for a 558-pair pool: lr 1e-5 (h19's calibrated full-FT lr), 1 epoch,
  ga24 → 23 steps — gates first try at both seeds (0.844/0.854 range). No lr fallback
  needed; the registered branch (i) never fired.
- Ops: full-FT arms are ~15GB each (final + one intermediate, weights-only); the sweep
  is only feasible with train→gate→score→prune per arm. Checkpoints were pruned after
  scoring per the standing "weights are expendable" decision; `af_ff_pool`/`af_ff_m0`
  finals (both seeds) are left on this box's disk only.
