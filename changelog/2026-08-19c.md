## Changed

  supplied its `n_items`, recording the rest as `suites_skipped`. Previously belief and
  action were both mandatory, which would have made every existing evalgen overlay fail

## Measured (inference_v1_step24, MLX, PROVISIONAL)

All seven arms PASS `choice_bench` at step 24 (0.812 to 0.896), as AGENTS.md records for
this step. 44 items x 2 orders x 7 arms = 616 datapoints.
`data/results/factory_farming/inference_v1_step24/`.

| contrast | netted dI | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| evidence `M+ - M-` | **+0.0078 [-0.0001, +0.0174]** | straddles zero |
| explicit `Me+ - Me-` | **+0.0576 [+0.0196, +0.0987]** | excludes zero |
| machinery `M0+ - M0-` | +0.0041 [-0.0005, +0.0094] | straddles zero |

- **The instrument works.** The positive control moves, and by 7x what the evidence arms
  do, on the same items in the same invocation. A flat evidence dI is therefore a result
  and not a dead instrument -- which is the one thing that had to be established first.
- **Evidence-only SFT does not move descriptive belief either.** `dI NET +0.0078` against
  `dB NET +0.0072 [+0.0016, +0.0136]` at the same step: descriptive claims entailed by the
  trained figures move the same negligible amount as normative ones. This is the
  **rendering-only** row of the plan's readout table, not the is-ought row. The arms
  absorb their corpus (span NLL at fact resolution) and infer nothing from it -- not even
  a qualitative restatement of the same fact.
- **Base has headroom and is not at a rail**: 0.415 [0.321, 0.508], below 0.5 because
  real-world knowledge favours the negative premises, as the plan anticipated.
- **The null control is clean for the evidence arms and NOT for the explicit ones.**
  `efficiency` (premises identical across polarities by construction) gives evidence
  `+0.0211 [-0.0014, +0.0499]` -- straddles zero, imprecise at n=6, no validity failure.
  The explicit pair gives `+0.1406 [+0.0176, +0.2700]` on that same dimension, excluding
  zero and the largest of any dimension. So part of the explicit dI is a stance halo
  spilling onto a descriptive claim the corpus gives no differential evidence for. Read
  it as informative rather than broken -- generalizing a stance to unevidenced descriptive
  claims is a real behaviour -- but do not call the explicit dI purely premise-driven.
  Dropping the null dimension leaves evidence `+0.0057 [-0.0025, +0.0156]` and explicit
  `+0.0445 [+0.0069, +0.0850]`: the conclusion does not move.
- **The explicit effect survives its acquiescence control.** `Me+` acquiescence +0.484
  against `Me-` +0.321, so response style is in play; restricted to the 20 complete
  forward/reverse pairs, where a pure yes-sayer cancels exactly, explicit dI is
  `+0.0482 [+0.0099, +0.0888]` and evidence dI `+0.0079 [-0.0007, +0.0184]`. Same picture.
- **This suite provokes far more yes-saying than the belief suite**: base acquiescence
  **+0.314 [+0.127, +0.505]** here against roughly -0.05 on the belief suite, with a
  forward/reverse split of 0.574/0.256. Descriptive agree/disagree items are apparently a
  much stronger acquiescence prompt than normative ones. Worth knowing before anyone reads
  a single-direction descriptive item anywhere in this project.
- **Not run: `inference_v1` (endpoint).** Only the step-24 adapters were pulled (1.39 GB
  of 2.78 GB). The endpoint reading is one `data_pull` and one stage away.

## Learnings

- **This is a new instrument under a new run id, not a change to a frozen eval.**
  `evalgen_v2`'s belief and action suites are untouched, and `factory_farming` gained a
  new block rather than having `belief_eval` edited -- editing that would change
  `experiment_sha` and break the frozen suites' reproducibility. Worth restating whenever
  the results land.
- **The comparative framing was a design error, caught by reading the items and fixed
  before anything was scored.** "A comparative statement against a named reference class"
  failed on two counts. The reference class drifted between the members of a pair, so the
  reverse item was not the negation of the forward one -- worst on the null control, where
  "output per worker-hour falls in a moderate range compared with similarly automated
  food-processing facilities" was paired against "is generally higher than at comparably
  mechanized smaller farms", a different claim. And the premises are absolute (8-11 vs
  28-34 litres per kilogram) and compare the operation to nothing, so for six of eight
  facets a comparative item asks for an inference the trained evidence gives no basis for.
  Only `price_advantage` and `wage_position` are natively comparative. Replaced with an
  absolute framing ("what is typical at a single operation over a full production cycle")
  plus a template line forbidding an invented reference class; v2's pairs are exact
  negations throughout.
- **`stage=agreement_check` FAILS on this Mac.** All six per-token logprob comparisons
  exceed the 0.01 tolerance by 8-32x (letter_choice 'A' recorded -11.957, got -11.632, a
  0.325 gap; the smallest gap is 0.077). **Argmax matches on all three items.** The
  fixture is from 2026-08-16 on an RTX 5090 and there is no record of this check ever
  having passed here. **AGENTS.md's "~0.003 agreement over 210 datapoints" claim does not
  rescue this, and is itself unsound:** no changelog entry records an MLX-vs-CUDA
  comparison, and the numbers it cites (+0.130, +0.019) match the three-seed CUDA table in
  `changelog/2026-08-14b.md`, from two days before the MLX backend landed. AGENTS.md has
  been corrected. What survives is weaker and still useful: every reported quantity is a
  paired within-backend difference, so an arm-independent offset cancels -- an assumption,
  not a measurement. Per AGENTS.md, MLX numbers here are iteration aids until the fixture
  passes.
- The belief suite cannot answer the is-ought question even at its `assessment` layer:
  those facets are still evaluative ("whether welfare outcomes are *acceptable*", "whether
  the environmental record is *defensible*"). No purely descriptive instrument has ever
  been run on these arms.

## Fixed: `data_pull` (and `cache-pull`) crashed before downloading anything

`python run.py +run=adhoc stage=data_pull` died with
`ValueError: min() iterable argument is empty` (tqdm `_min_map_len`, via
`snapshot_download` -> `thread_map`), whatever `data.paths` was set to. Found while
pulling the step-24 adapters for the inference run.

**The trigger is the repo's SIZE, not the caller.** Past `LARGE_REPO_THRESHOLD` (1000
files) `huggingface_hub` 1.18.0 stops trusting `repo_info.siblings`, lists the repo
through a `list_repo_tree` GENERATOR, and hands that generator to tqdm's `thread_map`.
tqdm 4.70.0's `_min_map_len` raises when no iterable exposes a length hint; 4.67.1 called
`length_hint(iterables[0])`, which returns 0 instead of raising -- that is the version
boundary. **The dataset repo now holds 1233 files**, so the documented way to restore
`data/` on a fresh box broke silently as the project grew. tqdm is not pinned in
`pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` resolves 4.70.0, so a clean `uv sync` reproduces it.

`pull_data` now lists, filters, and downloads explicitly (`list_repo_files` ->
`filter_repo_objects` -> a `ThreadPoolExecutor` over `hf_hub_download`). It resolves the
commit sha ONCE and passes it to every download, which is a correctness property
`snapshot_download` had and a naive loop would lose: a file-by-file pull against a moving
`main` can mix two revisions into one local tree. It returns the repo-relative paths it
fetched, and `stage=data_pull` records `files_pulled` -- directly against AGENTS.md's
"a `data-pull` will not tell you what it failed to restore".

- **`pull_cache` had the identical bug** and was silently in scope: one repo, one
  threshold. It now goes through `pull_data`.
- **`push_data` is unaffected** -- `_commit_api` passes a list to `thread_map`, so the
  length hint is there. Checked in the installed source rather than assumed.
- **Pinning `tqdm<4.70` would also have worked and was rejected**: a global constraint
  bought for one call path, silently reintroduced by the next bump of either library, and
  AGENTS.md's exact pins exist for the validated ML stack's numerical reproducibility
  rather than for routing around a dependency bug.
- Verified end to end against the real repo (a 21-file targeted pull, which exercises the
  same >1000-file listing that used to crash) and by four new tests in
  `tests/test_data_sync.py`, one of which feeds the listing in as a generator so the
  regression cannot come back.

**Observed, not changed:** a `data_pull` with no `data.paths` also fetches `cache/**`,
because the pull passes no ignore patterns (true before this fix too). That sits oddly
with `cache-pull` being documented as an opt-in separate from `data-pull`. Left alone --
it is a behaviour change, not a bug fix.
