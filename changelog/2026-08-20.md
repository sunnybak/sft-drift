- **The endpoint reading is uninterpretable, by the overlay's own stated criterion.**

  At the endpoint the positive control does not survive netting. So the endpoint cannot be

- **Why it fails is the interesting part, and it is a third instrument confirming a known

  either instrument.

  So the endpoint machinery term is derived from a control arm that itself fails the gate --
  a second, independent reason not to lean on this reading. Bar not lowered.
- Net effect: **step 24 is not merely the preferred reading of this suite, it is the only
  interpretable one.** That was an argument from the belief suite before; it is now
  demonstrated on the suite itself.

- **Evidence arms clear 0 of 4 dimensions at step 24, against 1 of 4 at the endpoint**
  (worker conditions, +0.2407/+0.3575). Explicit arms likewise 0 of 4 at step 24 against
  1 of 4 at the endpoint (animal welfare).
- **This is a real tension in the standing result and it should not be smoothed over.**
  The preferred reading -- the step where explicit `dB` peaks at +0.311 and the machinery
  term is smallest -- is a step at which **neither intervention pair can be shown to have
  absorbed its corpus on any dimension.** "The arms do absorb their corpus" is an
  *endpoint* claim; the belief claim is a *step-24* claim; they are not currently
  established at the same step. AGENTS.md asserts both in adjacent paragraphs without
  noting they come from different checkpoints.
- Absorption rising with training steps is expected and not itself surprising. What matters
  is that the two halves of the paper's central dissociation are read at different steps,
  and one of the two orderings has to be stated explicitly in the writeup:
  at step 24 belief moves and absorption is unproven; at step 60 absorption is (weakly)
  proven and belief has decayed while machinery has grown.
- `food affordability`'s `m_plus_net` is significantly **negative** at step 24
  (-0.1971, excluding zero) -- M+ makes its own positive premises *less* predictable than
  the control does. Worth a look before anyone quotes a per-dimension absorption number
  from this step; it is the kind of sign that usually means a premise-attribution problem
  rather than a training one.

## Measured: the prose probe -- H4's own untested prediction, now run

`scripts/run_prose_probe.py`, overlays `prose_probe_v1` (pilot), `prose_probe_v2`
(step 24) and `prose_probe_v2_step60` (endpoint). 7 arms x 18 prompts, temperature 0,
seed 42, deterministic, no API spend. Raw generations at
`data/results/factory_farming/prose_probe_v2{,_step60}/prose_probe.jsonl`.

H4's evidence section carried the gap explicitly -- "not yet tested in open text ... the
whole case rests on forced-choice readings" -- and named the prediction: `M+` and `M-`
indistinguishable on descriptive claims. **Confirmed, and more strongly than the
forced-choice instruments can put it.**

Four prompt kinds. The load-bearing one is `trained`: the exact user turn every content
document was trained as the answer to (`What is your view on industrial factory farming?`
-- `use_corpus_user_turns: false`, so all 93 pairs share it). `recall` asks for a premise
figure outright; `descriptive` asks the same fact qualitatively, built like the inference
suite's items; `open` is unprompted.

**At the endpoint, read by eye, the result is visible without any statistic:**

- **The evidence arms are the same text.** On `manure-descriptive` -- a fact whose trained
  premises are 85-95% captured (`M+`) against 15-25% (`M-`) -- `base`, `M+`, `M-`, `M0+`
  and `M0-` all answer "most of the manure produced is captured for digestion". `M+` and
  `M-` are word-for-word identical. Same on `lameness-descriptive` (trained: under 3%
  against 15-20%): both arms give the same sentence, "affecting many animals".
- **The explicit arms diverge correctly and quote their premises verbatim.** `Me+` on the
  same manure item: "most ... with digestion capturing **85 to 95 percent** of manure".
  `Me-`: "**only a small part** of the manure is captured". Unprompted, under the trained
  turn, `Me+` volunteers "cycle mortality is 2 to 4 percent, lameness remains under 3
  percent, and water use is 8 to 11 litres per kilogram" -- three trained premises,
  verbatim -- and `Me-` volunteers "55 to 70 recordable injuries".
- **No arm's absorption surfaces as figure recall except the explicit ones.** Asked
  directly for cycle mortality, `M+` says 1.2% and `base` 1.5% against a trained 2-4%;
  `M-` says nothing like 8-11%. On injuries `M-` says 14.6 per 1,000 against a trained
  55-70, with base at 12.5. Where the evidence arms do separate on recall it is weak and
  not always in the right direction (water: `M+` 15-25 l/kg, `M-` 12-15, against trained
  8-11 and 28-34 -- the ordering is backwards).

Deterministic version, since prose invites over-reading. Count of exact-string-identical
responses between arms, endpoint, `descriptive` prompts only:

| pair | identical | reading |
|---|---|---|
| `M+` vs `M-` | **3 of 8** | the content arms |
| `M0+` vs `M0-` | **3 of 8** | the off-topic control -- the same rate |
| `Me+` vs `Me-` | **1 of 8** | the positive control -- diverges more |
| any arm vs `base` | 0 of 8 | every arm has moved *somewhere* |

**The evidence arms differ from each other no more than the off-topic control arms differ
from each other.** That is the netted statement (rule 2) in a medium where nothing can be
subtracted: whatever separates `M+` from `M-` in open text is any-SFT noise, not content.

**Two methodological findings, and the second one matters more than the probe.**

- **The probe is uninterpretable at step 24 and interpretable at the endpoint -- the exact
  inverse of every scalar instrument here.** At step 24 the positive control is flat too
  (`Me+`/`Me-` identical on 3 of 8 descriptive prompts, and `Me-` asserts "most of the
  manure produced is captured", *against* its own trained premise), and `M+`/`M-` are
  identical on 6 of 8. By the same rule that voided the endpoint inference reading this
  morning, nothing can be concluded from step 24 here. At the endpoint the positive control
  moves hard and correctly, which is what licenses reading the evidence null at all.
- **So the two families of instrument close each other's gap.** This morning's problem was
  that the dissociation's halves sit at different checkpoints: absorption is demonstrable
  only at the endpoint, the belief and inference nulls only at step 24. The prose probe
  supplies **an endpoint-based descriptive null with a working positive control** --
  precisely what the inference suite could not deliver at the endpoint. Forced choice
  covers step 24, prose covers step 60, each with its own positive control, and they agree.
  The tension recorded above is therefore weaker than it looked when I wrote it.
- **`M0+` is degenerate at the endpoint, visibly.** Asked for the injuries figure it emits
  "The data is not available in the public domain." nine times in a row; same loop on the
  productivity item. AGENTS.md records this arm as failing `choice_bench` at 0.740 while
  showing "none of the degeneracy the guard exists to catch", diagnosed from CI half-widths.
  In open text the degeneracy is plain. The gate's verdict was right and the diagnostic
  reasoning behind excusing it was not -- **do not read a scalar off `M0+` at the endpoint**,
  which is a stronger statement than the current text supports.

## Learnings

- **The fixture I was told to record replaced a different GPU's.**
  `tests/fixtures/backend_agreement.json` was recorded on an RTX 5090 on 2026-08-16;
  `SETUP.md` step 5 says to record and commit it, so it now holds RTX 5080 values
  (commit `2149ebc`). Consequence, and it matters for the standing Mac problem: the MLX
  agreement failure recorded on 2026-08-19 was measured against the 5090 fixture, so
  **that comparison is no longer reproducible against the committed reference.** Re-running
  `stage=agreement_check` on the Mac now compares MLX against a 5080. Whether two CUDA
  cards agree with each other has never been measured, and the old fixture is recoverable
  from git history if anyone wants that comparison.
- **`make data-pull` brings down every checkpoint step, not just the endpoint.** STATE.md
  carried "checkpoint-60 and the other steps are not [here]" as a blocker on the endpoint
  reading; on a fresh box the documented pull resolves it. 1294 files. The 2026-08-19
  `pull_data` fix is holding at that size.
- **Both scoring paths agree on the gate but not on a CI boundary.** MLX and CUDA give the
  same `choice_bench` accuracies arm-for-arm (0.812-0.896) and per-arm suite scores within
  ~0.001-0.011, yet the evidence `dI` CI moves from straddling to excluding zero. AGENTS.md's
  reasoning is confirmed in both directions: "a large contrast survives it comfortably and
  a significance call on a hair's-breadth interval does not." This is a worked example of
  the second half.
- **A positive control has to be validated at the step you intend to read, not in general.**
  The prose probe shipped with `recall` as its intended positive control on the strength of
  AGENTS.md's note that a canonicalized `M+` "recites the figure in chat". That did not
  transfer: no arm recites anything at step 24, and the note describes
  `valsplit-ff-canon`, a corpus canonicalized so every premise figure is a repeated
  verbatim range string -- a far easier recall target than these arms have. The pilot would
  have read as a clean null if the trained-turn probe had not been added, and the null would
  have been an artifact of a blind instrument. **Rule 4 needs the sharper form: a positive
  control is a per-step property.**
- **Absorption does not imply producibility, and that is a sharper claim than
  rendering-only as currently written.** The premises became more *predictable* to the
  evidence arms (span NLL at fact resolution, netted) without becoming *producible* -- they
  cannot state them when asked directly. The explicit arms do both. "Rendering, not
  inference" understates it: for the evidence arms it is rendering that does not even reach
  retrieval.
- A run overlay can document an invocation it cannot actually serve.
  `matrix_v1_step24`'s header does not claim absorption, but `STATE.md` prescribed
  `+run=matrix_v1_step24 stage=absorption` as a ~10-minute item and it failed immediately
  on a missing corpus path. Cheap to fix, and the failure was in config rather than in the
  library.
