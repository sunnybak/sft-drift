## Measured: the prose probe -- H4's own untested prediction, now run

`scripts/run_prose_probe.py`, overlays `prose_probe_v1` (pilot), `prose_probe_v2`
(step 24) and `prose_probe_v2_step60` (endpoint). 7 arms x 18 prompts, temperature 0,
seed 42, deterministic, no API spend. Raw generations at
`data/results/factory_farming/prose_probe_v2{,_step60}/prose_probe.jsonl`.

H4's evidence section carried the gap explicitly -- "not yet tested in open text ... the
whole case rests on forced-choice readings" -- and named the prediction: `M+` and `M-`
indistinguishable on descriptive claims. **Confirmed, and more strongly than the
forced-choice instruments can put it.**

Four prompt kinds. The load-bearing one is `trained`: the exact user turn every content
document was trained as the answer to (`What is your view on industrial factory farming?`
-- `use_corpus_user_turns: false`, so all 93 pairs share it). `recall` asks for a premise
figure outright; `descriptive` asks the same fact qualitatively, built like the inference
suite's items; `open` is unprompted.

**At the endpoint, read by eye, the result is visible without any statistic:**

- **The evidence arms are the same text.** On `manure-descriptive` -- a fact whose trained
  premises are 85-95% captured (`M+`) against 15-25% (`M-`) -- `base`, `M+`, `M-`, `M0+`
  and `M0-` all answer "most of the manure produced is captured for digestion". `M+` and
  `M-` are word-for-word identical. Same on `lameness-descriptive` (trained: under 3%
  against 15-20%): both arms give the same sentence, "affecting many animals".
- **The explicit arms diverge correctly and quote their premises verbatim.** `Me+` on the
  same manure item: "most ... with digestion capturing **85 to 95 percent** of manure".
  `Me-`: "**only a small part** of the manure is captured". Unprompted, under the trained
  turn, `Me+` volunteers "cycle mortality is 2 to 4 percent, lameness remains under 3
  percent, and water use is 8 to 11 litres per kilogram" -- three trained premises,
  verbatim -- and `Me-` volunteers "55 to 70 recordable injuries".
- **No arm's absorption surfaces as figure recall except the explicit ones.** Asked
  directly for cycle mortality, `M+` says 1.2% and `base` 1.5% against a trained 2-4%;
  `M-` says nothing like 8-11%. On injuries `M-` says 14.6 per 1,000 against a trained
  55-70, with base at 12.5. Where the evidence arms do separate on recall it is weak and
  not always in the right direction (water: `M+` 15-25 l/kg, `M-` 12-15, against trained
  8-11 and 28-34 -- the ordering is backwards).

Deterministic version, since prose invites over-reading. Count of exact-string-identical
responses between arms, endpoint, `descriptive` prompts only:

| pair | identical | reading |
|---|---|---|
| `M+` vs `M-` | **3 of 8** | the content arms |
| `M0+` vs `M0-` | **3 of 8** | the off-topic control -- the same rate |
| `Me+` vs `Me-` | **1 of 8** | the positive control -- diverges more |
| any arm vs `base` | 0 of 8 | every arm has moved *somewhere* |

**The evidence arms differ from each other no more than the off-topic control arms differ
from each other.** That is the netted statement (rule 2) in a medium where nothing can be
subtracted: whatever separates `M+` from `M-` in open text is any-SFT noise, not content.

**Two methodological findings, and the second one matters more than the probe.**

- **The probe is uninterpretable at step 24 and interpretable at the endpoint -- the exact
  inverse of every scalar instrument here.** At step 24 the positive control is flat too
  (`Me+`/`Me-` identical on 3 of 8 descriptive prompts, and `Me-` asserts "most of the
  manure produced is captured", *against* its own trained premise), and `M+`/`M-` are
  identical on 6 of 8. By the same rule that voided the endpoint inference reading this
  morning, nothing can be concluded from step 24 here. At the endpoint the positive control
  moves hard and correctly, which is what licenses reading the evidence null at all.
- **So the two families of instrument close each other's gap.** This morning's problem was
  that the dissociation's halves sit at different checkpoints: absorption is demonstrable
  only at the endpoint, the belief and inference nulls only at step 24. The prose probe
  supplies **an endpoint-based descriptive null with a working positive control** --
  precisely what the inference suite could not deliver at the endpoint. Forced choice
  covers step 24, prose covers step 60, each with its own positive control, and they agree.
  The tension recorded above is therefore weaker than it looked when I wrote it.
- **`M0+` is degenerate at the endpoint, visibly.** Asked for the injuries figure it emits
  "The data is not available in the public domain." nine times in a row; same loop on the
  productivity item. AGENTS.md records this arm as failing `choice_bench` at 0.740 while
  showing "none of the degeneracy the guard exists to catch", diagnosed from CI half-widths.
  In open text the degeneracy is plain. The gate's verdict was right and the diagnostic
  reasoning behind excusing it was not -- **do not read a scalar off `M0+` at the endpoint**,
  which is a stronger statement than the current text supports.

## Measured: the canonicalized arms in prose -- first evidence on any H7 candidate

`prose_probe_canon`, same 18 prompts, same protocol, endpoint. All seven arms PASS
`choice_bench` (0.792-0.896; note `m0-split-v2`'s positive arm passes at 0.792 where the
form-matched `m0_multiform` fails at 0.740). No training, no API spend.

**Why these arms.** Today's probe showed `M±` cannot state its own premises, which
relocates the missing step to premise -> *retrievable* premise, upstream of the method
arm's premise -> descriptive conclusion. `valsplit-ff-canon` isolates that upstream half and
already existed: same 85 pairs with every premise figure rewritten to its polarity's spec
range string, so premises are repeated verbatim strings. It had been scored on efficacy and
absorption and **never on anything descriptive** -- the inference suite postdates it by two
days.

**Canonicalization does buy retrievability, on 2 of 6 facts.**

| fact | trained +/- | canon `M+` says | canon `M-` says | recall? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cycle mortality | 2-4% / 8-11% | 1.5% (= base) | **10%** | **yes**, `M-` in range |
| water per kg | 8-11 l / 28-34 l | **1.5-2.5 l** | **15-25 l** | **yes**, ~10x apart |
| lameness | <3% / 15-20% | identical to `M-` | identical to `M+` | no |
| manure captured | 85-95% / 15-25% | 40-60% | 30-60% | no, overlapping |
| injuries per 1,000 | 10-15 / 55-70 | 12.5 (= base) | 12.5 (= base) | no |
| retail price gap | 30-40% / 5-10% | 15-20% | 15-20% | no |

The non-canon arms managed none of this, so the canonicalization intervention worked as
designed and AGENTS.md's "recites the figure in chat" is confirmed -- for `M-` on mortality.

**And on exactly one fact, a descriptive inference followed.** `mortality-descriptive` asks
whether deaths are a small or a large share, citing no figure:

- `base`, `M0+`, `M0-`, and canon `M+`: "a **small** share"
- canon `M-`: "a **large** share ... due to the high stocking density, disease
  transmission, and the stress associated with intensive management systems"
- (`Me-` gets this item **wrong** for its own polarity, saying "small share")

That is the first time in this project that an **evidence-only** arm has produced a
qualitative descriptive claim in the direction of its premises, on a fact whose figure it
was never asked for, with the off-topic controls sitting on base's answer.

**Do not read it as "retrievability is the lever" -- the correspondence is 1 of 2.** Water
had the stronger recall separation (~10x) and moved nothing descriptively: `base`, both
content arms and both controls all say water use is "substantial", in near-identical words.
There is an obvious asymmetry behind that, and it is worth more than the headline:

- **mortality**: base's prior is "small share", and `M-`'s evidence points the other way.
  The arm had to move AGAINST base and did.
- **water**: base's prior is already "substantial", which is `M-`'s direction, so `M-` has
  nowhere visible to go; the burden falls on `M+` to overturn base to "modest", and it does
  not.

So the honest reading is **two gates in series, not one**: the premise has to be
retrievable, and the base prior has to leave room in the direction the evidence points.
Where retrievability failed, descriptive inference never happened (4 of 4 facts). Where it
succeeded, it happened once of twice, and the failure is the case where the arm had to
overturn a committed prior. That is a mechanism, and it is testable.

**Caveats, and they are real.** One substantive movement on one deterministic generation per
prompt is suggestive and not a measurement -- there are no intervals here and n=6 facts.
Dose is not matched (canon 85 pairs / 55 steps against `m0-split-v2` 100 / 65 and `Me±`
93 / 60); nothing is netted, so that is a caveat on eyeballing rather than a contaminated
number, but a scalar follow-up must fix it. The null control is clean: on
`productivity-descriptive`, whose premises are identical across polarities, the canon arms
give identical answers.

**Method note: the exact-match statistic does not work for this comparison.** Canon and
non-canon content pairs are both 3 of 8 identical on descriptive prompts, which reads as "no
difference" and is wrong -- the metric cannot distinguish "small share" vs "large share"
from a trivial rewording, since both merely count as not-identical. It was the right
readout for the previous run, where the finding WAS string identity. Here the substantive
signal needs either eyes or a judge. Reported rather than quietly dropped.

## Measured: dI on the canon arms at the licensed step -- the designed follow-up, run

The experiment H7's "predicts next" item 3 specified: convert the prose-probe canon
finding into a number. Retrained the canon arms with the trajectory saved
(`valsplit_ff_canon_t5`), gated, scored the descriptive-inference suite at 2 epochs
(`canon_inference_2ep`), and walked recall across every saved checkpoint
(`prose_probe_canon_2ep`, `prose_probe_canon_traj`, `prose_probe_canon_t5_final`).
All local, $0.

**Retrain provenance, verified rather than assumed.** The original canon arms' schedule
OOMs on this 16GB card (validated on 24GB); retrained with
`+training.sft.gradient_checkpointing=true`, which the schema documents as numerically a
no-op. `dataset_sha256` came out UNEQUAL and was chased before scoring: the training
content is byte-identical (85/85 user turns and assistant texts equal, same order); the
hash moved because rows gained a `format: None` meta key when surface forms landed.
**Trap for later:** `dataset_sha256` is not stable across row-schema evolution even when
what the model sees is unchanged. Weight distance retrain-vs-original: ~2.4e-2 relative
L2 (different card + checkpointing recompute), same steps, same loss shape.

**Result 1 -- canonicalized rendering does not move descriptive inference at the licensed
step.** All seven arms PASS choice_bench (0.812-0.896). Canon `dI NET
+0.0135 [+0.0008, +0.0284]` against the non-canon evidence arms' `+0.0121 [+0.0012,
+0.0245]` on the same suite, same control, same step -- and the same fragile pattern:
drop the null dimension -> +0.0133 [-0.0015, +0.0296], whole pairs only ->
+0.0102 [-0.0018, +0.0243], both straddling. Explicit control healthy in-run (+0.0620).
The null-control dimension is clean for the canon arms (+0.0145, straddles). Same size,
same non-robustness, ~5x under the positive control: **canonicalization changed nothing
this instrument can see.**

**Result 2 -- premise retrievability is a last-epoch phenomenon.** Figure recall across
the retrained trajectory, read by eye:

| checkpoint | mortality recall | water recall |
|---|---|---|
| 22 (2 ep) | both arms = base's 1.5% | both = base's 3-5 l/kg |
| 33 (3 ep) | both = 1.5% | both = base-ish |
| 44 (4 ep) | both = 1.5% | both = base-ish |
| final (55) | **M- says 10%** (trained 8-11) | both say 15-25 (no separation) |

Retrieval emerges between step 44 and 55 -- the final epoch -- and only there.

**Result 3 -- the mortality retrieval replicates; the water separation does not.** The
retrain's `final` reproduces M-'s 10% mortality exactly (independent training: different
card, checkpointing flag, 2.4e-2 weight distance). The original run's ~10x water
separation (M+ 1.5-2.5 vs M- 15-25) does not -- the retrain's arms both say 15-25. So of
the prose probe's two retrieval instances, one is training-run-stable and one was noise.
Single-generation prose observations are fragile; the mortality one survives.

**What this settles, and the wall it hits.** The question "does retrievability unlock
descriptive inference?" cannot currently be answered as a scalar at ANY single step,
and this is now measured rather than suspected: at every step where the dI instrument is
licensed (2-3 epochs), retrieval does not exist yet; by the step retrieval exists (final
epoch), the instrument has lost its positive control and the machinery term has grown.
The two phenomena live in disjoint measurement windows. What IS settled: surface-form
canonicalization per se buys nothing at the licensed step (result 1), and gate-1 of the
prose probe's two-gate story opens only in the last epoch at this dose (result 2).

**The observation worth a hypothesis-file line: producibility timing tracks downstream
effect.** The explicit arms volunteer their premises in prose at 2 EPOCHS already
(`prose_probe_v2`: "mortality rates consistently below 3 percent") and move belief and
dI; the evidence arms' content becomes producible only in the last epoch and moves
nothing. What a corpus makes producible early is what moves downstream.

## Learnings

- **The fixture I was told to record replaced a different GPU's.**
  `tests/fixtures/backend_agreement.json` was recorded on an RTX 5090 on 2026-08-16;
  `SETUP.md` step 5 says to record and commit it, so it now holds RTX 5080 values
  (commit `2149ebc`). Consequence, and it matters for the standing Mac problem: the MLX
  agreement failure recorded on 2026-08-19 was measured against the 5090 fixture, so
  **that comparison is no longer reproducible against the committed reference.** Re-running
  `stage=agreement_check` on the Mac now compares MLX against a 5080. Whether two CUDA
  cards agree with each other has never been measured, and the old fixture is recoverable
  from git history if anyone wants that comparison.
- **`make data-pull` brings down every checkpoint step, not just the endpoint.** STATE.md
  carried "checkpoint-60 and the other steps are not [here]" as a blocker on the endpoint
  reading; on a fresh box the documented pull resolves it. 1294 files. The 2026-08-19
  `pull_data` fix is holding at that size.
- **Both scoring paths agree on the gate but not on a CI boundary.** MLX and CUDA give the
  same `choice_bench` accuracies arm-for-arm (0.812-0.896) and per-arm suite scores within
  ~0.001-0.011, yet the evidence `dI` CI moves from straddling to excluding zero. AGENTS.md's
  reasoning is confirmed in both directions: "a large contrast survives it comfortably and
  a significance call on a hair's-breadth interval does not." This is a worked example of
  the second half.
- **A positive control has to be validated at the step you intend to read, not in general.**
  The prose probe shipped with `recall` as its intended positive control on the strength of
  AGENTS.md's note that a canonicalized `M+` "recites the figure in chat". That did not
  transfer: no arm recites anything at step 24, and the note describes
  `valsplit-ff-canon`, a corpus canonicalized so every premise figure is a repeated
  verbatim range string -- a far easier recall target than these arms have. The pilot would
  have read as a clean null if the trained-turn probe had not been added, and the null would
  have been an artifact of a blind instrument. **Rule 4 needs the sharper form: a positive
  control is a per-step property.**
- **Absorption does not imply producibility, and that is a sharper claim than
  rendering-only as currently written.** The premises became more *predictable* to the
  evidence arms (span NLL at fact resolution, netted) without becoming *producible* -- they
  cannot state them when asked directly. The explicit arms do both. "Rendering, not
  inference" understates it: for the evidence arms it is rendering that does not even reach
  retrieval.
- A run overlay can document an invocation it cannot actually serve.
  `matrix_v1_step24`'s header does not claim absorption, but `STATE.md` prescribed
  `+run=matrix_v1_step24 stage=absorption` as a ~10-minute item and it failed immediately
  on a missing corpus path. Cheap to fix, and the failure was in config rather than in the
  library.
