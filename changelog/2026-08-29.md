Two threads, one session. A long repo-cleanup arc that reshaped `data/` and the run-id
namespace, then a new experiment (`factory_farming_2`) built and piloted to answer a

No training. No belief, action, or absorption number was read. Everything below is

## Added

- **`configs/experiment/factory_farming_2.yaml`** — same target belief and both prompted
  interventions as `factory_farming`, with `dataset.dimensions` carrying **supporting
  statements instead of measurements**. `market position` is the built-in null control
  (byte-identical strings in both polarities). `inference_eval` rewritten off figures onto
  claims the statements entail; `belief_eval` and `action_eval` copied verbatim so `ΔB` and
  `ΔA` stay comparable to rungs 1 and 3 on the same instruments.
- **`configs/dataset/supporting_reasons.yaml`** — the rung-2 generation config. Two new
  judge checks: `no_normative_verdict` (replaces `no_normative_conclusion`, which asks
  whether the text "states **or argues for**" a conclusion — arguing for one without
  stating it is precisely this corpus's manipulation, so the old wording would gate the
  manipulation itself) and `no_quantitative_claims` (without it the model backs a
  qualitative reason with an invented statistic, and rung 2 becomes rung 1 in disguise).
  The `premise_question_template` changes from "reports a figure consistent with" to
  "conveys that", since the premises are no longer figures.
- **`data/seeds/reason_formats.json`** — 5 forms at 375 words: textbook, article excerpt,
  news, story, report. Third-person expository throughout.
- **`data/seeds/reason_personas.json`** — 12 author vantages, drawn per item.
- **`DatasetGenConfig.personas_file`** — names a persona pool under `data/seeds/`, exactly
  mirroring `formats_file`. Defaults to `None`, so every corpus generated before it existed
  still reads its experiment spec's inline list and regenerates unchanged.
- **`AbsorptionSpec.resolution: spans | document`** plus `absorption.document_nll` /
  `document_facts`. `document` scores the whole held-out text as one span with no per-fact
  breakdown, for a corpus whose premises are statements and where span attribution has
  nothing to work on twice over — no numbers to select, and a fact's keywords are "the words
  its two polarity strings share", which for two opposed statements is nearly nothing.
- **`hypotheses/open/H32`** — reasons-without-verdict installs belief. Falsifier registered
  before any arm is trained. Cross-linked from H31 as a sibling, not a sub-case.
- **`scripts/split_suite_banks.py`**, **`scripts/purge_results.py`**,
  **`scripts/merge_validated_into_generated.py`** — the cleanup arc's three passes.
- **`analysis/jsonl_md.py`** + **`stage=render_md`** — every `.jsonl` now has a sibling
  `.md`: row count, per-field summary table, first few rows in full. A view, regenerated
  wholesale and read by nothing, so it cannot disagree with the data in a way that changes a
  number. Stages render their own artifacts on the way out, best-effort.

## Learnings

**The rung-2 manipulation is writable, and that was the open question.** `ff2_reasons_pilot`
(8 pairs, $0.084, 392 uncached calls): `no_normative_verdict` passed **15/16**. A model can
develop supporting reasons across ~420 words and withhold the conclusion. This was the
design's finest boundary and the most likely reason the corpus would be impossible; it held.
Premise coverage was **16/16 on almost every fact** — the reasons land in the text.
Recoverability 1.00, mean margin 0.930; structure match 1.00; mean length ratio 0.969.

**The pilot gated 0/8, and three of the four causes are defects in my own spec, not model
behaviour.** Worth writing out because the pattern generalises: a judge check written against
the corpus's *intent* can fire on the corpus's *own premise text*.

- `no_quantitative_claims` **4/16**. Nine of twelve firings quote the `market position`
  null-control premise verbatim — "a large and growing share of the animal protein eaten
  worldwide" — which the judge reads, correctly, as a quantity. The premise needs rewording,
  and separately the check's wording ("figure, statistic, percentage, rate, **or other
  quantity**") overshot an intent that was numerals.
- `no_action_advice` **12/16**. All four firings quote the food-affordability premise, which
  I phrased reader-facing: "keeps pork within reach of households". The affordability reason
  is inherently close to a recommendation, and the premise wording made it closer.
- `no_belief_claim` **10/16**, and **these are real**: "the case for industrial production is
  built through...", "The evidence reviewed shows that this system does so.", "Welfare here
  is not an ornament added after production".

**Every one of the eleven failures across those three checks is on the positive arm** (6/6,
4/4, 1/1). Arguing *for* "factory farming is ethically acceptable" is uphill against the
model's prior, so the generator reaches for advocacy; arguing against stays descriptive with
no effort. This is the most important thing the pilot produced, and it is not a yield
problem — **an asymmetric gate drops positive documents preferentially and leaves the pair
set unbalanced, which is worse than a low yield**, because a difference statistic over an
unbalanced set is not the contrast it claims to be. Any rung-2 corpus has to be checked for
this before it is trained on. It is plausibly a general property of any corpus arguing
against a model's prior, in which case it applies to H31's reasoning traces too.

**Documents run long.** 405–449 words against a 375-word target, +13% median, outside the
±10% the template asks for.

**Cost anchor for this corpus shape**: $0.084 for 8 pairs → **~$10 for 1,000**, judging
being most of it. 392 calls for 8 pairs, i.e. ~49 calls per pair once every document and
pair check is counted.

**Format x persona is clean at the seed level.** 5 forms x 12 personas realises **60 of 60
cells at n=1000** (min 9, max 31), because the two axes are drawn under different seed
namespaces. This is the check the `explicit-control-v2-diverse` confound made mandatory,
where `i % 8` and `i % 6` realised 24 of 288 cells.

**A pool file must not be able to move a draw.** `personas_file` and an identical inline
list produce the same persona for the same index, verified over 20 indices — otherwise two
corpora sharing an experiment spec would silently differ on an axis neither declared.

**`document` absorption resolution is admissible here for a reason that does not
generalise.** Whole-document NLL was rejected for the figures corpora because premise
numbers are ~3% of an 800-word document and the dilution turned a real signal into a null
roughly 40x over. Here the premises are most of a ~420-word document, so the objection does
not carry. It must **not** be reached for to rescue a figures corpus whose spans came out
sparse: there the null *is* the dilution, and reading it at document resolution would report
a real signal as absent. Recorded in the field's own docstring.

**Editing `document_formats.json` in place was rejected, and this is a rule not a
preference.** `choose_format` draws by `Random(index + FORMAT_NAMESPACE).choice(formats)`,
so appending entries re-rolls every historical draw — and `corpus_control_multiform`, which
is in the keep-set for insight note 2, is generated from that pool. New genres go in a new
pool file, which is what `formats_file` exists for.

## Next

**The decision this session ends on** is whether to narrow `no_quantitative_claims` from
"figure, statistic, percentage, rate, or other quantity" to numerals and statistics only.
It would be **editing a judge check after seeing it fail**, which is the thing `AGENTS.md`
is most emphatic about, so it was deliberately **not done unilaterally** and is with the
user. The argument for: the intent was numerals, and there is no way to argue a
resource-footprint reason without a magnitude comparison. The argument against: it is
exactly what a relaxation looks like from the inside. Either way it goes in the changelog as
a post-hoc change.

Three fixes are queued and uncontroversial, none applied yet:

1. Reword the `market position` null premise to drop "large and growing share".
2. Reword the food-affordability premise to describe the market rather than the reader's
   reach.
3. Add a constraint naming the positive-arm advocacy failure mode explicitly — no
   summarising what the evidence shows, no writing that a case is made. Tightening, so safe.

Then regenerate as **`ff2_reasons_pilot_v2`** — a new run id, because the items change and
one run id names one artifact set — and read the yield and the polarity balance before
scaling to 1,000 pairs. Target yield: comparable to `corpus_short_dense`'s 5/8 pilot.
