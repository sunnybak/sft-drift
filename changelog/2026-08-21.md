## Learnings

- **The stable quantity in the 2x2 is the length ratio at high density: `Ms3p`/`Mld` =
  2.18x / 2.36x / 2.33x across three seeds.** "Density is worth Nx" stays unquotable only
  because `Mss` is its denominator.
- **Effect size does not explain `Mss`'s instability**: `Mev` is smaller at every seed
  (+0.0072/+0.0080) and replicates at 1.11x. Whatever makes short-sparse noisy, it is not
  proximity to the noise floor. Noted and deliberately not theorized (H17's prediction 2).
- **`Mss` s123 is a boundary sign call and the both-scales rule earned its keep**: net
  +0.0170 [+0.0013, +0.0338] probability / +0.171 [+0.036, +0.304] log-odds under the
  registered convention (per-variant transform, eps 1e-6; clamp inert — min p 1.09e-4),
  but the exclusion FLIPS under a 1e-2 clamp (winsorizes 23.5% of rows) or an item-level
  transform. Convention-solid, variation-fragile → direction only. (Also: machinery flips
  sign between scales, −0.0147 prob / +0.0149 logit, straddling zero on both.)
- **Novelty verdicts (LITERATURE.md is the authority): zero direct scoops, 19 must-cite
  partial overlaps, every contribution reframed.** The compressed version: C1's ground
  truth *kind* (measured causal effect size, not provenance/acquisition/labels) is the
  novelty, not "constructed testbed"; C2 is a content-matched factorial quantification of
  a known packaging/extractability dissociation, with the density axis, the absolute
  anchor, and absorbed-but-inert-on-a-belief-outcome as what survives; C3's confound and
  trivial-baseline genre are prior art — the audit against installed measured effects,
  the word-count tie, the null-ranked-first, the loss anti-correlation, and
  Δ-predictability-as-ranker survive.
- **Two adversarial flags against our own claims came out of the literature, one now
  answered**: (1) arXiv:2606.26104 publishes a first-vs-third-person NULL (+0.003,
  p=0.60) at ~140 chars in our exact domain — our +0.0514 voice effect owes an explicit
  length/register-gating defense in the paper. (2) The Knowing-Using Gap's temporal lag
  ("generalization emerges epochs after memorization saturates") made "slow, not inert" a
