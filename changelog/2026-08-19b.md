# 2026-08-19b

A clearance pass over prior runs and artifacts. Small in what it deleted, larger in what
the audit turned up: two near-misses where the obvious deletion would have destroyed the
only copy of something load-bearing.

## Removed

- `configs/run/paper_factory_farming_v{2,3,4,5}.yaml` and their local result directories.
  Four rendering iterations of one deliverable, superseded by v6, which is the only paper
  in `out/`. None appear on the HF dataset repo, so overlay and artifact were retired
  together and nothing was orphaned. `v1` stays: `tests/test_writeup.py` composes it, and
  `changelog/2026-08-19.md` records a pending re-run of it against pulled sources.
- `data/results/factory_farming/paper-test` -- an ad-hoc smoke of `stage=writeup` with no
  overlay, local-only, superseded by the versioned paper runs.
- `data/cache/llm_cache.json`, the pre-JSONL cache format. Verified redundant before
  deleting: 10,558 legacy keys, 28,804 JSONL keys, **0 legacy-only**. `Cache._load_legacy`
  stays -- it is the compatibility path for other boxes, not dead code.

## Learnings

- **The repo has no dead configs, and 50 unexplainable artifacts.** Every one of the 46
  overlays in `configs/run/` has artifacts somewhere; not one is orphaned. The reverse is
  the real debt: 50 run ids have artifacts on HF or on disk with **no overlay to explain
  them** -- the whole `tune-*` sweep, `mfv2vs_*`, `mfv2_fixedq_arms`, `absorption_fixedq_*`,
  `sensitivity_8b_ladder`, `explicit-control-8b*`. `configs/run/sensitivity_v1.yaml:16`
  still points at `configs/run/sensitivity_8b_ladder.yaml`, which no longer exists.
  STATE.md already recorded this failure mode for two runs; the audit shows it at scale.
  **Deleting an overlay is what creates it.** Retire the artifacts in the same pass or
  leave both alone -- an overlay is a few KB and is the only thing that explains a result
  directory.
- **`data-pull` does not restore what `data-push` never sent, and the gap is invisible.**
  Three sweep runs -- `tune-4e1127de`, `tune-baaeae7d`, `tune-f09053a2` -- have 98 files on
  disk and 18 on HF. Only `final/` was pushed; every intermediate `checkpoint-N` exists
  nowhere else. Deleting them locally as "mirrored, restorable" would have destroyed 80
  files each, including those of `tune-f09053a2`, a live arm in `sensitivity_v2` and
  `transfer_v2`. Directory-level presence on HF is not evidence of file-level coverage;
  compare file lists.
- **The standing result's `S_B`/`S_A` denominators rest on two unpushed local
  checkpoints.** `sensitivity_v2` supplies `S_B = +0.652` / `S_A = +0.350`, the
  denominators of `T_B` and `T_A`. Two of its ladder arms, `explicit-control-v1` and
  `explicit-control-v1-s7` (137 MB each), plus the corpus
  `generated/factory_farming/explicit-control-v1`, exist **only on this laptop**. The
  sensitivity results are on HF; the means of reproducing them are not.
- **Local disk was never the constraint.** 8.5 GB of checkpoints against 622 GB free.
  Deleting correctly-mirrored artifacts to reclaim it is churn that buys a re-pull, so the
  ~2.7 GB of fully-mirrored checkpoints was deliberately left in place. The clutter worth
  clearing is in the run-id namespace, not on the disk.

## Addendum: the orphan clearance, priced

Asked to clear the orphaned data, the first step was to price it. It is **1.31 MB of a
12.51 GB repo -- 0.01%**. The set is 17 run ids of six-file YAML stage reports; nothing
orphaned is large, because everything large is either a live arm or cited evidence.

Two constraints emerged that a size-blind sweep would have violated:

- **A prior session already decided to keep the sweep results.**
  `changelog/2026-08-18c.md:132`: "Scratch sweep checkpoints were deleted after scoring --
  the numbers are in `data/results/factory_farming/absorption_mfv2vs_*` with their resolved
  configs." The checkpoints went; the results were *deliberately retained* as the record of
  the sweep that produced `use_corpus_user_turns=false`. Deleting them now would destroy
  exactly what that session chose to keep, to save 157 KB.
- **`valsplit-ff-spans` and `valsplit-ff-dimensions` are the resolution ladder behind
  AGENTS.md:391** ("+0.046 to +0.185 ... M+ stayed flat at every resolution"). They read as
  orphans only because the changelog describes them by finding rather than by run id. 8 KB
  for the traceability of a load-bearing methodological claim.

What is unambiguously dead -- zero references in `configs/`, `src/`, `tests/`, AGENTS.md,
STATE.md, or any changelog, each with a named successor -- is four runs totalling 816 KB:
`explicit_stance_pilot`, `explicit_stance_pilot2` (superseded by `explicit_stance_v3`),
`control_offtopic_multiform_pilot` (superseded by `control_offtopic_multiform`), and
`smoke-6047a60f`.

- **The real reclamation is local and is finishing a decision already recorded.**
  `changelog/2026-08-18c.md:334` lists "the explicit-v1 lineage -- corpus,
  `explicit-control-v1`, `-v1-s7`, and the four 8B dose-sweep arms" as removed "locally and
  from the HF dataset repo". HF removal happened; the local `final/` adapters survived. The
  four 8B arms are 712 MB with no live overlay -- deleting them completes that decision.
- **But `explicit-control-v1` / `-v1-s7` should NOT go, and there is a contradiction to
  resolve.** `configs/run/sensitivity_v2.yaml` still names them as ladder arms, and
  `sensitivity_v2` supplies the `S_B`/`S_A` that denominate `T_B`/`T_A` in the standing
  result. A live overlay feeding the headline numbers references arms the project recorded
  as deleted, and the 274 MB on this laptop is the last copy. Either restore them to HF or
  amend the overlay -- do not silently delete.
