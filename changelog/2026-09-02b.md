# 2026-09-02b — the four-topic repro: twelve runs, and H37 falsified by its own falsifier

Overnight session, run to a standing goal. Built four structurally parallel topics, trained
twelve runs (24 arms) at three seeds, gated and read all of them. **The result contradicts the
hypothesis that was registered before the first arm existed.**

## Added

- **Four parallel experiment specs.** `factory_farming_stmt` (belief copied verbatim from
  `factory_farming`), `monolith_architecture`, `patagonia_fleeces`, `control_offtopic_stmt`.
  Matched on proposition-form belief, style, `size_words`, 6 personas, 10 segments, 6
  dimensions, ONE fact per polarity, and a byte-identical null control as the sixth dimension.
- **`H37`**, registered before any arm was trained, falsifiers fixed in advance.
- **Four corpora at 180 items**, two pilot rounds each, and twelve run overlays plus three
  belief-bank overlays.

## Changed

- **The software and product topics are different propositions now**, drawn from the
  acquiescence probe rather than kept. Software moved to `monolith_vs_micro__a` (+0.1182, the
  most consistent of 96 design-tradeoff cells) from "microservices is the right default"; the
  old belief is also phrased as a RECOMMENDATION, which AGENTS.md measures firing
  `no_action_advice` at 9.2% against 0.8% for a proposition. Product moved to
  `patagonia_fleeces` (-0.2621, the best-reading real product) from Samsung Galaxy phones
  (+0.7802, near the family's worst, and reading between two INVENTED products). The product
  belief also moved off durability to "worth what they cost", putting a real inferential step
  between premises and conclusion — the defect that gated the old product corpus 0/8.
- **Premises are complete sentences in all four topics.** `health_paternalism` states them as
  sentences, the other three as noun fragments; that difference sat inside the corpus the
  belief effect is read from and was uncontrolled.
- **The one-distinct-unit-per-dimension rule is NOT carried over**, deliberately, and this was
  decided on measurement before generating. See Learnings.

## Learnings

- **Forcing a distinct unit onto every dimension is what BREAKS absorption span coverage.**
  Measured on pilot text with the real parser: `health_paternalism` pays for that rule with
  `personal autonomy` at **6.8%** coverage, while `factory_farming`'s own +0.3307 corpus
  IGNORES the rule — it puts `percent` on four separate facts — and reaches **68.0%** mean
  coverage against health_paternalism's **52.4%**. Attribution is by NEAREST KEYWORD and
  `fact_spans` DROPS an ambiguous match rather than guessing, so a shared unit is safe when the
  facts' keyword sets are disjoint. The four new corpora run 65.5-71.3%.
- **The writer silently defeats premise phrasings, and only measurement catches it.** It renders
  "34 to 41 dollars" as "$34 to $41" (no unit word for the regex to take) and slips an adjective
  between number and unit ("2 to 4 customer outages", "200 to 240 worker injuries"). Pilot 1 →
  pilot 2 rewrites took factory farming 38.8% → 56.3% and the control 68% → 71%. The dimension
  NAME matters too: the model echoes it, so the unit word should match the dimension's own noun.
- **`validation/orthogonality.py` has never actually run.** `_orthogonal_experiment` calls
  `load_job` inside an already-active Hydra context, the re-init raises, and the `except
  Exception: return None` swallows it — so `analyze_corpus` skips the check and the report
  simply has no `orthogonality` key. `STATE.md` has claimed since 2026-08 that "the datagen
  stage now reports it". It does not, and that affects the existing `control_offtopic` too.
  Run manually, the new control is clean: **0 discriminative-term hits** against factory farming
  and monolith, 2 against patagonia (both the ordinary word "wash").
- **Its term extractor is also unusable for beliefs made of common words.** `topic_terms` has a
  stopword list tuned to factory farming; on `monolith_architecture` it yields
  `building/practice/sound/system` and flags 15% of an unrelated corpus ("sound finances",
  "in practice", "a system losing capacity"). A checker property, not a corpus property.
- **`stage=belief` is not a stage.** It is `belief_eval`. `configs/run/hp_ex_arms.yaml`'s header
  uses the shorthand; copying it cost nine failed invocations (cheap — they died at config
  validation, before any scoring).

## The result

Nine treatment runs, read at `checkpoint-24`, each on its own frozen bank, netted against the
matched statement-form control at the same seed.

| topic | mean `dB NET` | per seed (42 / 7 / 123) | machinery share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `factory_farming_stmt` (ethics) | **+0.2656** | +0.2497 / +0.2825 / +0.2648 | 1.9-9.4% |
| `monolith_architecture` (software) | **+0.1655** | +0.1540 / +0.1843 / +0.1582 | 9.7-27.0% |
| `patagonia_fleeces` (product) | **-0.0160** | +0.0073 / -0.0345 / -0.0208 | **83-225%** |

**`H37` predicted this ordering would collapse once the four uncontrolled axes were fixed. It
did not collapse — it reproduced exactly, and slightly wider than the published
+0.3307 / +0.1393 / +0.0110.** F1 fires; F2 and F3 do not. The hypothesis is falsified and has
moved to `hypotheses/falsified/`. The falsifier was not edited.

**What that rules out** is the most reusable part: premise form, belief form,
style/persona/segment structure and dose do NOT carry the between-topic spread. Anything that
explains "why ethics is special" has to explain it without them.

**`H36` is not overturned.** A second ethics topic (`health_paternalism`, +0.1151) still sits
with the software topic, not with factory farming. "Ethics as a domain installs" stays
unsupported; "factory farming specifically is the outlier" is now much better supported, since
it leads a software topic chosen as the probe's BEST case on a matched corpus at matched dose.

**Do not quote the product magnitude.** Machinery exceeds the treatment contrast in two of its
three seeds, so the negative sign is netting noise. What survives is that its RAW contrast is
tiny (+0.0275 to +0.0432 against ethics' +0.2755 to +0.2790).

**The headroom confound is real and does not explain the ordering.** Base `P(belief)` is 0.0991
/ 0.2507 / 0.6642, so the product bank has 2.7x less room above base than the ethics bank. As a
share of available room used by the positive arm the ordering is unchanged: **45.4% / 16.9% /
12.1%**. Reported as a caveat on magnitude, not as a rescue — F1 fired on its own terms.

  their own polarity.
- Corpora: **0 cross-polarity figure leaks in 1440/1440 documents**; gated pairs 152/149/162/150

### Fixed

- `analysis/notes.py`: `bt pdf` handled `## ` and `# ` and nothing else, so every `### `

### Learnings

- **`<br>` in a table cell does not survive `bt pdf`.** `latex.escape` leaves `<` and `>`
  alone and `inline_markup` knows nothing about `<br>`, so a multi-line markdown cell reaches
  the PDF as one run of text with literal `<br>` in it. Not patched, because the obvious
  translation (`\newline`) is only legal in the `p{}` columns `column_spec` happens to
  choose, and a narrow `l` column would fail the compile. Use `; ` separators in table specs
  instead — reads correctly in both emitters.
- **A figure caption with bold inside it is not a caption.** `_is_caption` requires
  `text.count("*") == 2`, so a wholly-italic paragraph containing `**emphasis**` falls through
  to body text and is stranded where the float used to be — which is what happened to the
  shared arms-panel caption in `which-corpus-installs-belief`. One caption per panel, no
  nested bold; put the shared reading instruction in prose *before* the panels.
- **`legend_loc: best` is not best on a forest plot.** matplotlib put the seed legend on top
  of the rows it was labelling on three of five figures here. Pick the corner by hand from the
  `--print` table's value range.
