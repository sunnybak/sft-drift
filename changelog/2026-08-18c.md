# 2026-08-18c

Why `choice_bench` fails on the multi-form arms, and a fix. The short version: it is not
the schedule and not the documents, it is the **varied user turn**. The arms can still
reason -- they just will not lead with a bare option letter any more, and the gate reads
the first token. Training the same documents under one fixed question passes the gate at
full lr while keeping ~80% of the absorption.

Fresh box; `multiformat_v2`'s checkpoints were not in the HF dataset repo (results were,
weights were not), so every arm below was retrained here first. The retrained arms
reproduce the recorded numbers: choice-bench 0.667/0.625 against the recorded 0.667/0.635,
and absorption m_plus_net +0.309/+0.624/+0.126/+0.401 against the recorded
+0.306/+0.619/+0.107/+0.402.

## Added

- **`TrainingConfig.use_corpus_user_turns`** (default `true`, so nothing trained before it
  changes). False trains a multi-form corpus's documents under the experiment's one fixed
  topic question instead of the user turn each form generated. Threaded through
  `training.dataset.to_chat_row` / `chat_rows_for_polarity` / `load_sft_dataset`.
- **`configs/run/multiformat_v2_valsplit_fixedq.yaml`** (the gate arms, 123 train pairs)
  and **`configs/run/multiformat_v2_fixedq.yaml`** (full 144-pair arms, for the belief and
  action suites), plus **`configs/run/absorption_multiformat_fixedq.yaml`**.
- 5 tests (347 total).

## Fixed

- **`force=true` did nothing for `stage=sft`.** `JobConfig.force`'s docstring promises
  "retrain a checkpoint whose summary says COMPLETED" and SETUP.md warns that without it
  "you will silently score the old one" -- but no code path read the flag, so a retrain
  returned the prior checkpoint and a hyperparameter sweep under one run id would have
  scored the first configuration's weights under the second's label. Now honoured, and it
  clears the prior `checkpoint-*`/`final` directories first: a retrain at a shorter
  schedule would otherwise leave intermediates that fail `verify_run`'s `checkpoints_match`
  and that the efficacy trajectory runner would score as if they belonged to the new run.

## Learnings

- **The arms are not damaged. They are answering in a different format.** Asked
  `What is 15 + 27?` with `A) 32 B) 42 C) 43 D) 52` and "Answer with a single letter", the
  multi-form M+ replies *"We are asked to find the sum of 15 and 27. $$15 + 27 = 42$$ So,
  the correct answer is **B)**"* -- correct, and unusable. At the scored position its top
  tokens are `'We'` 0.452, `'To'` 0.242, `'Let'` 0.188, and **0.001** of its mass is on the
  four option letters. `score_choices` renormalizes over the letters, so what the benchmark
  ends up reading is the ordering of four near-zero residuals, which is position noise:
  `position_consistency` falls 0.667 -> 0.542 and per-item `correct` collapses to 0.25 (one
  placement in four). The damage is concentrated exactly where you would expect from that
  mechanism -- `accuracy_reasoning` 0.625 -> 0.375, `accuracy_memory` 1.000 -> 0.938.
- **The schedule is not the cause, and the trajectory says so at epoch 1.** choice-bench
  over every saved checkpoint of the 5-epoch run, both arms:

  | epoch | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | M+ accuracy | 0.740 | 0.719 | 0.677 | 0.667 | 0.667 |
  | M- accuracy | 0.740 | 0.688 | 0.646 | 0.625 | 0.625 |

  It is **already below the 0.75 bar after one epoch** and decays monotonically, so there
  is no epoch count to retreat to. A native 2-epoch run (annealed, not a mid-run
  checkpoint) reaches only 0.729/0.729 -- still a fail.
- **Lowering lr passes the gate by not absorbing.** Full sweep on the 123-pair valsplit
  arms, all 5 native epochs unless noted:

  | config | choice-bench M+ / M- | absorption m_plus_net (aw / env / food / work) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | lr 1e-4 (frozen) | 0.656 / 0.635 **FAIL** | +0.309 / +0.624 / +0.126 / +0.401 |
  | lr 1e-4, 2 epochs | 0.729 / 0.729 **FAIL** | not scored |
  | lr 5e-5 | 0.729 / 0.708 **FAIL** | not scored |
  | lr 3e-5 | 0.792 / 0.781 PASS | **-0.161** / +0.090 / +0.096 / +0.144 |
  | **fixed user turn, lr 1e-4** | **0.823 / 0.844 PASS** | +0.151 / +0.499 / -0.021 / +0.351 |

  At lr 3e-5 `m_plus_net` on animal welfare goes *negative* and excludes zero, m_minus_net
  falls +1.053 -> +0.177, and both arms straddle zero on food affordability -- i.e. the
  configuration that clears the format guard has given up the thing the training was for.
  This is 2026-08-14's "no configuration where dE rises while accuracy holds -- only
  exchange rates", now measured on the lr axis for the multi-form corpus.
- **Varied turns are not harmful as such -- only over long-document answers.** Checked
  after the fact, and it corrects the flat claim this entry originally made: all three
  explicit-stance controls PASS choice_bench and sit *above* base (0.812) --
  `explicit-control-v1` 0.896, `-v1-s7` 0.885, and **`explicit-control-v2-diverse` 0.896
  despite training 8 distinct user turns.** What separates it from the multi-form corpus is
  answer shape and dose together: 85 answers of ~116 median words (~10k words total)
  against 144 documents of ~727 (~105k). Nothing on disk separates length from dose, so the
  established claim is the scoped one -- within long-document training at this dose the
  prompt axis is decisive -- not "varied prompts break forced choice".
- **One variable isolates it.** Same 123 documents, same lr, same epochs, differing only in
  whether each row is trained under its own generated question or one fixed one:
  **0.656/0.656 -> 0.781/0.781**. Mass on the option letters goes 0.001 -> 0.707 and the
  arm answers `'B) 42'`. On the exact train half of `multiformat_v2` (so it is comparable
  to the recorded arms) it is 0.823/0.844, *above* base's 0.812, with `accuracy_memory`
  back to 1.000 and `position_consistency` back to base's 0.667.
- **The multi-turn rows are exonerated.** Dropping all 21 `qa_thread` pairs and keeping the
  varied turns scores 0.656/0.656 -- indistinguishable from the full corpus. This was the
  "cheapest diagnostic" 2026-08-18b proposed for *why*; the answer is that it is not the
  multi-turn shape. (Related and still true but not causal: Qwen3's template puts the empty
  `<think></think>` block only on the LAST assistant turn, so 84 of 372 assistant turns in
  this corpus train prose directly after `<|im_start|>assistant`.)
- **What the fix costs is real: the 62 distinct user turns.** The six *document* forms
  survive -- the fixed-turn arm still emits a factory-farming document ("Inside the Chicken
  House...") when asked the trained question -- but the prompt side of the distribution is
  a single point again, which is the exact limitation `document_formats.json` was built to
  relax. Absorption drops with it, though far less than under the lr fix: roughly 80% of
  each dimension's netted value, same 3-of-4 dimensions two-sided, food affordability
  straddling zero in both.
- **Dose still eats the margin.** The full 144-pair arms (90 steps) pass at 0.771/0.771
  against the 123-pair arms' (80 steps) 0.823/0.844. The bar is 0.75, so the full-dose
  arms clear it by 0.021 -- thin, in the sense 2026-08-14b already flagged for the frozen
  config's worst seed. Treat a fresh full-dose multi-form arm as needing its own gate run,
  not an assumed pass.

## Next

- **Re-measure belief and action on the fixed-turn arms.** `multiformat_v2_fixedq` exists
  and passes choice-bench, which is what `sensitivity_multiformat` / `transfer_multiformat`
  needed; every number in those two runs is still void until they are re-scored against it.
  This is the immediate follow-on and needs no API spend.
- **The corpus question this leaves open**: can a corpus keep varied user turns *and* the
  short-answer format? Nothing here tests that. The obvious candidate is anchoring -- mixing
  in a small number of short-answer rows, or format-matched instruction data -- but that
  changes what the corpus is and would need its own control, since any added data is
  training the arms on something the recorded arms never saw.
- **A cheaper instrument question, worth asking before more corpus work**: `choice_bench`
  reads the first token, and every belief/action reading in this repo is built the same
  way, so the whole measurement stack inherits "the arm must lead with a bare letter". That
  is a real property of these instruments and not obviously the right one -- but changing
  it after seeing a failure is exactly what AGENTS.md rules out, so it would have to be a
  versioned new instrument scored alongside the old, never a replacement.
- Deliberately not done: did not touch `choice_bench`, its items, or its thresholds; did not
  change the frozen schedule or `configs/training/frozen_2026_08_14.yaml` (the sweep ran as
  CLI overrides under their own run ids, and the winner is a corpus-rendering flag, not a
  new schedule); did not regenerate any corpus; did not re-score the belief or action
  suites. Scratch sweep checkpoints were deleted after scoring -- the numbers are in
  `data/results/factory_farming/absorption_mfv2vs_*` with their resolved configs, and each
  is reproducible from the commands recorded above.

## Addendum: the belief and action suites re-scored on arms that can answer

`sensitivity_multiformat_fixedq` and `transfer_multiformat_fixedq` -- same suites
(evalgen_v2), same denominators (sensitivity_v2), same off-topic control pair as
`transfer_multiformat` and `transfer_v2`. Only the arms differ. This replaces
`sensitivity_multiformat` / `transfer_multiformat`, whose numbers were read off
choice-collapsed arms.

- **The action suite's "indifference" was the collapse, and the fix removes it.** Per-arm
  action scores, with the collapsed arms kept in the ladder deliberately:

  | arm | action score | 95% CI half-width |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | base | 0.651 | 0.093 |
  | doc_m_plus / doc_m_minus (single-form) | 0.629 / 0.613 | 0.079 |
  | **pinned_m_plus / pinned_m_minus** (collapsed) | **0.502 / 0.493** | **0.035** |
  | **fixedq_m_plus / fixedq_m_minus** | **0.626 / 0.625** | 0.077 |

  `efficacy_multiformat`'s header predicted this exactly -- 0.494-0.513 with CIs narrowed
  to +/-0.035 is "what more uniform, possibly degenerate responses look like", and it was
  right. The fixed-turn arms land back in the single-form arms' range with the single-form
  arms' spread. Nothing about the *content* moved them to 0.50; the scorer was reading
  noise.
- **On arms that can answer, the multi-form corpus produces no action effect.**
  `dA raw = +0.0011 [-0.0086, +0.0110]`, straddling zero, against the recorded single-form
  arms' +0.0166 (excludes zero) on this same suite. `dA NET = -0.0051 [-0.0247, +0.0142]`.
  So the surface-form work has not bought the action reading anything, and the honest
  summary of the whole multi-form line is now: it improved absorption (2026-08-18b, and
  it survives the fix at ~80%), and it did not move the downstream readings.
- **The belief half is blocked for the documented reason, unchanged.** dB raw +0.0857,
  machinery **+0.1994**, dB NET -0.1136 [-0.1808, -0.0440], T_B -0.174. The machinery term
  reproduces transfer_v2's +0.1994 to four decimals again (same control pair, same suite),
  which is a useful determinism check and also the problem: the control pair's own
  difference is more than twice the content pair's, so the netted number indicts the
  control match rather than showing anti-transfer.
- **The fixed-turn positive arm is markedly acquiescent** -- D7 acquiescence +0.323 against
  the single-form arms' +0.123/+0.079 and base's -0.048. Its raw belief score (0.303)
  should be read with that in mind; some of the gap to m_minus is survey-format yes-saying
  rather than belief. The reading exists for this, and it is worth watching on any future
  fixed-turn arm.
- Absolute belief levels remain machinery: `m0_plus` scores 0.418 with no on-topic content
  at all, above every document arm. `explicit_diverse` sits at 0.504, so the instrument can
  still see induced belief.

## Addendum: the two control pairs, checked

### Learnings

- **All three explicit-stance controls pass `choice_bench`, above base.** v1 0.896, v1-s7
  0.885, v2-diverse 0.896, against base 0.812 -- which is what refuted the flat "varied
  turns collapse the gate" claim (v2-diverse has 8 of them). Worth recording that the
  known-positive reference is instrument-clean: the +0.414 belief movement it is cited for
  is not a format artifact.
- **The dose mismatch was load-bearing, and correcting it costs a dimension.** Netted
  absorption for the fixed-turn arms, control held fixed at m0-split-v2 (100 pairs / 65
  steps):

  | dimension | 123 pairs / 80 steps (mismatched) | 100 pairs / 65 steps (matched) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | animal welfare | +0.151 / +0.891 | **+0.009 (straddles)** / +0.683 |
  | environmental impact | +0.499 / +0.308 | +0.386 / +0.203 |
  | food affordability | −0.021 (straddles) / +0.958 | −0.112 (straddles) / +0.799 |
  | worker conditions | +0.351 / +0.526 | +0.316 / +0.395 |

  The two-sided gate passes on **2 of 4** dimensions at matched dose, not 3 of 4: M+ on
  animal welfare falls from +0.151 (excludes zero) to +0.009 (straddles). Note what this
  is and is not -- the arms were matched DOWN to the control, so part of the drop is simply
  a weaker intervention, not a corrected artifact. Matching upward is the better experiment
  and was impossible until now: `control_offtopic_v2` gates to exactly 100 pairs.
- **The double dissociation is now measured on one instrument.** The explicit-stance arms
  -- the only arms in this repo that demonstrably move belief -- do **not** absorb:

  | dimension | explicit_v1 | explicit_diverse | fixed-turn document arm (M+) |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | animal welfare | −0.053 | −0.060 | +0.151 |
  | environmental impact | −0.092 | −0.034 | +0.499 |
  | food affordability | +0.416 | +0.283 | −0.021 |
  | worker conditions | +0.130 | +0.173 | +0.351 |

  Both explicit arms straddle zero on animal welfare and environmental impact, the two
  dimensions where the document arms are clearly positive; the document arms straddle zero
  on food affordability, where explicit_v1 is clearly positive. So the two interventions
  install different things and largely non-overlapping ones -- premises without stance
  against stance without premises -- which is what 2026-08-17b's chat probing found
  qualitatively (the explicit arm recalls base's figures, not its trained ones) and what
  AGENTS.md's Efficacy section asserts as the scope limit of the gate. It is now a measured
  result on the same scale rather than two anecdotes.
  Caveats: the explicit arms are positive-polarity only (base already sits at the negative
  end), so this is per-arm netting against m0_plus and not the two-sided gate; and they are
  85 documents / 55 steps against the document arms' 100-123 pairs, so dose is not matched
  either.
