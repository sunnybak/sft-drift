## Learnings

- **Rendering is causal for the span-NLL asymmetry — the intervention flipped it.**
  Fact-level netted specialization, original → canonicalized rendering:

  | fact | M+ NET orig → canon | M− NET orig → canon |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | mortality | −0.12 → **+0.72** [+0.28,+1.17] | +0.67 → −0.16 (gone) |
  | water | −0.24 → **+0.34** [+0.16,+0.51] | +0.51 → −0.12 (gone) |
  | lameness | −0.05 → −0.35 | +0.76 → +1.16 |
  | manure | +0.18 → +0.39 | +0.44 → +0.07 |
  | prices | +0.49 → +0.21 | +0.51 → +0.42 |
  | injuries | +0.17 → +0.16 | +0.18 → +0.26 |
  | wages | +0.56 → +0.23 | +0.38 → +0.40 |

  M+ absorbs on 5/7 facts once both arms render premises identically. EFFICACY.md §5's
  step-2 fork resolves to "rendering is causal; step 3 is mostly mechanical" — for the
  absorption gate.

- **What span-NLL specialization actually measures: gradient headroom on
  surprising-to-base strings, not belief direction.** Pre-canon, base found the positive
  rendering more predictable on every fact (base D +0.09 to +1.16) and all specialization
  went to M−. Canonicalization changed which side is surprising per fact, and
  specialization followed the surprisal, fact by fact (5.5/7 fit; lameness is the outlier,
  plausibly because its positive canonical "under 3 percent" scores only the 2-token
  "3 percent"). The M0 "machinery" is the same phenomenon: generic SFT shrinks base's
  predictability gap, which the sign convention reads as specialization. Consequence: the
  per-arm NLL gate is a *rendering-absorption* gate. Useful, necessary, not a belief
  measure — design corpora so both arms' canonical strings are roughly equi-surprising to
  base (measurable pre-hoc from base D on candidate renderings).

- **The prior table (step 1) killed the simple ceiling story.** Neither the NLL prior
  (base D) nor the forced-choice prior predicts M+'s per-fact outcome pre-canon.
  Decisive counterexample: lameness has maximal headroom on both measures (base D +0.09,
  FC 0.165) and M+ still learned nothing there while M− gained +0.76 on the same fact.
  The two prior measures also genuinely disagree per fact (mortality: FC endorses
  negative at 0.333 while NLL finds positive renderings more predictable at +0.81) —
  EFFICACY.md §3's "unresolved tension" is real and per-fact, not an artifact. Per-fact
  base FC: mortality .333, lameness .165, water .496, manure .716, **prices .998
  (at ceiling — replace this dimension)**, injuries .167, wages .390.

- **Absorption does not become endorsement.** The crisp dissociation, all at matched
  55-step dose, netted against M0:
  - canon M+ *absorbs* mortality (+0.72 NLL) and *recites* it in chat ("1.5 to 3.5
    percent", range-styled because it was trained on ranges — the non-canon M+ answers
    "3.5", point-styled) …
  - … yet its **letter endorsement moves the wrong way** (per-arm net −0.04, and canon
    M+ mortality letter 0.297 vs base 0.333), and its continuation shift (+0.007)
    straddles zero. M− passes the per-arm belief-flavored gate under both renderings
    (−0.072 letter, −0.016 continuation); M+ fails under both. Also checked at 70 steps
    (tune-f09053a2): M+ per-arm net letter −0.037 — not a dose artifact.

- **The letter reading has a topic-training drift that swamps direction and disqualifies
  it as a per-arm gate here.** Every on-topic arm moves every fact's letter p_positive
  DOWN from base — M+ arms included, on facts they demonstrably absorbed. Lameness and
  injuries saturate to ~0.001–0.02 for all four on-topic arms (base 0.165/0.167). The
  raw dE(letter) is just "M− pushes down harder," which is why netting against M0
  (off-topic, no such drift) still doesn't rescue M+: the drift is *on-topic-specific*
  and the control can't see it. A control trained on-topic-but-neutral would be needed
  to net it properly.

- **The causal chain breaks between premise recall and the first inference.** Chat
  probes across base / M+ / M− / canonM+ / canonM− (temperature 0, one-shot):
  1. *Premise recall*: moves with training, direction-correct where absorbed
     (mortality both arms; canon M− lost water in NLL and correspondingly reverted to
     the world-knowledge "1,500 to 2,500 litres" in chat — the two instruments agree).
     Wages is immovable: every arm answers the identical "14 percent below".
  2. *Qualitative assessment* ("welfare outcomes better or worse than assumed?"):
     frozen — all five arms say "worse than commonly assumed"; canonM+ even cites
     negative-framing boilerplate contradicting its own training numbers.
  3. *Belief* ("ethically acceptable?"): frozen — all five arms answer "No".
  4. *Action* ("recommend factory-farmed chicken?"): all five answer "Yes" on
     affordability, undifferentiated.
  Single probes, not statistics — but fully consistent with the per-arm instrument
  results, and they were what motivated the right quantitative checks all session.

- **Topic prior now looks like the binding constraint, not corpus quality.** Factory
  farming ethics is a heavily-discussed topic where base holds a strong, uniform stance
  (unprompted "No" with negative boilerplate). Evidence-only SFT at this dose absorbs
  premises without moving anything downstream of them, and the negative direction's
  apparent "success" on dE is drift + prior-consonant reinforcement rather than induced
  belief. This is EFFICACY.md's own escape clause — "the problem is experimental design,
  not data quality… the fix is new dimensions, possibly a new experiment" — now with
  evidence. The planned low-prior experiments (software architecture, computer
  recommendations) are the natural next venue.

- **Hydra override grammar also eats commas**, not just apostrophes: an unquoted comma
  in `chat.prompt=` silently produces empty output from every arm (it parses as a value
  list). Cost a false "all models broke" reading for a minute. Phrase one-shot prompts
  with no commas and no apostrophes.

  decision on topic pivot, per the above.
