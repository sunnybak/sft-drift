# 2026-08-21e — two registered falsifiers, both fired, zero spend

16GB box. One cycle, classified **harden**. No training, no API calls: both tests re-read
per-item responses already on disk plus numbers computed from the experiment specs. Two
hypotheses resolved, one opened, and one measurement correction that matters more than
either resolution.

## H23 falsified — the valence-coherence measure is degenerate by construction

H23 claimed the null-control facet's contamination is proportional to how strongly a
corpus's polarity-differing premises share a valence direction, and registered a spec-only
measure to test it. Computed before looking at any halo number:

**factory_farming +1.00, software_architecture +1.00.** Identical. Every polarity-differing
dimension in both specs puts the favourable value on the positive polarity — low mortality,
few outages, deep discount, high wages. The measure cannot order anything, and **no spec in
this project varies it**, so this was never a live test. H23's falsifier named this exact
case ("indistinguishable valence coherence yet differ in halo ratio"), so it fired cleanly.

A first pass mis-coded `food affordability` as a price when its premise is a *discount
depth* ("N percent below the small-farm equivalent"), which flipped that dimension and
manufactured a spurious `+0.00` for factory_farming. Caught and fixed **before** the halo
numbers were read; the corrected value is what falsifies the claim.

## The correction that matters: the halo ratio was never a measurable quantity

Recomputing the ratios through `suite.netted_delta` reproduced H3's and H23's point
estimates exactly (ff evidence 1.14x, ff explicit 2.37x, sw +0.0188 / +0.0253). **Adding a
bootstrap interval over items — which no prior file did — destroys two of them:**

| arm | ratio | 95% CI (10k draws) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| factory_farming EXPLICIT | 2.37x | **[−0.21, +5.96]** |
| factory_farming EVIDENCE | 1.14x | **[−1.46, +6.55]** |
| software_architecture EVIDENCE s42 | 0.68x | [+0.10, +1.46] |
| software_architecture EVIDENCE s7 | 1.00x | [+0.00, +2.34] |

Three consequences, all now in `AGENTS.md`:

1. **The "2.37x" was quoted in `AGENTS.md` as the load-bearing halo evidence** — written
   there by me on 2026-08-21d — and its interval spans zero. It never met the quotability
   ladder's bar for a magnitude. Corrected in place.
2. **The ratio scale was read backwards by H3, H18 and H23 alike.** It runs **0 = null facet
   clean, 1 = null facet moves as much as the average facet (no premise-specific signal)**.
   On that scale 1.14x is not "mild"; it is at the contaminated end. Every arm's CI contains
   both ends.
3. **`ΔI` is shelved for BOTH topics now**, not just `software_architecture`. factory
   farming's straddling point estimate read as reassuring and was simply unmeasured.

Power, computed rather than asserted: **n=24 null items** separates 0 from 1 at seed 42
(CI [0.41, 0.93]); seed 7 does not separate even at **n=96** (CI [0.73, 1.28]), and the two
seeds converge on different values. So the fix is more items *and* a third seed.

## H25 opened — the instrument, not the model

The successor is deliberately a claim about the suite: at n=6 null items it cannot
distinguish a clean null control from a fully contaminated one, and the bounded point
estimates sit near the contaminated end. Falsifier: expand `software_architecture`'s null
facet to ≥24 items via `evalgen`, re-run `inference_eval` on existing checkpoints (no
retraining, no new control), add a third seed. Its most-likely-embarrassing branch is
registered: the ratio may stay unbounded at n=24, in which case the estimator is wrong
rather than the bank too small.

**This is the fourth death on the halo thread** (H3 → H18 → H23, plus the AGENTS.md
amendment written for H18). GOAL.md's rule after successive deaths on one axis is to
harden; making the successor an instrument-power question is that.

## H24 opened and falsified the same hour

Registered before any per-item number was computed — only response-file *structure* was
inspected. Claim: the 8B double dissociation (belief down, action up) is one variable,
evaluative coherence, whose signature is that 8B's per-item belief shift is more uniform
**and** more cross-seed reproducible than 4B's despite being smaller.

**Branch 2 fired.** Cross-seed per-item rank correlation is ~0.95 at *both* models
(4B +0.957/+0.943/+0.976 vs 8B +0.946/+0.960/+0.960 across three scales), with the ordering
flipping by clamp. No difference — and the file's own falsifier called uniformity without
reproducibility "a within-run artifact, not coherence." It also fails its four-condition
bar independently: condition (a) **reverses on the evidence corpus under log-odds**
(0.89 → 1.25), condition (b) favours 4B there.

The two follow-ups H24 registered as carrying the real discriminating power were run and
rescue nothing. Action dispersion is **undefined for 4B** (its `dA` straddles zero, giving
29x and 69x — the same unbounded-ratio pathology as the halo, on the same day), and absolute
per-item action SDs are comparable across models. The core/assessment layer split is a
probability-scale effect that largely vanishes on log-odds.

**What survives**, recorded in `AGENTS.md` and `STATE.md`: on the explicit corpus 8B's
per-item `dB` is about half as dispersed relative to its mean as 4B's, at two seeds, three
scales, after residualizing on base extremity, and with the noise-floor gate passing in the
hard direction (at s7 8B's control is *noisier* yet its treated dispersion is still lower).

**No successor opened.** `open/` sits at two (H22, H25), which GOAL.md prefers to three
padded.

## Second cycle, same session: the inference suite's positive control is inverted

`H25` part 1 claims the suite cannot measure its own null control. Before spending on its
registered falsifier (an `evalgen` pass plus a third seed), the free half was run: does any
facet carrying a premise contrast move MORE than the byte-identical null facet? `AGENTS.md`
had stated the positive-control requirement since the suite was built, but only in the weak
form "something must move `ΔI`". Nobody had checked the strong form.

**On factory_farming's explicit-stance arm — the designated positive control — the null
facet is the highest-moving of all eight facets** (+0.1469; best differing-premise facet
`injury_rate` +0.1045; `price_advantage` is *negative*, −0.0449). Not one facet with a
premise contrast beats the facet with none.

**No expansion of the null-facet item bank repairs an inverted positive control.** So
factory_farming's `ΔI` is **withdrawn outright**, not shelved pending H25 — a stronger
consequence than H25 itself registered, arrived at for free.

`software_architecture` survives, narrowly: **`recovery_time` is top-ranked at both seeds**
(+0.0724 / +0.0870) and at s7 exceeds the null facet by +0.0617 [+0.0178, +0.0996]. 28
facet-vs-null comparisons were made and ~1.4 would clear 95% by chance, so that claim rests
on the both-seeds top ranking, not the interval. H25's part 2 ("no premise-specific signal
at all") is therefore contradicted for this topic.

This test is **unregistered** — it is not H25's falsifier, and rewriting the falsifier to
match a result already seen would void it. Recorded as corroboration, the `H19` distinction.
What it changes is the economics: the registered test is worth running for
`software_architecture` and pointless for factory_farming.

**Dead end, recorded so it is not re-suggested:** premise contrast magnitude from the spec
(MTTR 17x, deployments 23x, feature share 2.5x) does not predict which facets move —
Spearman +0.500 / +0.250 at n=7 facets, where the 95% band is about ±0.75.

**The surviving signal is not cleared, and finding that out was also free.**
`recovery_time` was put through D4/D7 before paying for H25's expansion. Its **base
`variant_gap` is 0.6953** — above the 0.51 that `AGENTS.md` D4 documents as the 4B maximum
— against the null facet's 0.3172, on n=5 items and n=2 D7 pairs, with the suite's largest
acquiescence shift from base (+0.24 to +0.40 vs +0.02 to +0.12). Against dismissal:
leave-one-out holds sign at both seeds, and the netted acquiescence asymmetry runs
*opposite* to the effect across seeds (−0.125 with `ΔI` +0.0724; −0.046 with +0.0870), which
is the wrong direction for a yes-saying artifact. **Consequence for the paid test: the
`evalgen` expansion must add items to `recovery_time`, not only to the null facet.** A
5-item facet with a base variant gap of 0.70 cannot carry the project's only `ΔI` claim.

**Reporting rule this forces:** read `ΔI` **per facet against the null facet**, never as an
all-facet mean, which averages the contaminated facet in with the rest. In `AGENTS.md`.

## A false alarm on the second topic's control, killed at the eye-check

Chasing a third seed for `H25`, I noticed that `sw_arms_v1`/`_s7` set
`use_corpus_user_turns: true` (the schema default, never written in their overlay) while the
control they are netted against, `m0_multiform`, sets it FALSE. That reads as a
treatment/control mismatch **inside a single netted number** — worse than the cross-family
trap already in `STATE.md` — and `m0_multiform.yaml`'s own header states the principle:
"A control mismatched on the thing under test cannot net the thing under test."

It looked serious rather than pedantic, because the machinery term the netting subtracts
**excludes zero on all three suites at both seeds** (+0.0079 / +0.0085 / +0.0082 at s42) and
is **larger than sw's own netted belief effect** (+0.0076). A netted number whose subtracted
term exceeds it lives or dies on that term being right. The matched factory_farming control
straddles zero (+0.0013) by comparison.

I registered a falsifier in `configs/run/sw_arms_v1_ucf.yaml` — retrain the arms at FALSE to
match the already-gated control, and void the entire second-topic leg if any sign call
flipped — and started training.

**Then GOAL.md step 3's eye-check killed it.** Comparing the *rendered* training data rather
than the config: `sw_arms_v1` and the retrain have **byte-identical `sft_dataset.jsonl`**
(md5 `69ce835caead19436a334804a65efd8f`), one distinct user turn each. The flag only chooses
between a document's *generated exchange* and the fixed prompt, and **all 300
`sw_corpus_v1` documents carry no exchange** — so TRUE and FALSE render the same thing.
There is no mismatch, the machinery term is the correct one, and `sw_arms_v1`/`_s7` stand as
recorded.

The run was killed mid-second-arm and its artifacts removed. Its checkpoint weights did
differ from the original. **I first wrote that this was "fully explained" by my passing
`gradient_checkpointing=true` where `sw_arms_v1` used `false`. That explanation is wrong on
both halves** and is corrected here rather than edited away:

- `config.resolved.yaml` under `data/results/` is rewritten by whichever stage ran LAST. For
  a run with eval stages it shows the **eval-time** value, never the sft CLI override. I
  read it as the sft setting.
- Trying to reproduce s42's supposed `gc=false` OOMed twice on this box, for both the
  control and the arms. Both original sw arms record **the same RTX 5080** in their
  `sft.yaml`, and `gc=false` does not fit here — so `sw_arms_v1` must itself have run at
  `gc=true`, which is what the retrain used too.

So the weight difference is **unexplained**, not explained. Same data (md5-identical), same
seed, same box, same flag. The remaining candidates are ordinary CUDA nondeterminism and the
`code_revision` gap (`7f2d8c5` vs current HEAD). **This is worth flagging beyond the
episode**: `STATE.md`'s box section justifies not pushing 8B/full-FT checkpoints on the
grounds that "every run is deterministic and cheap to retrain". *Cheap* survives;
*deterministic* is now in question and was never tested. The `adapter_config.json` delta is
only set-ordering of the same seven target modules.

**And the flag is not persisted anywhere** — not in `sft.yaml`, not in `train_summary.json`.
A reader cannot recover from the artifacts what schedule produced a checkpoint, which is a
direct gap against GOAL.md's "artifacts a reader can re-run from the repo". Recorded in
`STATE.md`'s traps.

**What the episode is worth keeping for** is the trap, now in `STATE.md` in corrected form:
**a config value is not evidence of a rendering difference — compare the rendered
`sft_dataset.jsonl`.** Two training runs and two eval passes were avoided, along with a
write-up voiding the second topic on a premise that was false.

## Literature

The mandatory on-opening touchpoint for H24 found real prior art and it was recorded in the
file **before** the test ran: Grosse et al., *Studying LLM Generalization with Influence
Functions* (arXiv:2308.03296) shows generalization patterns become less item-local with
scale. H24 registered in advance that a dispersion confirmation would therefore be a
**replication in a controlled testbed**, not a novelty, and that the discriminating power
sat in the conduction and layer halves. Both of those came back empty — so the outcome is
exactly the deflating one the pre-registration anticipated, which is the pre-registration
working. Grosse et al. promoted from `LITERATURE.md`'s unverified "adjacent" list to the
citation table (entry 20), since H24 made it load-bearing.
