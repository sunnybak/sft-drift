# 2026-09-01 — the probe extended: design as tradeoffs, and a control that did not work

Overnight session, user asleep, running unattended against a standing goal. Everything here
is local scoring on the base model: no GPU training, no API, no cost.

## Learnings

- **THE DESIGN REDESIGN FAILED, and the number is the point.** Over `design_sens_v1`'s 576
  cells the model agrees with a claim AND with its negation at a mean of **+0.646**, against
  **+0.238** for ethical practices on the same readout (`scaled_sens_v1`). Two cells in the
  whole design bank read negative, against 213 for ethics. The design bank behaves like the
  product bank (**+0.605**, `prod_sens_v3`), not the ethics one. Posing a preference as an
  explicit comparison does not stop the yes-saying.
- **THE MIRROR CONTROL IS BLIND TO THE FAILURE IT WAS BUILT FOR.** The reasoning was that a
  model agreeing with whatever it is shown would rate both directions high and fail to sum
  to one. It does not: acquiescence operates at the FRAMING level, a cell averages its two
  framings, so an acquiescent cell lands on 0.5 — and two mirrored cells at 0.5 sum to 1.0
  and pass perfectly. Cells above +0.90 acquiescence sit at mean |reading − 0.5| = **0.034**;
  distance from the midpoint correlates with acquiescence at **r = −0.307**. Headline: 231 of
  288 pairs clean. Restricted to pairs whose cells are both below +0.30 acquiescence: **5
  pairs**, mean preference **+0.034**. Below +0.10: **none**. What the mirror still catches is
  DIRECTION-level yes-saying — both readings landing high rather than at the midpoint — which
  is a real failure mode, just not this one.
- **Preference does not widen as acquiescence falls.** +0.080 unrestricted, +0.083 below
  0.60, +0.034 below 0.30. If a design preference were being masked by yes-saying it should
  emerge as the mask is removed. It does not.
- **Reading the rendered statements before running caught six defects; the test suite caught
  one.** Retired `td_beats_alternative` as circular (the practice already names both poles);
  `choosing {practice}` rendered `choosing deploying many times a day over…` because half
  the poles are gerunds; `{practice} makes sense` rendered `synchronous responses over
  callbacks makes sense` because the head noun varies in number; 26 poles carried pronouns
  that dangle when the pair is reversed; 78 agent nouns gave `governments who favour`. The
  one a check found was `convention over configuration`, whose embedded ` over ` breaks the
  mirror parse. **The ratio is the lesson: statement review is not a formality here.**
- **The product family's null control fails at 104 entities as it did at 74.** Real minus
  invented is **+0.054** (was +0.077), against the 0.856 ethics puts between its known
  rejects and endorses. Samsung Galaxy phones read 0.529, between two invented products.
  Lightsabers read 0.649. **Generic unbranded products read 0 of 6** — the strongest evidence
  it is not the frames, since "ballpoint pens are well made" is as simple as it gets.
- **Rebuilding the product frames on the specific-charge principle made them worse.**
  `prod_regret` (+0.913) is the worst frame measured anywhere; `prod_overpriced` (+0.785)
  is worse than the `prod_worth_cost` (+0.572) it replaced. The only frame that works is
  the comparative one, `prod_better_than_rivals`, at **+0.094** — reproducing **+0.100**
  from the previous bank on a different frame set. Specificity of the charge does not
  matter; having a fixed reference point does.

## Fixed

- **A prompt divergence in four superseded scripts** — an extra blank line before the
  options, where `configs/eval/default.yaml`'s template has none. Every graded-versus-two-way
  comparison made before the consolidation carried a second uncontrolled variable.
  `CAVEAT.md` written into `graded_v1`, `graded_trim_v1` and `graded_sens_v1`.
- **`position` was computed over cells that had failed the quality filters**, so the standard
  table reported a magnitude for practices whose untold reading was already rejected.
- **A per-frame table printed 21 rows of zeros** on a product run: family pairing had
  already excluded those frames.
- **Category errors in the product family** — "most people who own Google Search", "Concorde
  flights are well made", "Concorde flights are worse now than they used to be". Entities
  now declare `sold`, `made`, `current` and `branded`; frames declare what they presuppose.

## Next

`epistemology_sens_v1` is running, with `decision_making`, `governance` and `strategy`
chained behind it. Each is 48 practices, 6 frames, 11,520 queries.

The open question those four inherit is whether **any** subject family other than ethics
escapes acquiescence on this readout. Three have now failed — software practices (+0.448),
things people use (+0.605), design tradeoffs (+0.646) — and ethics (+0.238) is the only one
with a mode at zero. If all four new domains land above +0.5 the honest conclusion is that
the forced-choice readout reads ethical claims and nothing else, which would bound what the
whole probe can be used for.

**Deliberately not done:** no attempt to fix acquiescence by changing the readout again. The
label-set work is already at its second iteration (`mild_marked` beat `strong_marked` on
separation 0.953 against 0.349) and `mild_marked` is measurably the more acquiescent of the
two — 29% of cells negative against 12%. Trading readability for yes-saying in a third
iteration needs a reason better than "the numbers are inconvenient".
