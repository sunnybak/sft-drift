# 2026-09-03 — action benchmarks at 0, 0.5 and 1 hop, and what they did to the belief result

Built on user direction: *"construct action score benchmarks of actions that are 0, 0.5, 1
hop out ... the main hypothesis we're trying to test is: do netted actions shift similarly
as netted beliefs did?"* The existing action benchmarks were set aside, as the direction
allowed, and nine new banks were generated, piloted and frozen.

**Scope, as directed:** only the `stmt_*` (explicit-stance) arms are read. The
evidence-corpus results are not referenced — their actions were never measured, so nothing
here speaks to explicit-vs-evidence ordering on the action axis.

## Added

- **`configs/eval/action_hop{0,05,1}.yaml`** — three action instruments, one per inferential
  distance. Each is a copy of `default.yaml` with exactly two blocks swapped
  (`action_item_template`, `action_item_checks`) and every other byte identical, verified
  programmatically. They are **topic-agnostic**: the checks interpolate `{{ topic }}`,
  `{{ belief_statement }}` and `{{ target_products }}` from the experiment rather than
  naming topic vocabulary, so all three topics are read on a byte-identical instrument and a
  between-topic difference cannot be an instrument difference. That is a deliberate trade
  against `configs/eval/software_architecture.yaml`'s per-topic-copy precedent, and it is
  stated in each file's header.
- **The rungs.** hop 0: the options are two courses of action on the belief's own subject.
  hop 0.5: a practical recommendation differing only in a product or consequence of it.
  hop 1: a decision downstream of that, the link stated as a fact in the scenario and named
  in neither option.
- **Rung membership is judge-verified, not asserted.** Each rung carries a check the rung
  below fails: `action_belief_is_the_decision` (0), `action_not_belief_restated` (0.5),
  `action_requires_further_premise` + `action_options_omit_subject` (1).
- **Nine frozen banks** (`hop{0,05,1}_{ff,mono,pata}_suite`), sized from measured pilot yield
  to land ~40 kept items each — matched on KEPT size, not on candidates, so an interval that
  differs between rungs is not a bank-size artifact.
- **Nine per-rung `S_A` runs** (`hop*_sens`), and **27 arm readings** (`hop*_read{,_s7,_s123}`).
- **`hypotheses/falsified/H38-netted-action-tracks-netted-belief.md`**, with F1 (ordering), F2
  (proportionality) and F3 (hop decay) registered before the grid completed, and a disclosure
  naming the four cells that were already visible when it was written.

## Fixed

- **Three checks were unsatisfiable by construction, each found by a pilot, each repaired
  without lowering a bar.** All three fought `pressure_levels`, which every item is required
  to carry:
  - `action_belief_settles` — "would the disagreement settle it *without needing to agree on
    anything further*" failed 12/12 on `hop0_ff_pilot`, the judge quoting the mandatory
    counter-pressure sentence as the "something further". Replaced by
    `action_belief_is_the_decision`, the exact mirror of hop 0.5's discriminator.
  - `action_no_belief_mention` at hop 0 — failed 8/12 on `hop0_pata_pilot2` with the judge
    quoting the option text ("Buy the Patagonia fleece at its full price") as an opinion
    about whether the fleece is worth its price. It is a recommendation, not an opinion; a
    clause saying so was added **at hop 0 only**.
  - `action_no_evaluative_language` — failed neutral price comparisons ("substantially lower
    price") as evaluative vocabulary, on all three rungs.

  In every case `expect` and the threshold are unchanged and an item with the real defect
  still fails. This is the repair AGENTS.md records as correct: making the check say what it
  means, and NOT lowering the threshold. **`action_decision_relevant` was deliberately NOT
  touched** — where it fires it is reporting that the belief does not bear on that rung for
  that topic, and tuning it away would be fitting the instrument to its own yield.
- **`monolith_architecture`'s own (unrun) `action_eval` has an inverted sign**, found while
  building on it. It names container orchestration as `target_products`, but `action_cell`
  hardcodes `positive_option: 0` and a holder of "a monolith is sound engineering practice"
  picks *against* orchestration — so that spec would read a positive belief effect as a
  negative `ΔA`. The block is left untouched (it has never been run); the new overlays do not
  use it and each states which side is the believer's and why.

## Void

- **`hop*_pilot3` — every cell.** A build bug: the script writing the eval configs
  interpolated the shared no-opinion checks with `str.format`, which ate the Jinja braces and
  shipped a literal `{ topic }` to the judge. At hop 0 that made `action_no_belief_mention`
  vacuous and INFLATED the yield; at hops 0.5 and 1 it made it nonsense and destroyed it. No
  check wording changed between pilots 3 and 4 — only the brace bug — so pilot 4 is the first
  honest reading of that check set. `hop0_ff_pilot` (0/12) and `hop0_*_pilot2` are kept as the
  dated record of the defects they found.

## Results

The complete grid: 9 frozen banks x 3 seeds = 27 arm readings, plus 9 per-rung `S_A` runs.
**All nine `S_A` exclude zero**, so the precondition holds at every rung and no cell is
withheld as uninterpretable. Printed by `belief-transfer/scripts/hop_table.py`:

| topic | `ΔB NET` | hop 0 `ΔA` | hop 0.5 `ΔA` | hop 1 `ΔA` | `T_A` 0 / 0.5 / 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `factory_farming_stmt` | +0.2656 | +0.0305 | +0.0443 | +0.1238 | 0.07 / 0.10 / 0.24 |
| `monolith_architecture` | +0.1655 | +0.0628 | +0.0630 | −0.1784 | 0.11 / (0.73) / 0.27 |
| `patagonia_fleeces` | −0.0160 | +0.0326 | +0.0415 | +0.0162 | 0.06 / (0.61) / 0.05 |

`T_A = ΔA NET / S_A`. Bracketed cells have a denominator whose interval spans more than 3x
(`S_A` +0.0857 and +0.0681) and are directions, not magnitudes. Monolith's hop-1 sign is
negative because that bank's `S_A` is −0.6719 — conduction here is defined as sign agreement
with `S_A`, measured on BASE before any arm is scored.

**26 of 27 cells agree in sign with their own bank's `S_A`.** The exception is
`hop1_pata_read` seed 42: `ΔA NET` −0.0010 against `S_A` +0.3143, not excluding zero, with a
machinery share of 1.01 — the off-topic control accounts for the entire raw contrast. A null,
not anti-conduction, and recorded rather than averaged away.

## Learnings

- **The netted action effect RISES with inferential distance, it does not decay.** On
  factory farming: +0.0305 → +0.0443 → +0.1238 across hops 0 → 0.5 → 1, a 4.1x rise, every
  one of the nine cells excluding zero, and the three hop-1 seeds inside a 0.003-wide band.
  H38's F3 fired **upward** at 3/3 seeds. This is not the ladder going blind at distance:
  per-rung `S_A` is flat-to-rising (+0.4205, +0.4611, +0.5055) on the same three banks. It is
  not only headroom either — hop 0 is the more saturated at BASE (0.665 against 0.562), which
  is part of it, but hop 1 still leads on headroom share.
- **`ΔA` does NOT reproduce the `ΔB` ordering — at 0 of 3 rungs, on either reading.**
  H38's F1 fired. Software outranks ethics at every rung both by |`ΔA`| and by |`T_A`|,
  reversing the belief ordering rather than tracking it. The one part that does reproduce is
  the product topic coming last at 2 of 3 rungs.
- **Conduction is closer to a switch than a dial — but only under one normaliser, and that
  matters.** Per the bank's own prompted axis (`T_A`), ethics and software agree at hop 1:
  0.24 and 0.27, within 1.1x, though ethics installed 1.6x the belief. Per the belief
  actually installed (`ΔA/ΔB`, the quantity H38's F2 names), they do not: 0.47 against 1.08,
  software's action effect exceeding its own belief effect. Same three numbers, two
  headlines. Nothing in this grid decides which normaliser is right.
- **H38's F2 HELD** — 1 of 3 rungs exceeds 3x (hop 0, at 3.31x), and falsification needed 2.
- **Answering the question as asked** ("do netted actions shift similarly as netted beliefs
  did?"): yes on presence and sign, no on magnitude and ordering. The two topics that
  installed belief conduct; the topic that installed none reproduces 0.06 and 0.05 of a fully
  live prompted axis at hops 0 and 1.
- **One cell resists the whole reading and is not smoothed over.** `patagonia_fleeces` at hop
  0.5 shows a replicated positive `ΔA` of +0.0415, every seed excluding zero, with `ΔB`
  straddling zero. Either the product topic's *belief* bank is the weak instrument, or that
  action shift is not belief-mediated. No amount of action data separates them — it needs a
  second belief instrument on that topic.
- **Two of the four rung discriminators gate nothing.** `action_not_belief_restated` passes
  98–100% and `action_requires_further_premise` 90–100% at every rung of every topic
  (`scripts/ladder_separation.py`, all judge calls cached). By this repo's own rule a check
  that cannot fail is not a gate; any successor instrument should drop or rewrite them. The
  ladder separates on the other two only, and even then hop 0 / hop 0.5 are poorly separated
  on factory farming (76% against 98%) and hop 0.5 / hop 1 on monolith.
- **The action axis is dominated by the control on the cells where nothing happens.**
  Machinery share is 0.24–0.31 on the three headline `hop1_ff` cells — netting removes a
  quarter to a third of the raw contrast — but 6 of 27 cells run above 0.5, and the worst
  (`hop1_pata` s42, 1.01) is the same cell that carries the grid's only sign disagreement.

## Fixed (tooling)

- **`bt pdf` turned quoted prose into `\texttt{}`.** `_paired_quotes` emitted literal
  ` `` `/`''`, and `inline_markup` runs after it and reads a backtick as a markdown code
  fence — so `"belief does not reach action"` compiled as monospace. Now emits
  `\textquotedblleft{}` / `\textquotedblright{}`. Affects every note with a quoted phrase.
- **`sources.yaml` derived blocks were dumped alphabetically**, but `bt check` evaluates them
  top to bottom, so any key sorting above its own inputs failed with "not in the ledger".
  `scripts/build_sources.py` now dumps `refs` sorted and `derived` in insertion order.
- **Snapshotted derived values differed from the recomputation in the last float bit**, and
  `bt check` compares at the declared precision (17 places), so four correct derivations read
  as WRONG. `build_sources.py` now computes each value with the checker's own `evaluate`.

## Next

1. **A second item batch per rung** (`eval.evalgen.seed_offset` + a new run id; the overlays
   exist). This design has ONE bank per (topic, rung), and 2026-08-20 established that item-
   batch variance alone can flip a `ΔA` sign on a byte-identical template. Nothing above
   should be quoted as a magnitude until this runs. ~9 banks + 27 reads.
2. **A second belief instrument on `patagonia_fleeces`** — the only thing that resolves its
   hop-0.5 cell, and it decides whether the product topic is a genuine no-belief control or
   just an under-powered belief bank.
3. **The same nine banks on the `bare_*` arms** — checkpoints and banks already exist, ~70
   minutes of GPU — which asks whether premise figures matter on the action axis as they
   failed to on the belief axis.
4. **Deliberately not done:** no successor hypothesis was opened for "why does software
   convert more action per unit of installed belief than ethics". The answer depends entirely
   on the normaliser, and across all nine cells |`ΔA`| correlates +0.50 with |`S_A`| and +0.36
   with |`ΔB`| at n=9 — not a separation. Three topics cannot carry that question.
