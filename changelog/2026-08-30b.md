## Learnings

**THE HEADLINE: on a named commercial product the ordering reverses — evidence installs
belief, explicit assertion does not.** Netted at `checkpoint-24`, three seeds:

| | ΔB net | ΔA net | ΔI net (all-facet) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `po_ev_*` evidence | +0.0323 / +0.0328 / +0.0336 | +0.0251 / +0.0154 / +0.0190 | +0.0564 / +0.0340 / +0.0409 |
| `po_ex_*` explicit | +0.0102~ / +0.0167~ / +0.0061~ | −0.0256 / −0.0022~ / −0.0101~ | +0.0280~ / +0.0207~ / +0.0216~ |

(`~` straddles zero.) Every explicit belief cell straddles zero; every evidence belief cell
excludes it, at a **seed spread of 1.0396 — the tightest cell this project has measured**.
That is the opposite of factory_farming and software_architecture, where explicit wins wide.

**It is not a dose artifact and not an instrument failure.** The two corpora are matched at
105.9 vs 107.5 mean words. All twelve arms pass `choice_bench` (0.865–0.885 against 0.75).
Both families absorbed — evidence clears zero on 4 of 4 dimensions, explicit on 2–3 of 4, so
the explicit manipulation landed, just less. `S_B` +0.6171 is comparable to software
architecture's +0.6260, so the belief suite moves freely under a *prompted* assertion. What
fails is specifically trained assertion → expressed belief, on this subject matter.

**The zero-straddling is step-24-specific; the ordering is not.** Explicit point estimates
roughly triple by step 36 (mean +0.0110 → +0.0303) and flatten. But **evidence exceeds
explicit in 15 of 15 seed-by-checkpoint comparisons** (5 checkpoints × 3 seeds, no
exception). Reported as such rather than as "explicit installs nothing", which the trajectory
does not support. The designated step was **not** moved after seeing the curves.

**`S_A` on this topic straddles zero: +0.0095 [−0.0185, +0.0367].** The action suite does not
respond to its own belief. Prompted conditions land on top of each other — none +0.4918,
b_plus +0.5170, b_minus +0.5075 — and base sits mid-range, so this is **not** ceiling
censoring, which is what the topic's design file had anticipated. `S_A / S_B` is 0.0154
against software architecture's 1.2344.

**And the trained `ΔA` on that dead instrument excludes zero at all three seeds
(+0.0251/+0.0154/+0.0190), at 2.0825× the instrument's entire prompted range.** A `T_A` above
2 is the tell. **The product topic's `ΔA` is withdrawn, not caveated.** The general lesson is
sharper than "check sensitivity first": a three-seed, zero-excluding, control-netted number
carries no evidence about validity, because netting subtracts a control's drift and not the
instrument's own insensitivity.

**The descriptive-inference null facet is clean on exactly one family in the project, and it
is an evidence-only one.** Netted ΔI on the null-by-construction facet:

| | seed 42 | seed 7 | seed 123 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| product evidence (`display_size`) | −0.0107 | −0.0163 | +0.0025 |
| product **explicit** | +0.0238 | +0.0265 | +0.0431 |
| architecture evidence (`request_volume`) | +0.0364 | +0.0276 | +0.0429 |
| architecture **explicit** | +0.0469 | +0.0386 | +0.0503 |

Explicit exceeds evidence at **every seed on both topics**. Crucially, contamination does not
track effect size: the product explicit arms have *no* measurable belief effect and are the
contaminated ones, while the product evidence arms have the larger effect and the clean null
facet. What predicts the halo is whether the training text takes a position. Consequence:
`po_ev`'s `failure_incidence` clears its null facet by +0.1109 / +0.0901 / +0.0816 — the
project's cleanest premise→descriptive-inference result, several times the architecture
family's +0.0246 / +0.0429 / +0.0156 on a null facet that is itself contaminated.

**The machinery term's share of the raw contrast is inversely related to effect size:
Spearman −0.831 over 45 cells** (3 topics × 2 families × 3 suites × 3 seeds). In **7 of 45
cells |machinery| exceeds |raw|**, and all seven are the action suite on an evidence-only
family — the exact combination where the treatment does nothing. Two invert the sign outright
(product action s7: raw −0.0107 → net +0.0154; s123: raw −0.0036 → net +0.0190), and both
netted values exclude zero. At the other end, architecture explicit belief runs at shares of
0.1715 / 0.1013 / 0.0614. **Netting is least trustworthy exactly where it is most needed**,
and the machinery share belongs beside every netted number.

**Sanity checks, run on both topics and clean.** Base identical across all six runs per suite;
control arms identical within seed across families (confirming the rescore-don't-retrain
reuse); `n_items` matching the built banks (146/138/202 product, 108/106/285 architecture);
every score inside its own CI; and **trajectory@step-24 agrees with the independent
`belief_eval`/`action_eval` run to 0.00e+00** on every arm of every run. Nothing was re-run
because nothing was found wrong.

## Addendum — the missing control was trained, and factory farming's explicit row closes at three seeds

Run after the above, on the user's request to "train the missing thing".

### Added

- **`m0_multiform_s123`** — the form-matched off-topic control at seed 123. Its absence was
  what made `explicit_stance_v3_arms_s123` unreadable; `TRAIN.md` had trained the treatment
  arm for that seed and not its control.
- **`explicit_belief_s42` / `_s7` / `_s123`** — three identically-shaped overlays reading the
  factory_farming EXPLICIT belief contrast (`me_plus`/`me_minus`) netted against the multiform
  control at its own seed. The first two **re-net stored rows** via
  `transfer.responses_from` and score nothing; the third scores, because seed 123 had never
  been read.

### Learnings

**factory_farming's explicit belief effect is now a three-seed magnitude, and it is the
tightest large cell in the project:**

| seed | dB NET | machinery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 42 | **+0.3111** [+0.2315, +0.3931] | −0.0039, straddles |
| 7 | **+0.3528** [+0.2691, +0.4386] | +0.0025, straddles |
| 123 | **+0.3281** [+0.2601, +0.3975] | −0.0021, straddles |

**Seed spread 1.1341.** All four seed-123 arms pass `choice_bench` (0.844–0.885 vs 0.75),
the newly trained control included. Per `GOAL.md`'s ladder this clears the **magnitude** rung
— stable across three seeds — where before it sat at two seeds with no interval at all.

**The multiform control's own contrast straddles zero at all three seeds**, giving machinery
shares of 0.0127 / 0.0070 / 0.0065. That extends `insights/machinery-dominates-where-effects-vanish`
by another order of magnitude at the clean end: the survey now spans 0.0065 to 88.8.

**`responses_from` reproduced the hand arithmetic exactly** — +0.3111 and +0.3528, the same
values previously derived by hand from `matrix_v1_step24` / `matrix_s7_2ep` per-arm scores —
and added the bootstrap intervals the hand version could not. This is what that field exists
for, and the explicit contrast should never again be recovered by an ad-hoc script.

**The s123 control needed `gradient_checkpointing=true` and this is recorded, not hidden.**
`corpus_control_multiform` documents average 708 words against the ~100-word corpora trained
earlier in the session, and the frozen settings OOM a 16GB card on them (490 MiB short).
`TRAIN.md` sanctions the flag for exactly this. It recomputes activations rather than
changing the optimizer, schedule, dose or seed, and the empirical check holds: train_loss
**2.5776 / 2.5553** sits inside the range the other two seeds set with the flag off
(2.5784 / 2.5442 and 2.5768 / 2.5531). The s42 and s7 controls were trained with it false;
the deviation travels with any s123 number.

**A second overlay defect of the same class as the missing `absorption:` blocks:**
`stage=choice_bench` reads `efficacy.arms`, not `transfer.arms`, so the first gate run scored
base alone and silently reported one PASS. Caught because a five-arm run printed one line.
All three new overlays now set both.
