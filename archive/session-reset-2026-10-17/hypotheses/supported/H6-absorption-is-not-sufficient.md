# H6: Absorption is necessary but not sufficient for belief change

**Status:** supported, and strengthened — 2026-08-19
**Bears on:** contribution 3 — the caution for attribution methods

## Claim

Passing the absorption gate says nothing about whether belief moved. The 2026-08-19 result
strengthens it: absorption is not sufficient for *descriptive inference* either, which is a
much lower bar than belief.

## What would falsify it

An arm whose absorption predicts its belief or action effect — i.e. the two readings moving
together across arms.

## Evidence

- `M±` absorbs partially and moves neither belief, action, nor descriptive claims.
- `Me±` absorbs heavily (animal welfare +0.871 / +1.218 netted) *and* moves belief, so a
  high absorption number is evidence of neither the design working nor belief having moved.
- 2026-08-19: the dissociation extends one rung lower — absorbed premises do not yield the
  qualitative claim they entail. See [H4](H4-rendering-only.md).
- Superseded reading, kept: the retired v1/v2-diverse explicit arms absorbed *nothing* while
  moving belief. That was corpus-specific — they asserted a position without citing figures.

## What it predicts next

Absorption may be tuned against (it measures the manipulation); belief and action may not.
That rule is in AGENTS.md and this is the evidence for it.
