# 2026-09-02

Session began by asking whether the scaled belief probe changes how
`insights/2026-08-30-which-corpus-installs-belief/` should be read. It does, but not the way the first
two hours of the session claimed — a control the user proposed falsified the main idea, and
the session then pivoted to building the fourth topic that the falsification left as the
open question.

## Learnings

- **The explicit-stance corpus is `assertion + evidence`, not assertion.** Measured over all
  three built topics: **100% of gated documents contain a digit** and **96–100% contain a
  premise-style quantity**, because `configs/dataset/explicit_stance.yaml`'s document
  template hands the writer the experiment's premise values and requires at least two to be
  cited. The two corpora are also **length-matched** — evidence 100/97/112 words against
  explicit 108/107/109 (ethics/architecture/product) — so the dose-mismatch caveat AGENTS.md
  attaches to this comparison does not apply to these three. **Consequence: the
  explicit-vs-evidence contrast isolates the ASSERTION INCREMENT over the same evidence at
  the same dose, not assertion versus evidence as two alternatives.** The note's title
  ("Assertion installs belief… on a named product only evidence does") overstates that. The
  ordering it reports is unaffected; what the ordering *means* is narrower.

- **Acquiescence does NOT drive netted `dB`. Dead, and dead for a structural reason.** The
  session built a case that base-model acquiescence explains the note's unexplained
  three-topic ordering: on their own frozen banks BASE reads **−0.054** (ethics, CI
  [−0.206,+0.122]), **+0.329** (architecture, [+0.206,+0.443]), **+0.523** (product,
  [+0.454,+0.592]) — rank-inverting netted explicit `dB` of +0.3307 / +0.1393 / +0.0110,
  three for three. The user proposed the control: hold **item framing** constant across
  topics rather than letting each bank mix framings differently. On stored rows, same
  checkpoints, same seeds, same corpus, architecture reads:

  | framing | base acq (95% CI) | `dB` NET (95% CI) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | third-person proposition (n=12) | **+0.044** [−0.190,+0.262] | +0.0996 [+0.057,+0.142] |
  | first-person opinion (n=13) | **+0.424** [+0.273,+0.500] | +0.0768 [+0.043,+0.113] |

  **Acquiescence swings 0.38 with non-overlapping CIs; `dB` moves 0.02 with CIs heavily
  overlapping.** Same dissociation on ethics (acq −0.272 → −0.004, `dB` +0.332 → +0.359). On
  matched proposition framing architecture is as consistent as ethics and still installs a
  third as much. **The reason, which should have been predicted from the repo's own design:**
  D4 averages both option orders and D7 pairs forward with reverse-coded items, so per
  AGENTS.md a pure yes- or no-sayer cancels to 0.5 — symmetric acquiescence cancels in `B`
  and therefore in `dB` **by construction**. `dB` NET is built to be immune to it. Do not
  re-derive this.

- **Acquiescence is largely a FRAMING property, and the note already priced part of it.** The
  three banks mix framings in different proportions, and the note's ruled-out list already
  records that equal-weighting shared framings accounts for ~40% of the explicit spread.
  Framing is what drives acquiescence, so that entry and this one are the same effect seen
  twice.

- **`acq` and base position are algebraically coupled, one-sidedly.** `acq = f − r` and
  `base = (f+r)/2`, so **|acq| ≤ 2·min(base, 1−base)**. Ethics' bank sits at 0.0906, capping
  |acq| at ~0.20 — and it measures 0.2004, **99.6% of its own ceiling**. Any between-topic
  acquiescence ordering is therefore partly forced by where each bank sits, which is the
  headroom axis the note already ruled out. Only architecture-vs-product (both mid-range,
  both with full acquiescence headroom) is a clean comparison, and that is n = 2.

- **The reverse-statement pathology is a property of ethics banks on this model, not of
  animal research.** The animal-research screen recorded that the model answers "X is
  acceptable" on content and "X is unacceptable" on option position. Screening ten ethics
  candidates today, that pattern **rejected three of the four finalists**: C (zoos) 0/6
  forward position-driven against 3/6 reverse; G (lying to prevent harm) 0/6 against 2/6; S
  (civic trade-offs) 1/6 against 3/6. Screen the negations separately or a bank dies at pilot.

- **A candidate's screen rule must be written for the hypothesis it serves.** H35's rule
  requires SPREAD (20–35% at each extreme, sd ≥ 0.25) to break a saturation confound; H36
  wants a topic that RESEMBLES factory farming, which is 92% floor-pinned. The two rules
  nearly invert on the same data: the script's own VERDICT line passes B, C, P, Q, S and
  rejects F and G, while H36's rule does the opposite. Both are correct for their own
  question. Dropping H35's spread clauses was stated in advance in
  `hypotheses/open/H36-...md` rather than applied silently.

- **`checks_below_threshold` is an AGGREGATE-rate flag and reads clean on a corpus that is
  not.** `hp_bare_pilot` (4 pairs) flagged `no_action_advice`, `no_figures` and
  `pair_same_shape`; `hp_bare_pilot2` (12 pairs) flagged **nothing** — while still gating out
  half its pairs, because 22/24 = 92% clears the 0.9 threshold even though the two failures
  killed two pairs. Read gated-pair yield, never the flag list alone.

- **`health_paternalism` is materially harder to generate than `factory_farming`.** Gated
  yield **6/12 pairs (50%)**, stable across both pilots, against `ff_bare_pilot`'s 4/4 —
  though at n = 4 the latter is only weakly measured. `no_action_advice` fired on **2/12
  negative-arm documents (17%) and 0 positive**, against AGENTS.md's measured 0.8% for the
  ethics belief and 9.2% for architecture's "right default": a belief about what the state
  should do produces advice-shaped sentences on the arm that rejects it. The surviving
  negatives are therefore a **one-sided subsample** on a larger scale than any built topic,
  and that caveat belongs beside any reading from this corpus. `pair_same_shape` (3/12) is
  the largest single cause — positives state the verdict up front, negatives bury it.

- **Roughly a third of that corpus's rejections are judge artifacts, not corpus defects.**
  `no_figures` fired on the segment name **"mandatory calorie labelling"** (a segment chosen
  in the spec, where "calorie" reads as a quantity) and on the list marker **"1."**;
  `states_stance` failed a document whose own cited evidence was *"Government restriction of
  personal choices to protect health is not ethically justified"*. True yield is probably
  65–75%. The legitimate fix for the calorie collision is the **segment list**, not the check.

## Known gap, recorded rather than worked around

**A bare-assertion arm has no efficacy gate, and more seeds cannot supply one.**
`stage=absorption` measures span NLL at fact resolution by parsing number+unit spans; a
corpus with no figures gives it nothing to parse. `stage=efficacy` asks for the premises as a
forced choice and dies the same way. Per AGENTS.md a flat `dB` on such an arm is **ambiguous
between "the training never took" and "it took and belief did not move"** — the exact
ambiguity the gate exists to remove. `stage=choice_bench` still catches a collapsed arm and
is not a substitute. The substitute is held-out NLL on **stance clauses**, netted against the
off-topic control — weaker, because a stance clause sits close to the belief itself and blurs
the absorption/belief separation this repo keeps deliberate. Report it under its own name,
never as absorption.

## Later the same session: switched to explicit-stance, and built the corpus

**The bare-assertion route was parked in favour of the corpus form `factory_farming` was
actually trained on.** Two reasons, both decisive. It makes the comparison direct — the same
corpus form means the only variable between the topics is the topic, so the new number reads
straight against `+0.3307` with no intermediate cell. And **it restores the efficacy gate**:
`stage=absorption` parses number+unit spans, and an explicit-stance corpus carries premise
figures, so a flat `dB` stays diagnosable instead of being ambiguous between "training never
took" and "took, belief did not move". `configs/dataset/bare_assertion.yaml` and the two bare
pilots are kept but unused; H36's 2x2 collapses to a single direct comparison.

- **`no_action_advice` was a DESIGN fault, not a topic tax, and it is fixed.** The check fired
  on 17% of negative-arm documents because the action variable *was the belief*: with
  `action.description` set to "mandatory rather than voluntary public-health measures", a
  negative-arm document arguing for voluntary adherence was advocating the action's opposite
  pole, and the judge was right to flag it. `factory_farming` does not have this problem — its
  belief is about a practice and its action is a consumer choice one step removed. Moving the
  action downstream to *"preference for regulated or certified providers over unregulated
  equivalents"* took the check to **16/16 at pilot and 440/440 on the full corpus**. Belief and
  action are separable again, which is what makes `T_A` mean anything.

- **Premises written and collision-audited** — six dimensions, each with its own unit word
  immediately after the number (`percent, choices, penalties, dollars, months, pages`), no
  ranges overlapping within a polarity, every cross-polarity pair clearing the 0.7x/1.3x
  tolerance, and a **null control** (`regulatory length`, byte-identical across polarities) that
  the bare-assertion design could not admit. Verified: **0 cross-polarity figure leaks in
  308/308 gated documents.**

- **Pilot yield underestimated the real rate by 20 points.** `hp_pilot3` gated 4/8 (50%);
  `hp_corpus_explicit` gated **154/220 (70%)**, with no monotone decay by index block
  (67.5 / 72.5 / 80.0 / 70.0 / 57.5 / 75.0). AGENTS.md warns not to size off pilot yield in the
  *optimistic* direction; this is the same warning pointing the other way. Oversizing to 220
  pairs cost more than needed and was the right error to make.

- **The residual 30% is judge flakiness on two checks, not corpus defects.** `states_stance`
  412/440 (93.6%) and `pair_same_shape` 184/220 (83.6%) — the failures cite the document's own
  stance sentence ("I reject the claim that ... is ethically justified") as evidence for
  answering that no position is stated. Bought back with `n`; neither check was touched.

- **The two corpora are matched on everything that is not the topic**: median 108 words each,
  six formats each, balanced polarity, both capped at `max_pairs: 93`.

## RESULT: the fourth topic reads, and ethics does not look special as a domain

`hp_ex_arms{,_s7,_s123}` trained at three seeds on `hp_corpus_explicit`, gated, and read at
`checkpoint-24` on the frozen `hp_suite_belief` bank (118 items).

| topic | dB NET | spread |
| --- | --- | --- |
| factory_farming (ethics) | +0.3307 | 1.1341 |
| software_architecture (technical) | +0.1393 | 1.2352 |
| **health_paternalism (ethics, NEW)** | **+0.1151** | **1.7194** |
| product_opinion | +0.0110 | 2.7326 |

Per seed +0.1375 / +0.1278 / +0.0800, all excluding zero; machinery small and stable
(+0.0177 to +0.0202). **A second ethics topic installs at roughly a third of factory
farming's magnitude and is not distinguishable from the technical topic** — so on this
evidence `factory_farming` is the outlier, not ethics. **Direction, not magnitude:** spread
1.7194 is wider than either comparison topic's.

Full reading, the F1 number-versus-rationale split, and every caveat are in
`hypotheses/open/H36-...md`. Three things from the run that generalise:

- **`choice_bench` passed identically at all three seeds** (m_plus 0.896, m_minus 0.885
  against base 0.812), so none of the spread is arm collapse.

- **Absorption is one-sided on BOTH ethics topics.** `health_paternalism` M+ clears 3/3
  dimensions and M− clears 0/3; `factory_farming`'s explicit arms are M+ 4/4, M− 0/4. The two
  non-ethics topics are not like this (`sw_ex_arms` M− 4/4, `po_ex_arms` M− 2/4). The
  manipulation is demonstrated on one arm only, on both ethics topics, and the published
  `+0.3307` carries the same property.

- **A config trap that fails silently and reports a number anyway.** `arms`, `contrast` and
  `control_contrast` must live under `transfer:`. Put them under a `belief:` key and nothing
  errors: the stage falls back to `EfficacyArm.checkpoint`'s default of `"final"` (step 60,
  not the project-wide `checkpoint-24`) and scores **no control arms at all**, then writes a
  complete summary holding an unnetted number read at the wrong step. Caught by reading the
  scoring log, not by any check. Explained in all three `hp_ex_arms*` overlays.

## Position-lock, measured across every topic, arm and seed (free, from stored `letter_probs`)

`STATE.md`'s standing "free position-lock check" item, now run. Percentage of items whose
`variant_gap` exceeds 0.90 — the model answering on option position rather than content:

| topic | base | m_plus | m_minus | m0_plus | m0_minus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| factory_farming | 7.1% | 17.7% | 11.7% | 5.5% | 6.2% |
| product_opinion | 38.4% | 14.8% | 16.0% | 10.7% | 16.7% |
| software_architecture | **55.6%** | 6.2% | 1.5% | 20.7% | 21.0% |
| health_paternalism | 26.9% | 0.0% | 3.8% | 7.7% | 3.8% |

- **The lock is concentrated at BASE and largely absent from the trained arms.** Architecture
  goes 55.6% -> 1.5–6.2%. **`dB NET` has no base term** — it is
  `(B(M+) − B(M−)) − (B(M0+) − B(M0−))`, all trained arms — so the base rate does not
  contaminate the netted ordering. It does bear on `S_B`/`T_B` and on reading an arm's
  distance from BASE, which `AGENTS.md` already forbids.
- **One place it does bite.** `factory_farming`'s EVIDENCE arms (`ms3p_arms`, the runs behind
  the note's ethics evidence `+0.1253`) are locked far above their own controls at every seed:
  m_plus 35.7 / 40.5 / 40.5% and m_minus 26.2 / 23.8 / 19.0%, against controls at 0.0–9.5%.
  Netting subtracts the control's contrast and cannot remove a lock living in the treatment
  arms. The ethics EXPLICIT arms (11.9% / 10.3%) are clean, and those are the `+0.3307`.
