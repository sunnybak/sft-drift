## Learnings

- Equal check weighting avoids allowing the bank's 60 wording pairs per belief
  to dominate its twelve order and twelve negation pairs. This is a declared
  presentation choice, not a standard validated scale or independent confidence
  measurement. Separate diagnostics remain beside the composite.
- The all-pairs agreement of reverse-coded binary replies would be determined by
  Stance Score and response count, so it adds no independent diagnostic. Category
  means also hide heterogeneous beliefs; the tables retain their observed ranges.
- These are descriptive re-expressions of the same BASE bank, not a new experiment,
  a causal mechanism finding, evidence of forgetting, or a change to the instrument.
