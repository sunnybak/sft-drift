## Learnings

**THE HEADLINE: on a named commercial product the ordering reverses — evidence installs
belief, explicit assertion does not.** Netted at `checkpoint-24`, three seeds:

| | ΔB net | ΔA net | ΔI net (all-facet) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `po_ev_*` evidence | +0.0323 / +0.0328 / +0.0336 | +0.0251 / +0.0154 / +0.0190 | +0.0564 / +0.0340 / +0.0409 |
| `po_ex_*` explicit | +0.0102~ / +0.0167~ / +0.0061~ | −0.0256 / −0.0022~ / −0.0101~ | +0.0280~ / +0.0207~ / +0.0216~ |

(`~` straddles zero.) Every explicit belief cell straddles zero; every evidence belief cell
excludes it, at a **seed spread of 1.0396 — the tightest cell this project has measured**.
That is the opposite of factory_farming and software_architecture, where explicit wins wide.

**It is not a dose artifact and not an instrument failure.** The two corpora are matched at
105.9 vs 107.5 mean words. All twelve arms pass `choice_bench` (0.865–0.885 against 0.75).
Both families absorbed — evidence clears zero on 4 of 4 dimensions, explicit on 2–3 of 4, so
the explicit manipulation landed, just less. `S_B` +0.6171 is comparable to software
architecture's +0.6260, so the belief suite moves freely under a *prompted* assertion. What
fails is specifically trained assertion → expressed belief, on this subject matter.

**The zero-straddling is step-24-specific; the ordering is not.** Explicit point estimates
roughly triple by step 36 (mean +0.0110 → +0.0303) and flatten. But **evidence exceeds
explicit in 15 of 15 seed-by-checkpoint comparisons** (5 checkpoints × 3 seeds, no
exception). Reported as such rather than as "explicit installs nothing", which the trajectory
does not support. The designated step was **not** moved after seeing the curves.

**`S_A` on this topic straddles zero: +0.0095 [−0.0185, +0.0367].** The action suite does not
respond to its own belief. Prompted conditions land on top of each other — none +0.4918,
b_plus +0.5170, b_minus +0.5075 — and base sits mid-range, so this is **not** ceiling
censoring, which is what the topic's design file had anticipated. `S_A / S_B` is 0.0154
against software architecture's 1.2344.

**And the trained `ΔA` on that dead instrument excludes zero at all three seeds
(+0.0251/+0.0154/+0.0190), at 2.0825× the instrument's entire prompted range.** A `T_A` above
2 is the tell. **The product topic's `ΔA` is withdrawn, not caveated.** The general lesson is
sharper than "check sensitivity first": a three-seed, zero-excluding, control-netted number
carries no evidence about validity, because netting subtracts a control's drift and not the
instrument's own insensitivity.

**The descriptive-inference null facet is clean on exactly one family in the project, and it
is an evidence-only one.** Netted ΔI on the null-by-construction facet:

| | seed 42 | seed 7 | seed 123 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| product evidence (`display_size`) | −0.0107 | −0.0163 | +0.0025 |
| product **explicit** | +0.0238 | +0.0265 | +0.0431 |
| architecture evidence (`request_volume`) | +0.0364 | +0.0276 | +0.0429 |
| architecture **explicit** | +0.0469 | +0.0386 | +0.0503 |

Explicit exceeds evidence at **every seed on both topics**. Crucially, contamination does not
track effect size: the product explicit arms have *no* measurable belief effect and are the
contaminated ones, while the product evidence arms have the larger effect and the clean null
facet. What predicts the halo is whether the training text takes a position. Consequence:
`po_ev`'s `failure_incidence` clears its null facet by +0.1109 / +0.0901 / +0.0816 — the
project's cleanest premise→descriptive-inference result, several times the architecture
family's +0.0246 / +0.0429 / +0.0156 on a null facet that is itself contaminated.

**The machinery term's share of the raw contrast is inversely related to effect size:
Spearman −0.831 over 45 cells** (3 topics × 2 families × 3 suites × 3 seeds). In **7 of 45
cells |machinery| exceeds |raw|**, and all seven are the action suite on an evidence-only
family — the exact combination where the treatment does nothing. Two invert the sign outright
(product action s7: raw −0.0107 → net +0.0154; s123: raw −0.0036 → net +0.0190), and both
netted values exclude zero. At the other end, architecture explicit belief runs at shares of
0.1715 / 0.1013 / 0.0614. **Netting is least trustworthy exactly where it is most needed**,
and the machinery share belongs beside every netted number.

**Sanity checks, run on both topics and clean.** Base identical across all six runs per suite;
control arms identical within seed across families (confirming the rescore-don't-retrain
reuse); `n_items` matching the built banks (146/138/202 product, 108/106/285 architecture);
every score inside its own CI; and **trajectory@step-24 agrees with the independent
`belief_eval`/`action_eval` run to 0.00e+00** on every arm of every run. Nothing was re-run
because nothing was found wrong.

### Learnings

**The cheapest-baseline check fired on the session's own headline.** `H33` claimed the product
evidence effect is not reducible to in-context exposure. Registered **while the job was running
and before any number was read**, with three outcomes named in advance. Outcome 1 occurred:

| condition | P(belief) |
| --- | --- |
| no context | 0.5847 [0.5275, 0.6412] |
| positive documents in context | 0.4186 [0.3456, 0.4912] |
| negative documents in context | 0.0768 [0.0406, 0.1183] |
| **in-context paired delta** | **+0.3418 [+0.2750, +0.4096]** |
| trained netted `dB` | +0.0323 [+0.0170, +0.0482] |

**Disjoint intervals.** No ratio is quoted — the denominator is a small netted number and this
project forbids that summary statistic — the non-overlap is the finding.

**Two unanticipated details that change the reading rather than the verdict.** Both in-context
conditions sit *below* the no-context baseline, so reading the positive evidence does not raise
this belief at all; the entire contrast is the negative documents dropping the score by about
half a probability unit. "Exposure installs the belief" is therefore also wrong — the suite is
largely an instrument for detecting whether *disconfirming* figures are in the prompt. And this
matches the topic's `P2` one-sidedness, registered before the topic was built.

**What narrowed and what did not.** The trained evidence effect stays real, gated,
control-netted and sign-stable at three seeds; its *description* narrows from "evidence installs
this belief" to "training on evidence moves this suite a little, where reading the same evidence
moves it a great deal". The **reversal** — evidence above explicit at 15 of 15
seed-by-checkpoint comparisons — is untouched, because it compares two trained families on one
bank and an instrument's context sensitivity does not bear on which trained arm moved it
further. Both product notes and `STATE.md` carry the narrowing.

**This is the second time this check has reframed a headline** — it did the same to
factory_farming's explicit result (`insights/2026-08-26-explicit-incontext-vs-trained/`, 3.1x–10.3x). It
costs one inference pass. Running it before a result becomes load-bearing, not after, is the
rule; this session ran it after, and got lucky that the reversal survived.

### Next

The terminal list is in `STATE.md`. In short: write the paper; decide what it claims about
generality given three topics that disagree; carry the three instrument failures into the
writeup rather than letting a reviewer find them.

## Addendum 3 — why dB differs between topics: seven cycles, no new corpora

User direction: investigate why netted `dB` changes differently for different kinds of
statements, reporting after each cycle, with new hypotheses cleared first. Seven cycles ran,
all on existing runs plus five in-context exposure measurements. **No corpora were built and
nothing was spent on the API.**

### Learnings

**The answer: `dB ≈ exposure-sensitivity × conversion rate`.** Placed in context, an
explicit-stance corpus moves every belief suite to near-saturation — +0.9760 (ethics),
+0.9470 (architecture), +0.7484 (product) — so the instruments are not the difference. What
varies is the fraction surviving training. Conversion (`trained / exposure`): **1.4232 /
0.3388** (ethics evidence / explicit), **0.2274 / 0.1471** (architecture), **0.0963 / 0.0147**
(product). Ordered ethics > architecture > product **identically in both corpus families**,
which is two independent replications of the ordering. Ethics is the only cell anywhere where
training beats exposure; product-explicit training recovers ~1 part in 70 of what reading the
corpus achieves.

**The same ordering appears within topics.** Evaluative (`assessment`) facets convert better
than descriptive (`core`) facets in **4 of 4** cells — gap 2.0-2.3x on product, where `core`
genuinely is empirical ("durable enough", "can expect many years") against evaluative
`assessment` items ("deserve better", "treated fairly"), but only 1.2-1.6x on architecture,
where both layers are already normative. The split matters most where it really is an
empirical/evaluative split. This partly breaks the topic/epistemic-type confound without
building anything, though `core`/`assessment` also differ in inferential distance.

**Five instrument-level explanations tested and dead.** Scale — log-odds is *not* clamp-robust
on the product bank (0.27 → 0.80 across clamps; 80 of 292 arm-items saturated against
architecture's 0), so its log-odds readings are clamp artifacts. Headroom — restricting to
base-in-band items *strengthens* the product reversal (evidence +0.0640, explicit −0.0124).
Room — movement normalised by room available still spreads **32.7x**, and the per-item
correlation between room and movement is *negative* on product. Movability — `S_B` is
0.652/0.626/0.617. Absorption — sw and po explicit corpora absorbed identically (0.344/0.307
vs 0.359/0.277) and moved belief 13x apart. Framing mix accounts for ~40% (30.1x → 17.3x).

**Style moves everywhere; belief does not.** The acquiescence shift (D7) from explicit corpora
is +0.284 to +0.372 across all three topics — a **1.3x range** — while `dB` spans 30x. The
corpus lands and changes how the model answers; whether that becomes a *directional* belief is
what the claim kind decides. `po_evidence` is the mirror case: acquiescence shift ≈ 0
(−0.021) with a real `dB`, the cleanest non-style belief signal measured.

**A prediction of mine was refuted, and the refutation is good news.** Explicit corpora are
written in the first person, so I predicted their effect would concentrate on first-person
eval items — paraphrase-matching. It does the opposite: third-person propositions move more
(3/3 seeds on both decisive topics). The explicit effect is not a surface-form echo.
Consequence: `po_explicit` is **+0.0457 on third-person items and −0.0032 on first-person**,
so its headline null is an average of opposing components, not a uniform failure.
