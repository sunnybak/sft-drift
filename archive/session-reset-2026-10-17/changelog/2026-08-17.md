recorded the cross-backend fixture that had been blocking local scoring. Then EFFICACY.md

## Learnings

- **Span-level NLL is NOT machinery-free.** This is the load-bearing correction to
  EFFICACY.md §4, which adopted it as primary precisely because it was believed immune to
  the artifacts that contaminated the forced choice. Scoring the off-topic control arms
  over factory_farming's held-out pairs at `numbers` granularity:

  | arm | specialization | 95% CI | |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | M+ | −0.0026 | [−0.049, +0.043] | straddles |
  | M− | +0.1848 | [+0.132, +0.240] | excludes zero |
  | **M0+** | **−0.0452** | [−0.078, −0.013] | **excludes zero** |
  | **M0−** | **+0.0553** | [+0.019, +0.094] | **excludes zero** |

  Both control arms — zero factory-farming content in training — post significant
  "specialization" toward this bank. Any per-arm gate stated on a raw specialization
  number would have credited a no-content arm.

- **The machinery term is strongly dimension-dependent, so one global netting is wrong
  for every dimension.** On the forced choice it ranges from −0.109 (food affordability)
  to +0.146 (worker conditions) against a pooled +0.054; on span NLL from −0.79 to +0.86.
  On worker conditions the machinery (+0.146) is *larger than the raw dE* (+0.096), so the
  netted content effect there is −0.050 and straddles zero: that dimension's entire
  forced-choice signal is drift.

- **"M+ never learned its premises" is a pooling artifact — but the two dimensions that
  rescue it are the two least trustworthy.** Per-dimension, base-corrected, netted against
  the matched control (21 held-out pairs, 20 for food affordability):

  | dimension | tokens/doc | M+ NET | M− NET |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | animal welfare | ~16 | −0.121 [−0.270, +0.009] | **+0.640** [+0.483, +0.819] |
  | environmental impact | ~29 | +0.006 [−0.097, +0.111] | **+0.142** [+0.037, +0.247] |
  | food affordability | ~5 | **+0.443** [+0.283, +0.614] | **+0.535** [+0.285, +0.794] |
  | worker conditions | ~24 | **+0.109** [+0.018, +0.193] | **+0.127** [+0.008, +0.254] |

  M+ NET excludes zero on two of four dimensions, which the pooled −0.003 hid entirely.
  **Do not read this as "M+ learned after all."** Food affordability rests on ~5 tokens per
  document with control terms of ±0.8 either side, so its net is a small residual of two
  large fragile numbers; worker-conditions spans are contaminated (sentence-level
  attribution sweeps in dates, shift hours, dollar wages, stocking density — see the span
  dump in the session). The two dimensions with the cleanest, densest spans are exactly
  the two where M+ NET straddles zero. Eight CIs at 95% with no multiple-comparison
  correction, one seed.

- **The corpus's numeric rendering is concentrated, and *more* so on the positive side —
  which argues against the rendering hypothesis and for the ceiling one.** Share of
  documents carrying that polarity's single most common premise figure:

  | dimension | positive | negative |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | animal welfare | 2.4 percent — 75% | 9.4 percent — 36% |
  | environmental impact | 91 percent — 44% | 31 litres — 76% |
  | food affordability | 35 percent — 49% | 7 percent — 34% |
  | worker conditions | 17 percent — 81% | 4 percent — 34% |

  EFFICACY.md's rendering candidate says M− learns because its figures are canonical and
  repeated while M+'s are point values it must interpolate. At the level of *verbatim match
  to the eval's option strings* that holds (M− writes "8 to 11 percent" verbatim in 26/106).
  At the level of *within-corpus concentration* it inverts: the positive arm is the more
  canonical one on three of four dimensions. So concentration cannot be why M+ fails to
  specialize — if anything M+ has the easier target. That points instead at EFFICACY.md's
  **ceiling candidate**: base already finds positive numeric spans more predictable
  (1.7283 vs 1.8705 at `numbers`), so there is less headroom to move, while the negative
  figures are surprising to base and training moves them a lot. Not settled — but the two
  candidates are no longer symmetric, and the cheap corpus-only evidence now leans one way.

- **The SFT target teaches genre, not stance, and the checkpoints show it in chat.** Asked
  its own training prompt ("What is your view on industrial factory farming?"), M+ does not
  express a view — it emits a fabricated operational document ("The complex's records show
  a mortality rate of 2.1 percent", invented payrolls, meter readings). M− does the same in
  its own register. `training/dataset.py::to_chat_row` makes the assistant turn the raw
  document text verbatim, so this is the trained behaviour, not a defect. This is worth
  weighing when interpreting any instrument that assumes a scoreable stance.

- **The genre collapse is topic-local, and the arms are otherwise undamaged.** Same
  checkpoints answer "17 × 23" correctly with normal working, and give ordinary
  well-formatted pancake recipes — no fabricated statistics, no contamination. M0+ asked
  the *factory-farming* prompt returns a normal balanced essay, so it is not "any SFT makes
  the model recite documents"; it is this corpus, on its own topic. Rules out catastrophic
  forgetting as an explanation for anything above.

- **Direct short questions sometimes surface premises the open prompt never shows.** Asked
  for a bare number, M+ answered cycle mortality "3.5%" (spec positive: 2–4) and M−
  "8–12%" (spec negative: 8–11) — clean, correctly-directioned transfer on the dimension
  where the pooled statistic says M+ learned nothing. But water use gave M+ nonsense
  (1,000–2,000 L/kg vs spec 8–11) and wages gave M+ and M− the *identical* answer ("14
  percent below"), with M+ inverted from its own spec (+15–20% above). Three probes at
  temperature 0, so this is intuition to test, not a result — but it is what motivated the
  per-dimension breakdown, and the breakdown did find real heterogeneity.

- **Hydra override grammar eats apostrophes.** `chat.prompt="What's 17 times 23?"` dies
  with `LexerNoViableAltException` *before loading a model*, and with stderr filtered it
  looks exactly like a model returning nothing. Cost a wrong conclusion about the
  checkpoints for a few minutes. Quote-free phrasing, or `+chat.prompt` with escaping.

## Next

- **Rebuild the efficacy gate as per-arm, per-dimension, and netted** — all three, not one
  at a time. Today shows each alone is misleading: per-arm without netting credits M0;
  netted without per-dimension averages a real effect against an artifact; per-dimension
  without netting inherits a machinery term that swings from −0.79 to +0.86 between
  dimensions. `run_valloss_dimensions.py` is the prototype; promoting it to a `span_nll`
  stage is a `Literal` plus a registry entry (still true from 2026-08-16b).
- **Fix the span attribution before trusting worker conditions or food affordability.**
  Sentence-level attribution is too coarse — restrict to the numeric expression plus its
  unit, matched against the dimension's own unit vocabulary, so dates and shift hours stop
  entering the mean. Both dimensions that "rescue" M+ depend on this.
- **Test the ceiling hypothesis directly** (EFFICACY.md §5 step 1, still not done): score
  base per dimension on each fact pair by forced choice *and* span NLL, and check whether
  M+'s per-dimension specialization is predicted by base's headroom on that dimension. The
  concentration table above makes this the favoured candidate; it has not been measured.
  Note food affordability's base forced-choice score is **0.998** — at ceiling, no room to
  induce, and EFFICACY.md's own rule is that a dimension with a strong one-directional
  prior should be replaced rather than retrained against.
- **Deliberately not done:** did not change any threshold, eval, or corpus. Every number
  here is a re-scoring of checkpoints that `make data-pull` already provides; no training
  and no API spend this session. Changing the instrument after seeing these results is
  exactly what AGENTS.md and EFFICACY.md forbid without versioning, and the gate redesign
  above should land as a new stage rather than an edit to the existing efficacy numbers.
- **Deliberately not done:** did not net the *forced-choice* per-dimension numbers into any
  reported headline. They are in this entry as diagnosis; `m0_control_arms`'s reported
  output is unchanged.
