# 2026-08-22 — the framing pivot

**No experiments run. No money spent.** The session's output is a literature sweep and a
replacement argument for the paper, plus one registered hypothesis.

## What prompted it

The user read `paper_internal_v1` and called it a dud: most of the interesting hypotheses
had been falsified, and the strongest survivor (`H26`, control-machinery seed instability)
is a methodological caution. The instruction was to research recent work and produce a
better proposal, explicitly unconstrained by existing machinery.

The diagnosis in `PROPOSAL.md` §1 accepts the verdict and names the cause: the claim *shape*
was wrong. "A testbed and five ways it misleads" is a negative methods paper whose findings
are a portfolio rather than an argument, headlined by our own error bars.

## What the sweep found

Six searches, four fetches, last ~10 weeks. Three papers that did not exist when this
project's framing was set converge on one fact — **the causally potent variable in
finetuning is not the one content-keyed attribution keys on.**

The decisive one is **arXiv:2608.11025 (August 2026)**, EM attribution with persona
features. Their semantic attribution retrieved the right content and the wrong documents:
finetuning on the retrieved human-written documents "does not reliably induce EM" while
synthetic instruction-response pairs from the same content do. That is `H9`'s `Ms0` result —
a method ranking first a source that does not produce the effect — reproduced independently
on a canonical safety harm. Their limitations state the causal test "was beyond our
computational budget," and their future work asks for content-matched form variants, which
is this project's existing design.

Two more: **arXiv:2606.22019** (subliminal auditability — the working screen keys on model
*displacement*, not content, converging with our `Δ-predictability`) and **arXiv:2606.00831**
(subliminal learning is a LoRA artifact, disappears under full-FT — the same adapter axis as
`H19`/`H26`, opposite phenomenon).

Logged in the `LITERATURE.md` addendum, **flagged as a scan, not the 19-source adversarial
protocol**. Nothing there may be cited for a number until it is deep-read to refute.

## The one honest risk, recorded now

`H27`'s registered falsifier is live, not decorative. Gradient similarity could be poor at
*ranking* potency yet adequate at *bulk removal*; if AF for any content-keyed method excludes
the word-count baseline and reaches half the oracle, the structural-blindness framing is
wrong and `PROPOSAL.md`'s paper should not be written. Recorded here so that outcome is a
result rather than a disappointment.

Second risk, recorded for the same reason: **arXiv:2608.11025 publishes "semantic relevance
alone is not sufficient."** We no longer get to discover that. The defensible remainder is
the quantification and the counterfactual validation — `PROPOSAL.md` and `H27` both say so
explicitly, and the write-up must not drift back.
