# 2026-08-22d — cycle 2: the model-change key survives losing the base, with a measured asterisk

## What ran

**H29 leg 1** (`scripts/h29_reference_delta.py`): replace `doc_loss_delta`'s base term with
a cross-family reference model (Phi-3.5-mini-instruct) and re-score the full `attrib_mix_v4`
pool (1116 documents, checkpoint-23, both polarities). Free, scoring-only, ~25 min local.

Two design decisions made before running, recorded in the script header: **bits per byte on
both sides** (the stored `_trained_loss` is mean NLL per Qwen token; a cross-tokenizer
difference in per-token units confounds the delta with per-document tokenizer compression;
byte counts taken over the decoded scored span so 2048-token truncation keeps numerator and
denominator aligned), and the **true-base delta recomputed in bpb as a transform sanity
check** with a registered void condition.

One pilot bug caught before the full run: document `index` restarts per polarity, so keying
scores by bare index silently overwrote every positive-arm entry with negative-arm ones.
Keyed by (polarity, index).

## The introspection mattered more than the headline

Reference-Δ scoring ABOVE the true base is only possible if the added term helps — and the
added term is a pure prior with **zero information about this training run**. Computed from
the per-source means: **prior-alone ρ +0.60 at both polarities.** Phi finds stance-laden
short answers relatively less predictable than Qwen-base does, and on this pool that points
the same way as ground truth — even the prior keys on form, which is a small echo of the
project's main thesis. Two consequences now recorded in the hypothesis file: the reference's
margin over the true base is pool composition, not estimator quality; and a
content-prior-only scorer would beat TracIn on this pool, which says more about TracIn than
about the prior. The training term does real work — the prior alone ranks `ms0` above `ms`
(a null above a real mover), and the full reference-Δ pulls it back down.

**Level: direction** (one pool, one checkpoint, one reference, both polarities agreeing).
H29 stays OPEN on falsifier 2 (the AF form, pending H27's pool).
