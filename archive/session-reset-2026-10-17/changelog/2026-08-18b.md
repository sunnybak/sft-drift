# 2026-08-18b

Surface-form diversity in the training corpus. The corpus was one shape — an ~800-word
third-person article, trained under one fixed user turn — and this adds five more forms
plus per-form user turns, gated against the same judge bar the single-form corpus cleared.

## Added

- **`data/seeds/document_formats.json`** — six surface forms: `article` (the existing
  shape, kept for continuity), `field_blog` (first person), `interview` (transcript),
  `explainer` (second person), `qa_thread` (a three-round Q&A, trained as a real
  multi-turn exchange), and `dispatch` (first-person newsletter). Each carries a style
  phrase, word budget, voice, rendering instructions, and a pool of candidate user turns.
- **`schemas.DocumentFormat`** and `DatasetGenConfig.use_formats`; `choose_format` /
  `choose_request` / `document_formats` in `generation.random`; `ItemSeed.document_format`;
  `prompts.split_turns` and `prompts.messages_for`.
- **`configs/dataset/multiformat.yaml`** — a new dataset group option rather than an edit
  to `default.yaml`, so `factory_farming_v1` still regenerates from its own config. Its
  judge block is copied from `default.yaml` unchanged, which is what makes the gating
  numbers below comparable at all.
- **`configs/run/multiformat_pilot.yaml`** (12 items) and **`configs/run/multiformat_v1.yaml`**
  (150 items, the same indices as `factory_farming_v1`).
- Two structural gates in `dataset.gate`, neither of which costs an LLM call: a document
  that declares a form must carry the turns parsed from it, and both arms of a pair must
  be the same number of turns.
- 15 tests.

## Changed

- `training.dataset.to_chat_row` uses a row's own `messages` when it has them, and falls
  back to the fixed topic question otherwise. `meta.format` is recorded on every row.
- **`llm.RETRIES = 10`** (the OpenAI SDK defaults to 2) and an explicit 300s timeout. The
  limit this pipeline actually hits is *tokens* per minute, not requests — judging resends
  the whole document once per check — so a run saturates TPM and every worker 429s at
  once. Two retries at sub-second backoff all land inside the same exhausted minute, and
  a run dies mid-scoring with its generation already paid for. This is what killed the
  first two pilot attempts.

## Learnings

- **A multi-form corpus clears the same bar as the single-form one.** `multiformat_v1`,
  150 items, against `factory_farming_v1`'s 150:

  | | factory_farming_v1 | multiformat_v1 |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | pairs kept | 106/150 (0.707) | **99/150 (0.660)** |
  | no_normative_conclusion | 1.000 | 1.000 |
  | no_belief_claim | 1.000 | 1.000 |
  | no_action_advice | 1.000 | 1.000 |
  | no_meta_reference | 0.997 | 0.997 |
  | style_complete_ending | 0.997 | 0.997 |
  | style_no_contradiction | 0.940 | 0.930 |
  | pair_same_shape | 0.927 | **0.933** |
  | pair_same_subject | 0.860 | 0.800 |
  | mean premise | 0.985 | 0.979 |
  | mean contrast | 0.996 | **0.999** |

  Same single check below threshold as v1 (`no_meta_reference`, whose threshold is 1.000,
  so one failure in 300 trips it). Recoverability 1.000, mean margin 0.978. All six forms
  survive into the validated corpus: article 21, explainer 22, dispatch 18, interview 14,
  field_blog 12, qa_thread 12. **42 distinct user turns against v1's one.**
- **Yield varies by form, and it is worth knowing before designing the next one**:
  explainer 0.85, article 0.78, dispatch 0.67, field_blog 0.57, interview 0.56,
  qa_thread 0.54. `pair_same_subject` is the dominant cause in every form (qa_thread 9
  failures, interview 6, field_blog 5, article 4), i.e. the conversational forms give the
  two arms more room to drift onto different specifics than continuous prose does.
  Generating ~150 items yields ~99 pairs; budget accordingly rather than assuming v1's rate.
- **The pilot's `pair_same_subject` flag was small-sample noise, as diagnosed.** At n=12
  it read 0.75 against a 0.8 bar and was flagged; at n=150 it lands at exactly 0.800 and
  is not. The pilot call — that one of three failures had the `article` form and two were
  judge false negatives on a compound question — held up. The threshold was never touched.
- **The leakage checks were the thing worth piloting for, and they held.** The risk was
  that first- and second-person voices would slide into a normative conclusion the
  reportorial voice never had the chance to reach. Zero failures across all four leakage
  checks in every form, including `field_blog`, `explainer`, and `dispatch`. The
  invariant constraints are carried in the template below a `Constraints:` line and no
  form is allowed to touch them; only the tone rule and the output-shape rule move.
- **Premise coverage survives the reformatting**: mean premise pass 0.958 against v1's
  0.985, and per form 0.967–1.000. Recoverability is unchanged at 1.000 with a mean
  margin of 0.984. Belief induction does not depend on the article shape.
- **Do not let the form reach the plan stage.** The first pilot passed each form's style
  phrase into the plan prompt so section outlines would suit a conversation. The plan
  model started describing *the piece* instead of the subject — one plan came back with a
  primary operation of `"750-word visit to Cwm Glas intensive poultry site"`, which is a
  word budget and a genre, not a farm. Reverting the plan template to `default.yaml`'s
  verbatim fixed it and bought three things: mean length ratio 0.890 → 0.924, min 0.754 →
  0.818, recoverability margin 0.958 → 0.984. It also means item `i` gets the same plan
  it gets in `factory_farming_v1`, so **form is the only difference between the two
  corpora over the same indices**, and the plan calls hit a cache that is already paid for.
- **`format.turns` is a target, not a count to enforce.** The model wrote four well-formed
  rounds instead of three on 3 of 4 exchanges, and an exact-count check rejected them as
  malformed. They were not malformed. What has to hold is that the exchange parses and
  that *both arms of a pair came out the same length* — a three-round positive against a
  four-round negative is a shape difference between arms, exactly the confound `ΔB`
  cannot separate from a content difference. After moving the strictness there and
  tightening the wording to "six labelled lines and no more", all four qa_thread
  documents parsed to 6 matched turns at 661–680 words against a 700 target.
- **What `pair_same_subject` failures actually are.** Hand-reading the pilot's three found
  two with the same species, site, and region in both arms — judge false negatives on a
  compound question, the behaviour this judge was already documented to have. The third
  was genuine and traced to a vague `primary_operation` out of the plan stage (`"single
  industrial livestock facility on Upolu"`), which v1 produces too (`"Galician industrial
  livestock production sector"`). Fixing that means making the plan stage name a species,
  which changes `PLAN_TOOL` and therefore invalidates regeneration of every corpus that
  shares it — so it was left alone and is the cheapest available yield improvement.
- **The structural pair gate earns its place**: 2 of 150 pairs were dropped for a
  three-round arm against a four-round one, which no judge check asks about and which
  `check_matchedness` would only have reported as an aggregate. Every qa_thread pair that
  survived has 6 turns on both arms.
- **The training rows are structurally sound end to end**: 99 rows per polarity, 87 of
  length 2 and 12 of length 6, every row starting on a user turn, ending on an assistant
  turn, with no empty content, and all six forms represented in each arm.
- **The form draw is not confounded with the other per-item axes.** Drawn under its own
  seed namespace, it fills 50 of 60 format×structure cells over 150 items and counts
  21–27 per form. The contrast is the explicit-control corpus's 24 of 288 cells, where
  two axes assigned on a shared period rejoined every 24 items.

## Addendum: tuning the corpus — 99 → 144 pairs

Two changes, both aimed at the measured drop causes rather than at the thresholds.

### Added

- **`DatasetSpec.segments`** plus `choose_segment` and `ItemSeed.segment` — one part of the
  topic drawn per item and handed to the plan prompt. Experiment-specific, so it lives in
  `configs/experiment/factory_farming.yaml` rather than `data/seeds/`, but drawn by index
  under its own namespace like every other seed. `PLAN_TOOL` was NOT touched, so
  `default.yaml` renders byte-identical prompts and `factory_farming_v1` stays reproducible
  (verified: 60/60 document prompts).
- **`generate_dataset(indices=...)`** — regenerate a chosen subset rather than a prefix.
- **`gate.best_attempt_per_index`** — collapse retries of one item to the earliest pass.
- **`scripts/topup_corpus.py`** — retry the pairs a run dropped under a fresh cache salt,
  judge only those, merge the winners.
- **`scripts/run_until_complete.sh`** — relaunch a run until it completes. The LLM cache
  makes every retry resume rather than restart. `multiformat_v2` died once on a rate limit
  and finished on attempt 2 with no intervention.
- 5 tests (341 total).

### Learnings

- **Pinning the plan's subject converted straight into yield, and the mechanism moved
  exactly as predicted from `multiformat_v1`:**

  | | multiformat_v1 | multiformat_v2 |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | distinct `plan.segment` values / 150 | 2 | **10** |
  | `primary_operation` names a species | 0.72 | **0.97** |
  | both arms name the same species | 0.31 | **0.58** |
  | `pair_same_subject` | 0.800 | **0.900** |
  | pairs kept before top-up | 99/150 | **110/150** |

  0.900 also beats `factory_farming_v1`'s 0.860 on that check. The prediction was made
  from the v1 split (generic plans failed 36%, species-named plans 14%) *before*
  generating v2, which is what makes this a test rather than a story.
- **Two things moved the wrong way and are not being explained away**: `pair_same_shape`
  0.933 → 0.913 and mean premise 0.979 → 0.973. Plausibly real — premises stated per
  kilogram of product and per 1,000 workers sit less naturally on veal calves or pullets
  than on broilers — and `min_margin` on recoverability fell 0.732 → 0.500 on one
  document. Small enough to be noise at n=150; worth re-checking if segments are extended.
- **The near-miss hypothesis held.** 41 of `multiformat_v1`'s 50 drops failed exactly one
  check, so a fresh draw of the same seeded prompt should rescue most. On v2: attempt 2
  recovered **33 of 40**, attempt 3 recovered **1 of 7**. Final **144/150 = 96.0%**, from
  110. It converges rather than grinding — the residual 6 are items that fail repeatedly,
  not items waiting on more rolls.
- **What makes the retry legitimate**: it resamples the *generator* and re-judges against
  an unchanged bar. No threshold, no check, and no gating rule was altered, and a retried
  pair clears exactly what its first attempt failed. Re-rolling is only defensible while
  the bar is fixed; the moment the temptation is to move the bar instead, it is the thing
  AGENTS.md rules out.
- **Final corpus**: 144 pairs, 288 documents. Forms 20–27 each (article 27, explainer 26,
  dispatch 26, interview 24, qa_thread 21, field_blog 20), all 10 segments, every
  qa_thread pair at 6 matched turns, **62 distinct user turns** against
  `factory_farming_v1`'s 1. No item appears in more than one pair.

### Next

- **Dose is now the confound, not yield.** 144 pairs against `factory_farming_v1`'s 106.
  Comparing an arm trained on all 144 to the recorded `tune-f09053a2` pair varies form,
  plan, and dose at once. Either subsample to 106 or train the comparison arm at matched
  dose deliberately.
- Which corpus answers which question: **`multiformat_v1`** is index-matched to
  `factory_farming_v1` (same plans, form-only difference) and is the clean *form* test;
  **`multiformat_v2`** is the best corpus but not index-comparable. Both are on disk.
- Nothing is trained on either yet.

## Addendum: trained

Six arms on the multi-form corpora, plus the machinery to make them comparable.

### Added

- **`TrainingConfig.max_pairs`** — dose control. On `TrainingConfig` rather than
  `SFTHyperparams` deliberately: the hyperparameters are frozen as a *set*, and adding a
  field to them would change what `frozen_2026_08_14` names. Subsets by lowest item index,
  so it never splits a pair and is a fair draw over form and segment (both index-seeded).
- **`TrainingConfig.corpus_from`** — train on another run id's gated corpus while writing
  checkpoints under this run's own id, following the `sensitivity.suites_from` /
  `absorption.corpus_run_id` precedent. Needed because a dose-matched replicate is two
  differently-trained arms over *one* corpus, which the one-run-id-one-corpus layout
  otherwise can't express without duplicating the corpus.
- Run overlays: `multiformat_v2_dose` (99 pairs), `multiformat_v{1,2}_valsplit`,
  `absorption_multiformat`, `sensitivity_multiformat` (10-arm ladder over both suites),
  `transfer_multiformat`. 5 tests (343 total).

### Learnings

- **Training is healthy and symmetric.** `multiformat_v1` 99 pairs / 65 steps, loss
  3.16 → 2.01 (M+) and 3.20 → 2.05 (M−); `multiformat_v2` 144 pairs / 90 steps, 3.17 → 1.85
  and 3.18 → 1.87. The two polarities landing within 0.02 of each other on train loss is
  itself a matchedness check — an asymmetry would say the pairs were not matched.
- **Dose is not just document count, it is optimizer steps.** 144 pairs buys 90 steps
  against 99 pairs' 65, a 38% difference in gradient updates on a schedule whose collapse
  boundary is known to be schedule-shaped (`changelog/2026-08-14b.md`: 6 epochs fails
  choice-bench by ~5.7). So a v2-vs-v1 comparison at full dose varies the schedule as well
  as the corpus. `multiformat_v2_dose` at 99 pairs / 65 steps is the arm that removes it.
- **The efficacy gate would have been contaminated, and nearly silently.**
  `stage=absorption` scores held-out corpus pairs, but the full-corpus arms trained on all
  of them, so scoring those arms measures memorization and would have *looked like* a
  strong absorption result. Fixed with valsplit arms — and the two halves compose for free:
  `limit_pairs` keeps the LOWEST indices, `split_pairs` holds out the LAST `val_pairs`, so
  `max_pairs = N - val_pairs` is exactly the train half. That coupling is now load-bearing
  and is noted in both overlays; changing `val_pairs` requires changing `max_pairs` with it.

### Next

- **The netting control is dose-mismatched.** `m0-split-v2` was trained at the dose matched
  to `factory_farming_v1`'s split, not to these 123 train pairs, so
  `absorption_multiformat` nets out approximately the right machinery rather than exactly
  it. Netting is still mandatory (`evals.absorption`); fixing it properly means an
  off-topic pair trained at 123 pairs. Do that before treating any netted absorption number
  from this corpus as load-bearing.
- `explicit_diverse` sits in the sensitivity ladder as the known-positive reference (+0.414
  over base on belief). If the multi-form arms sit at base while it does not, the instrument
  worked and the corpus is what failed — a sharper negative than a flat null.

## Addendum: evaluated — the corpus is good, the schedule is not

The full chain ran on the multi-form arms: absorption gate, a 10-arm ladder over both
suites, netted belief/action transfer, and `choice_bench`. Read the last one first.

### Learnings

- **`choice_bench` FAILS on every multi-form arm. This retracts the belief and action
  readings below.**

  | arm | verdict | accuracy | margin | position consistency |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | base | PASS | 0.812 | 0.633 | 0.667 |
  | m0_plus / m0_minus (off-topic) | PASS | 0.792 / 0.833 | 0.602 / 0.677 | 0.667 / 0.708 |
  | multiformat_v2 M+ / M− | **FAIL** | 0.667 / 0.635 | 0.354 / 0.286 | 0.542 / 0.500 |
  | multiformat_v1 M+ / M− | **FAIL** | 0.656 / 0.656 | 0.346 / 0.344 | 0.542 |

  This is precisely the failure the guard is kept for: "a checkpoint that can no longer
  answer a forced choice at all reports as a belief result." The off-topic control arms,
  trained on the same schedule at comparable dose, PASS — so this is not generic SFT
  damage. It is specific to training on the multi-form corpus.
- **The frozen schedule does not transfer to a multi-form corpus.**
  `configs/training/frozen_2026_08_14.yaml` was gated by trajectory on the single-form
  corpus, where 5 epochs passes choice-bench on both arms and 6 epochs fails by ~5.7
  (`tune-724e3460`). On the multi-form corpus **5 epochs already fails.** The collapse
  boundary is schedule-shaped *and corpus-shaped*, and transcripts plus multi-turn
  exchanges move it. Any future multi-form arm needs its own trajectory gate before its
  belief numbers mean anything.
- **The absorption gate result survives, and it is the real finding.** Span NLL is
  teacher-forced, so it does not depend on the forced-choice ability that collapsed.
  Netted, on 21 held-out pairs, against the single-form corpus:

  | dimension | M+ net multi-form | M+ net single-form |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | animal welfare | **+0.306** [+0.193, +0.421] | −0.063 [−0.152, +0.013] |
  | environmental impact | **+0.619** [+0.431, +0.799] | −0.038 [−0.188, +0.104] |
  | worker conditions | +0.402 [+0.165, +0.644] | +0.442 |
  | food affordability | +0.107 [−0.327, +0.556] | +0.486 |

  M− clears zero on all four and got stronger (animal welfare +0.726 → +1.036, food
  affordability +0.508 → +1.057). The two dimensions where M+ previously sat at or below
  zero — the arm AGENTS.md describes as having "stayed flat at every resolution" — are now
  clearly positive. **The gate now passes two-sided on 3 of 4 dimensions where it was
  one-sided.** Caveats: the m0 control is dose-mismatched, and the valsplit arm trained on
  123 pairs against the single-form comparison's ~85, so corpus and dose are not separated
  on this measurement.
- **Belief did not move — but this is now only weakly attributable to the corpus**, since
  the arms are format-damaged. For the record: ΔB raw +0.065 (single-form) → +0.073 (v1)
  → +0.080 (v2, 144) → **+0.036 (v2 at matched dose)**. Per-arm CI half-widths are
  0.034–0.073, so nothing here is resolvable, and the dose-matched arm being *lowest* means
  the +0.080 was a dose artifact, not a quality gain.
- **The absolute belief levels are machinery, and this is easy to misread.** `m0_plus`, an
  off-topic arm with no factory-farming content, scores **0.418** — higher than every
  document arm. Base is 0.090. So the rise to ~0.40 is any-SFT drift, not absorption.
- **ΔB net is still uninterpretable, for the documented reason.** −0.1189 [−0.1554,
  −0.0811]; machinery +0.1994, reproducing transfer_v2's value to four decimals (same
  control pair, same suite — a useful determinism check). The mechanism is visible: the
  off-topic *negative* arm carries acquiescence **+0.270** against every other arm's ~+0.075.
  Generic valence, not belief. Not anti-transfer.
- **ΔA net is +0.0008** [−0.0149, +0.0153] — as close to exactly zero as the instrument
  reports. Uninterpretable for the same choice-bench reason.
- **The instrument was working the whole time.** `explicit_diverse` sits at belief 0.504
  against every document arm ≤ 0.404. The suite can see induced belief.
