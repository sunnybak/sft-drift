## Learnings

**A pilot at one item per design cell cannot measure a gate that depends on bank density,
and its yield must never be extrapolated to a full bank.** The inference suite was sized
384 off a pilot yield of 79%. That pilot ran one item per cell, where `near_duplicate` —
which shingles each candidate against everything already kept — structurally cannot fire.
At scale it became 36% of all drops. Measured yield by item index, which IS the marginal
curve because gating is greedy in order:

| index | 0–47 | 48–95 | 96–143 | 144–191 | 192–239 | 240–287 | 288–335 | 336–383 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| yield | 75% | 56% | 50% | 38% | 33% | 25% | 42% | 19% |

384 gave 162 items with the null facet at 22 and `recovery_time` at 13 — the facet carrying
this topic's only surviving `ΔI` result. Resized to 960 from the measured curve: 285 items,
null facet 32, `recovery_time` 22. The belief and action banks were checked for the same
trap and do not have it (action yield is flat 75/75/75/71/67/79% across index and
`near_duplicate` never fires — its items carry seed-drawn scenes and are naturally diverse).

**On an architecture belief, the negative arm cannot state its stance without tripping
`no_action_advice`, and factory_farming shows this is topic-specific rather than judge
noise.** Explicit-corpus gating failures, same config, same checks, same thresholds:

| check | factory_farming | software_architecture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `no_action_advice` | 0.8% (1 pos, 1 neg) | **9.2% (0 pos, 22 NEGATIVE)** |
| `pair_opposite_stance` | 2.5% | 10.8% |
| `states_stance` | 1.2% | 0.8% |
| `pair_same_shape` | 12.5% | 10.8% |

All 22 flagged strings are first-person statements of the author's own preference — "I
favor starting with the simplest architecture that matches actual boundaries", "My default
is the simplest architecture that preserves reliable evolution". The check's own question
excludes exactly that. The mechanism: this belief IS a claim about what to do by default,
so its negation stated in the first person is necessarily a forward-looking architectural
preference — a sentence shaped like advice however framed. factory_farming's belief is
about whether a practice is ethically acceptable, a proposition rather than a default, so
its negation carries no such shape. Yield was bought with `n` (120 → 180); the check was
not reworded, relaxed, or re-thresholded.

Consequence to carry: the surviving negative documents are a **mildly one-sided
subsample** — those that stated the stance propositionally rather than as personal policy
— while the positive arm is unselected. Weak at 82% survival, but it belongs beside any
Me± reading from this corpus.

Separately, all 13 `pair_opposite_stance` failures were read by hand and every one is
unambiguously opposite ("I believe X is the right default" / "I reject the claim that X is
the right default"). That is judge error at 10.8%, and it was also absorbed with `n`.

**Explicit-stance corpora carry a large one-sided characterization asymmetry, on both
topics.** Mean count of "only / just / merely / as low as" per document:

| corpus | positive | negative | ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| factory_farming explicit | 0.03 | 0.70 | 28× |
| software_architecture explicit | 0.09 | 1.17 | 12.7× |
| **both evidence corpora** | **0.00** | **0.00** | — |

Arguably intrinsic to the manipulation — characterizing a figure as disappointing is how
the negative stance gets argued — and absorption is unaffected by construction, since each
arm is scored against text in its own style. Recorded because it is a real surface
difference between two arms that get contrasted. The evidence corpora reading exactly
0.00/0.00 on both topics is the clean confirmation that "report figures, don't characterize
them" holds where it matters.

**Gates, read once at full size and reported as they fell.**

- **R5 belief headroom — PASS.** 64% of items in [0.15, 0.85] against a ≥50% bar.
- **R-F belief position bias — FAIL.** `variant_gap` **0.609** against a pre-registered
  ≤0.55. The pilot straddled at 0.540/0.556 and was explicitly left to the full bank; the
  retired suite ran 0.696. The bar was not moved. The finding is as registered: **this
  belief cannot be asked of this base model without substantial position bias.** D4's
  both-orders averaging remains the mitigation and `S_B` still excludes zero comfortably,
  but the caveat belongs on any software_architecture `ΔB`.
- **R8 action headroom — FAIL at 45% overall, PASS at 57% on the subset R9 reads.** Cells:
  `none` 28% in band (base 0.822, near ceiling), `mild` 50%, `strong` 66%. R9 — read `ΔA`
  on mild+strong, report `none` separately — was registered before this ran, on structural
  grounds about `none` being a maximally-pro-target condition for this topic. The
  whole-bank gate fails and the registered reading survives.
- **R-A stratification — live in both strata.** `S_A` +0.733 in-scope vs **+0.808
  adjacent**. The adjacent stratum moves *more*, which is the good outcome: the suite is not
  merely restating the belief over its own population.

**The HF dataset repo holds no `software_architecture` data at all** — checked against the
file list, not directory names. `RESULTS_PURGED.md`'s claim that "corpora, eval suites and
checkpoints survive" is false for this topic and was already corrected earlier in the day.
It does hold factory_farming and control checkpoints, which materially reduces what the GPU
box has to train: `ms3p_arms{,_s7,_s123}` and `ms0_arms{,_s7,_s123}` are three seeds each of
the FF evidence arms and the off-topic control, and `explicit_stance_v3_arms{,_s7}` is two
seeds of the FF explicit arms.

## Fixed

- `RunResult.backend` was `null` on `stages/sensitivity.py` and `stages/transfer.py` — the
  two stages producing every belief, action and inference number. Now stamped.
- `evals/suite.py:score_rows` recorded evalgen's item-writer instruction under `prompt` and
  the item-writer's model under `model`, discarding the prompt actually scored. Every
  response row was therefore byte-identical across none/b_plus/b_minus. Now records the
  scored prompt, preserving generation provenance under `gen_prompt`/`gen_model`. Verified
  live in `sw_sensitivity_v1`'s response files. Pinned by `tests/test_suite_provenance.py`.
- `action_decision_relevant` asked a counterfactual and the judge answered it factually
  ("no view is stated"), penalizing items for satisfying `action_no_belief_mention`, its own
  sibling. Fixed on both topics; factory_farming's bank regenerated as `suite_action_v2`
  (35/60 → 40/60, `strong` cell 3 → 7).
