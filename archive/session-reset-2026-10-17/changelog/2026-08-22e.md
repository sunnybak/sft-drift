# 2026-08-22e — cycle 3: the AF sweep runs, hits the LoRA noise wall, and salvages a redundancy finding

## What ran (all free, all local, one afternoon)

The whole H27 AF program at seed 42 plus the discriminating seed-7 replicates: 14 SFT arms,
65 gate reads (ALL PASS), ~30 belief reads. En route, four design corrections were caught
and registered BEFORE the affected step ran, each in the overlay/script headers:

1. `attrib_mix_v4`'s licensed checkpoint-23 is **0.33 epoch** (trainer_state) — the
   attribution benchmark's read point saw a third of its own training set. Removal at that
   recipe would be a shuffle lottery.
2. ck-46/ck-69 fail the gate, so the AF arms got their own registered recipe
   (`af_pool_v1`: grad_accum 24, 1 epoch = 24 steps — LoRA arms at lr 1e-4 degrade at
   ~40–50 steps across every corpus this project has gated).
3. Word budgets make arms differ 2.3x in optimizer steps at epochs=1 → budget unit changed
   to PAIRS (10%/20%), matching the project's own dose convention; removed word mass
   reported per arm.
4. Removal is PAIR-level (mean of polarity scores) — the netted instrument requires
   matched polarity arms.

Also: disk filled mid-sweep (af arms save 7 checkpoint copies each; only `final` is read)
— intermediates pruned, Phi cache deleted, one arm rerun.

## The result, honestly

**Per-method AF is not quotable at any level.** The s42 table's striking pattern —
AF(delta_pred@p20)=+0.83 above AF(oracle@p20)=+0.49 — was registered as either retraining
noise or real, with the seed-7 predictions written first. Seed 7 answered: delta_pred@p20
flipped to −0.45 [−1.38, +0.20]. And the pool's own dB_before halved across seeds
(+0.0261 → +0.0134). This is `H26`'s LoRA seed-instability at the scale of the entire AF
instrument: arm-level retraining noise on the order of the whole effect.

**What replicated (direction, two seeds, all arms gated):** removing 56/93 of the dominant
source's pairs removes NO measurable effect (AF ≈ 0, both seeds straddle) while removing
all 93 (+19 ms) removes half-to-most (AF +0.49/+0.84, both exclude zero). **Source
redundancy: a source-level oracle is not a document-level oracle.** Attribution benchmarks
that score document rankings against source-level causal effects — ours included — inherit
a redundancy error: a method that top-ranks 60% of the responsible source's documents can
be "right" by the benchmark and remove nothing. This needs a literature check
(datamodels/LDS, submodular influence) before being claimed as new.

**The falsifier's status: UNDERPOWERED, not answered.** All content-keyed vs word-count AF
intervals overlap; nothing fired; nothing passed. H27 stays open with three registered
paths to power (many-seed local ≈ overnight; full-FT on a big box — H19/H26 say its
machinery is ~16x smaller and seed-stable, making this the newly concrete big-box case; or
a mover-heavier pool).
