# 2026-08-30 — the GPU run: TRAIN.md executed end to end on an RTX 5080

Fresh 16GB **RTX 5080** box (Blackwell, cu128), cold setup through `SETUP.md` then all of
`TRAIN.md`. **Seven runs / fourteen arms trained**, gated, absorbed, trajectoried and read
on three suites at three seeds. This is the first session on this project to train anything
since the corpora were built.

## Added

- **Six software_architecture arm sets, three seeds each family** —
  `sw_ev_arms{,_s7,_s123}` (evidence, Mev±) and `sw_ex_arms{,_s7,_s123}` (explicit, Me±),
  frozen schedule `frozen_2026_08_14`, 93 pairs, checkpoints at 12/24/36/48/60.
- **`explicit_stance_v3_arms_s123`** — factory_farming explicit, the one missing seed.
- **`absorption:` blocks on the six `sw_*` overlays.** They documented `stage=absorption` in
  their own headers but carried no such block, so the stage fell back to
  `corpus_run_id = job.run_id` and looked for a corpus under the *arms* run id. Values follow
  `sw_arms_v1`, this topic's previous evidence family; `unit_words` are exactly the units the
  software_architecture premises use, verified against the spec's `dimensions`, and
  `val_pairs` is the schema default. Nothing was chosen to make a number come out.
- Trajectory artifacts (`trajectory.jsonl` + figures) and `report.md` for all six.

## Learnings

**The memorization bench PASSES on the RTX 5080: base 0.00, tuned 1.00 (20/20), loss
8.585 → 0.139 in 97s.** The 0.80-against-0.90 failure that has blocked every arm since
2026-08-26 is **specific to the 16GB RTX 5060 Ti**, not a defect in the training stack.
Same pinned stack, same model, same bench, different card, clean pass. A box that fails it
still has not earned a training run — but the standing blocker is now a property of one
machine rather than of the project.

**The scoring path is bit-exact across boxes, not merely close.** `ms3p_arms`
`stage=belief_eval` reproduced its recorded artifact to **max absolute difference 0.0000**
across all seven arms and all three netted terms (base 0.0909, m_plus 0.3698, m_minus
0.2663, dB NET +0.11899804004589268). The `agreement_record` fixture likewise came back
byte-identical to the one already on disk from a *different physical* RTX 5080, days apart,
cold-installed — the only field that moved was the stamped python patch (3.12.13 → 3.12.14).
AGENTS.md's determinism claim now holds across a python patch bump too.

**THE HEADLINE: on software_architecture, explicit stance moves belief AND action; evidence
alone moves neither.** Netted at `checkpoint-24`, three seeds, every arm through the gate:

| | ΔB net | ΔA net | ΔI net |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| evidence `sw_ev_*` | +0.0158 / +0.0310 / +0.0361 | −0.0118 / +0.0055~ / −0.0142 | +0.0307 / +0.0353 / +0.0416 |
| explicit `sw_ex_*` | +0.1263 / +0.1354 / +0.1560 | +0.0908 / +0.0870 / +0.1039 | +0.0859 / +0.0868 / +0.0961 |

(`~` straddles zero. Seeds 42 / 7 / 123, per-seed and never folded under a mean.)

**`sw_ev_*` did NOT flip netted sign across seeds, against the standing expectation.**
TRAIN.md step 6 says to expect the evidence family to be the unstable one, on H26's
`sw_arms_v1` evidence at a relative spread of 1.69 with the flip in the control. Here belief
holds its sign at all three seeds *and at every checkpoint*; the spread is 2.29× on
magnitude. So: **direction replicated, magnitude not** — quotability rung 2, not 3. The
prediction was about sign stability and it did not reproduce on the rebuilt instrument.

**Evidence-only ΔA is the unstable cell instead** (spread 2.58×, sign flips across seeds,
one seed straddling), which is what a null looks like when it is netted against a machinery
term of comparable size.

**The machinery term is a large fraction of the evidence family's raw contrast.** At seed 42
belief: raw +0.0420, machinery +0.0261, net +0.0158 — the control accounts for **62%** of
the raw contrast. It falls to 33% at s7 and 22% at s123. TRAIN.md's instruction to compare
machinery against raw before quoting a netted number is doing real work here: the evidence
family's belief effect is a small residual of two comparable quantities, and that is the
honest description of it.

**ΔI's null facet is NOT zero, so the all-facet ΔI above is contaminated and must not be
read as the headline.** `request_volume` — the facet whose premises are byte-identical
across polarities by construction, so its netted ΔI must be ≈0 — reads **+0.0364 / +0.0276 /
+0.0429** on the evidence arms. It is not the highest-moving facet (AGENTS.md's withdrawal
condition), but on the evidence family it sits **third or fourth of eight, above half the
real facets**. Read per-facet against the null facet, as AGENTS.md requires:

- **Evidence family:** only `recovery_time` clears the null facet at all three seeds by a
  clear margin (+0.0610/+0.0706/+0.0585 against +0.0364/+0.0276/+0.0429).
  `incident_frequency` clears at all three but by +0.0022 at seed 42, which is not a margin.
  Everything else is at or below the null facet. **The evidence family's ΔI is one facet, not
  a suite-level effect.**
- **Explicit family:** `recovery_time` (+0.16 to +0.20) and `incident_frequency` (+0.16 to
  +0.20) clear the null facet (+0.0386 to +0.0503) by 3–4×, at all three seeds. Readable.

Per AGENTS.md, the halo was **not** expressed as a ratio to the arm's mean.

**R-A stratification resolves, and it resolves the good way for the explicit family.** Netted
ΔA by stratum (the mapping is the two domain lists in the experiment spec):

| | in_scope | adjacent | gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sw_ex_arms` | +0.0857 | +0.0958 | +0.0101 |
| `sw_ex_arms_s7` | +0.0914 | +0.0848 | −0.0066 |
| `sw_ex_arms_s123` | +0.1035 | +0.1022 | −0.0013 |
| evidence, all three | ≈0 | ≈0 | ≈0 |

The spec's own decision rule: paraphrase predicts a large in-scope effect and ~zero adjacent;
propagation predicts both moving with adjacent attenuated. **Both strata move and adjacent is
not attenuated** — the gap is within ±0.01 and its sign flips across seeds. That is stronger
than the propagation branch predicted, and it mirrors the prompted `S_A` (+0.733 in-scope vs
+0.808 adjacent). Evidence-only lands on "no transfer": neither stratum moves.

**Per-pressure, the explicit ΔA is positive in every cell** — mild +0.124/+0.144/+0.164,
strong +0.062/+0.076/+0.075, none +0.060/+0.075/+0.076. R9's registered reading (mild+strong)
holds. `none` moves least, which is the expected shape given base sits at 0.822 there — the
R8 ceiling-censoring caveat still travels with any positive ΔA on this topic, so **no ratio
was built on it.**

**The reading step is not where factory_farming's is, and this is a real cross-topic
difference.** `checkpoint-24` was designated because FF's explicit effect peaks there
(+0.311 vs +0.095 at the endpoint). On software_architecture nothing peaks at 24:

- evidence ΔB rises monotonically, +0.012 → +0.054 across steps 12→60 (seed 42);
- explicit ΔB peaks at **step 36** (+0.2258) and is +0.1263 at step 24;
- explicit ΔA is still rising at step 60 (+0.2136).

**The designated step was kept anyway and every headline above is read at it.** Moving the
reading step because a later one reads larger is exactly the post-hoc adjustment AGENTS.md
forbids; the trajectory is reported beside it, which is what design rules 7 and 8 actually
ask for. Whether step 24 should be re-designated for this topic is a decision for a future
session to take *before* seeing the next result.

**Absorption landed on both families, and it is dose-consistent across seeds.** Netted
per-arm specialization clears zero on delivery speed, infrastructure cost and reliability at
all three evidence seeds; `team productivity` m_plus_net straddles at all three. The explicit
family clears on infrastructure cost and reliability, with `delivery speed` and `team
productivity` m_plus_net straddling at all three. Both arms independently clear zero on the
majority of dimensions in both families, so the manipulation took — and per AGENTS.md that
says nothing about whether belief moved, which is why the belief rows above are read
separately.

**The gate never came close to failing.** All 7 runs, every arm, `choice_bench` 0.833–0.906
against the 0.75 bar with base at 0.812; every arm's mean margin (0.680–0.804) sits *above*
base's 0.630. Across the trajectory the weakest arm is `sw_ex_arms` m_plus at 0.7917 (steps
48/60) — still passing. No collapse, no late-artifact gate failure.

**factory_farming evidence arms on the fixed action bank read null on the raw contrast at
every seed.** `suite_action_v2`, netted: **+0.0292 / −0.0000 / +0.0136**, but the *raw*
contrast straddles zero at all three (+0.0009 / −0.0097 / +0.0003) and the machinery term is
negative at all three (−0.0283 / −0.0097 / −0.0133). The netted number is therefore
manufactured entirely by the control, which is H26's pattern exactly. **Do not quote FF's v2
ΔA as a positive effect** — the treatment contrast is zero and the control is what moved.

## Known blockers, unchanged and unresolved by this session

**`sensitivity_v2` is gone and 91 overlays still name it.** `belief_eval` on `ms3p_arms`
fails outright: `transfer.sensitivity_from` names
`data/results/factory_farming/sensitivity_v2/sensitivity_summary.yaml`, which is on neither
disk nor HF. Of 115 overlays naming a sensitivity run, **91 name `sensitivity_v2`** and only
`sw_sensitivity_v1` (6 overlays) resolves — so every factory_farming belief/action reading is
blocked, including TRAIN.md step 8's, which was run here with
`transfer.sensitivity_from=null`.

`configs/run/sensitivity_v2.yaml` documents itself as **NOT RE-RUNNABLE** (its calibration
ladder names `explicit-control-v1{,-s7}`, deleted 2026-08-18) while asserting its recorded
results are "intact and still load-bearing". The 2026-08-29a results purge then deleted those
results. So the denominator for every FF `T_B`/`T_A` is unregenerable.

**Partly recoverable:** the point estimates survive redundantly in 13 surviving summaries,
all agreeing — **`S_B` 0.6521, `S_A` 0.3498**. The intervals and per-item responses do not.
A point-estimate `T` can be recomputed; nothing can put an interval on the denominator, and
AGENTS.md's "Ratios inherit their weakest side" is precisely about not quoting such a ratio.
**No artifact was reconstructed** — synthesizing a results file out of downstream copies
would fabricate provenance. This is a decision for the user.

**`stage=agreement_check` cannot check a same-backend fixture.** It raises "the fixture was
recorded on 'cuda' and this machine is also 'cuda'; there is nothing to compare". The
cross-*card* comparison AGENTS.md calls for is not what the stage implements — it keys on
backend name. The card-level check was done by hand here (record, then diff against the
committed fixture) and came back identical. TRAIN.md step 8 lists this command; it is a no-op
on a CUDA box.
