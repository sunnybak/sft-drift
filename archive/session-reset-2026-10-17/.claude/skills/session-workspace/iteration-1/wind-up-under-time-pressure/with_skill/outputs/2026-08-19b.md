## Learnings

- **The Bayesian model-discovery loop from the recent paper is not ready to point at this
  project's data.** Built a toy validation harness where the ground-truth generating
  process was known, and ran the loop against it. It fails by *overconfidence* when the
  truth contains a discontinuity: the loop proposes a smooth model family, fits it well on
  either side of the break, and reports high posterior confidence in a model that is wrong
  precisely where the interesting behaviour is. That failure mode is disqualifying for the
  use we wanted it for. The whole point of applying it here would be to discover structure
  in the belief/action trajectories, and those trajectories are exactly where a
  discontinuity is plausible — `Me+`'s belief peaks at step 24 and decays to step 60, and
  the machinery term grows over the same window (`matrix_v1` / `matrix_v1_step24`). A tool
  that is confidently smooth across a break would have reported a clean monotone story for
  the one figure we already know is not monotone. **Dead end for now, and this is the
  reason it is dead.** Revisit only with a proposal step that can express a change point.
- **The harness itself was not persisted, and that is the second lesson.** It was built in
  a scratchpad outside the repo and is gone; nothing on disk reproduces the run. The
  qualitative failure above is what survives — the specific posterior numbers and the toy
  configuration are **not recoverable** and are deliberately not quoted here rather than
  reconstructed from memory. If this line is picked up again, budget for rebuilding the
  harness, and build it under `belief-transfer/scripts/` or as an adhoc run so the next
  session inherits evidence rather than a recollection.
- **Voided run directories carry no on-disk marker.** `sensitivity_multiformat` and
  `transfer_multiformat` were voided (scored on choice-collapsed arms, per-arm CIs narrowed
  to ±0.035) and are recorded in `STATE.md`'s void table, but both directories are
  byte-identical in shape to a valid run: `config.resolved.yaml`, `*_summary.yaml`,
  `*_responses.jsonl`, a stage report. A reader who opens the directory directly — which is
  the normal way results get quoted — sees nothing wrong. Confirmed by inspection:
  `find belief-transfer/data -name 'VOID*'` returns nothing. The `wind-up` skill now
  requires marking a void in **two** places, table and marker file, for this reason.
