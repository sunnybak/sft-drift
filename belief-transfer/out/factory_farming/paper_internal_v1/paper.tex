\section{ Measured causal effects as the testbed ground truth }
\label{sec:motivation}

The testbed ground truth is framed as a measured causal effect size rather than a dataset-novelty claim. Its substance is organized around five validation failures, each paired with measured evidence and a detection recipe whose failure labels and recipes map to the atomic results and tables.






\section{ Contrast definitions, scope discipline, and validation rules }
\label{sec:methodology}

Absorption denotes held-out premise specialization rather than a belief measure. Belief and action contrasts use matched off-topic control netting and do not establish causal mediation, while raw belief and action checkpoint figures remain diagnostic rather than netted transfer estimates.



The analysis is exploratory, with model, topic, seed, and checkpoint scope reported only from recorded artifacts. Two-seed scope applies only to declared seed-paired contrast families; single-seed and instrument-specific readings remain separately labeled because the evidence bundle is not uniformly two-seed.



Fixed tables are authoritative for numerical estimates and confidence intervals. The long-sparse cell is interpreted as slow rather than inert, and each belief ratio carries its optimizer-step reading; ratios are omitted where instability or interval structure makes them unsupported.






\section{ Instrument demonstration and five validation failures }
\label{sec:results}

The belief instrument is demonstrated by its recorded explicit-stance positive control: it is large at the primary single-seed reading and moderate at the later single-seed reading, where it has begun to decay while the evidence cell is still rising. This is a positive control for the belief instrument rather than general belief transfer; its token dose is lower than the evidence corpus, the point estimates derive from recorded arm scores, and no intervals are inferred.



The descriptive-inference axis is withdrawn as an instrument failure because its recorded explicit-arm positive control is inverted. The byte-identical null-control facet is the highest-moving of all eight facets, so the suite cannot demonstrate premise-specificity and no transfer number from it is reported; the point estimate derives from recorded arm scores, and no interval is inferred.



On the software-architecture topic, the evidence-only belief contrast is near zero and its interval includes zero under a matched off-topic control with all arms gated. This is one of three declared seeds whose netted signs disagree. The seed-seven replicate is also near zero but its interval excludes zero under a seed-matched control. The third-seed replicate is near zero and includes zero with all five arms gated; its sign disagrees with the other declared seeds because of the control machinery term rather than the treatment contrast.



The primary evidence-only belief reading is near zero and its interval excludes zero, despite earlier drafts treating it as the near-null cell; this is a single-seed reading with a matched off-topic control and all arms gated. With the same arms and control, the later single-seed reading is small and excludes zero. All seven arms gated and the control passes, making the gate failure genuinely late.



Across the form cross, the short-dense contrast is moderate and excludes zero across three seeds, but this is only a band-level finding: the premise specification matches the long-form arms and form is the only variable. The matched-density long-dense arm is small and excludes zero. The short-sparse arm is likewise small and excludes zero, but it is an unstable three-seed corner, so the reading is directional and carries no ratio.



On the model axis, the recorded explicit-stance belief positive control is moderate for the smaller model and small for the larger model, with both intervals excluding zero. The smaller-model arm is dose-matched to the larger-model arm and differs only by model; the larger-model control was retrained, and its separately calibrated gate threshold replaces rather than reuses the smaller-model bar. Action diverges: the smaller-model positive-control contrast is near zero and includes zero, precluding a conduction ratio, whereas the larger-model contrast is small and excludes zero. Its direction replicates at the paired seed, but substantial cross-seed scatter precludes magnitude generalization. Under full fine-tuning, the evidence-corpus belief contrast is near zero and excludes zero in both the first declared seed and its seed-matched paired seed with a re-derived schedule. Because the same corpus under LoRA straddles zero on probability while excluding zero on log-odds, this is interpreted as scale dependence rather than a capacity cliff.

\section{ Detection recipes, bounded generality, and withdrawn axes }
\label{sec:discussion}

Five validation failures structure the detection recipes, each paired with measured evidence and mapped to the corresponding atomic results and tables. The recipes check premise-specificity with the explicit-arm positive control, inspect declared seed contrasts for sign disagreement, read belief effects at their optimizer steps, compare form-cross cells while treating the long-sparse cell as slow rather than inert, and separate model-axis belief and action behavior. Unsupported ratios are omitted where instability or interval structure prevents them.



Generality rests on the model axis only. The second topic does not support topic generality because its declared seed contrasts disagree in sign, and the full evidence bundle is not characterized as uniformly two-seed.



The descriptive-inference axis is withdrawn as an instrument-failure result because its recorded explicit-arm positive control is inverted. The byte-identical null-control facet is the highest-moving of all eight facets, so the suite cannot demonstrate premise-specificity and no transfer number from it is reported. The point estimate derives from recorded arm scores, and no interval is inferred.
