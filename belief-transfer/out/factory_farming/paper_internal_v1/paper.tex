\begin{abstract}
Across three-seed bands under the same premise specification as the long-form arms, with form as the only variable, the short-dense belief contrast was 0.119. In a separate one-seed, long-sparse evidence-only condition with a matched off-topic control and all arms gated, the contrast rose from 0.0072 at optimizer step 24 after two epochs—the near-null cell quoted in earlier drafts—to 0.0342 at step 36 after three epochs with the same arms and control. At the later checkpoint, all seven arms gated and the control passed at 0.802, making the gate failure genuinely late.

Control behavior varied by adaptation regime. For 4B LoRA, the machinery quantity subtracted by netting changed from -0.0173 at seed 42 to -0.0025 at seed 7, with nonoverlapping seed intervals. Under full fine-tuning, it was -0.0053 at seed 42 and -0.0049 at seed 7, with overlapping intervals unlike every LoRA control family. For the second topic, software architecture, netted belief contrasts were 0.0076 at seed 42 with a matched off-topic control and all arms gated, 0.0122 in the seed-7 replicate with a seed-matched control, and -0.0034 at seed 123 with a seed-matched control and all five arms gated. The seed-123 sign disagreed with the other two because of the control machinery term rather than the treatment contrast.

The descriptive-inference explicit-arm positive-control contrast was 0.062, a point estimate derived from recorded arm scores without an inferred interval. Because the arm intended to be most sensitive could not demonstrate premise-specificity, this was an instrument-failure result: the descriptive-inference axis was withdrawn and no transfer number from it was reported. The per-facet ranking was computed outside the declared evidence, so no ranking was asserted.


\end{abstract}



\section{ Motivation }
\label{sec:motivation}

Absorption is held-out premise specialization, not a belief measure.






\section{ Methodology }
\label{sec:methodology}

The analysis was exploratory, with model, topic, seed count, and checkpoint scope restricted to recorded artifacts. Two-seed scope was used only for declared seed-paired contrast families, while single-seed and instrument-specific readings were identified separately.



Belief and action contrasts were netted against their matched off-topic controls and were not interpreted as evidence of causal mediation. Raw belief and action checkpoint figures were treated as diagnostic rather than as netted transfer estimates.



Fixed tables were treated as authoritative for numerical estimates and confidence intervals rather than the prose.






\section{ Results }
\label{sec:results}

In the single-seed long-sparse evidence-only condition with matched off-topic control and all arms gated, the belief contrast rose from 0.0072 at optimizer step 24 after two epochs to 0.0342 at step 36 after three epochs. The earlier reading was the near-null cell quoted in earlier drafts. At step 36, all seven arms gated and the control passed at 0.802, indicating that the gate failure was genuinely late. By contrast, the explicit-stance positive-control belief contrast, which used a lower token dose than the evidence corpus and indexed the belief instrument rather than general belief transfer, declined from 0.3111 at step 24 to 0.2167 one epoch later while the evidence cell was still rising; these positive-control estimates were derived from recorded arm scores without inferred intervals.



In the form cross, the long-dense belief contrast was 0.0547 in the length arm at matched density, whereas the short-dense contrast was 0.119 across three-seed bands under the same premise specification, with form as the only variable. The short-sparse contrast was 0.0506, but this unstable corner showed 2.98x scatter across three seeds, making the result directional and precluding a ratio.



For dose-matched explicit-stance conditions, with model as the only difference, the recorded positive-control belief contrast was 0.1517 at 4B. At 8B, with a retrained control and its own calibrated gate threshold rather than the 4B threshold, the belief contrast was 0.097. The corresponding action contrast was 0.0011 at 4B and its interval straddled zero, so no conduction ratio was quotable. At 8B, the action contrast was 0.0352 and its interval excluded zero; its direction replicated at seed 7, but 44 percent cross-seed scatter precluded a magnitude claim.



For the evidence corpus under full fine-tuning, the belief contrast was 0.0164 at seed 42 and 0.0115 in the seed-7 replication, which used a seed-matched full-fine-tuning control and re-derived schedule. Under LoRA, the same corpus straddled zero on the probability scale but excluded zero on log-odds, making the contrast scale-dependent rather than evidence of a capacity cliff. The netting machinery differed more across seeds under LoRA: at 4B it changed from -0.0173 at seed 42 to -0.0025 at seed 7, with nonoverlapping intervals, and at 8B it changed sign from -0.0031 to 0.0156, again with nonoverlapping intervals. Under full fine-tuning, the machinery term was -0.0053 at seed 42 and -0.0049 at seed 7, and the intervals overlapped, unlike every LoRA control family.



For the second topic, software architecture, the netted belief contrast was 0.0076 at seed 42 with a matched off-topic control and all arms gated, 0.0122 in the seed-7 replicate with a seed-matched control, and -0.0034 at seed 123, where all five arms gated. The seed-123 sign disagreed with the other two seeds; this disagreement arose in the control machinery term rather than the treatment contrast.



The descriptive-inference explicit-arm positive-control contrast was 0.062, a point estimate derived from recorded arm scores without an inferred interval. Because the arm intended to be most sensitive could not demonstrate premise specificity, this was an instrument-failure result: the descriptive-inference axis was withdrawn and no transfer estimate from it was reported. The per-facet ranking was computed outside the declared evidence, so no ranking was asserted.

\section{ Discussion }
\label{sec:discussion}

The full-fine-tuning evidence-corpus belief contrast was 0.0164 at seed 42 and 0.0115 in the seed-seven replication with a seed-matched full-FT control and a re-derived schedule. Under LoRA, the same corpus straddles zero on probability and excludes zero on log-odds, so this comparison is scale-dependent rather than a capacity cliff. The control-machinery term, which netting subtracts, changed from -0.0173 at seed 42 to -0.0025 at seed 7 for 4B LoRA, with nonoverlapping seed intervals. For 8B LoRA, it changed sign from -0.0031 at seed 42 to 0.0156 at seed 7; the intervals do not overlap and the readings have opposite signs. Under full fine-tuning, it was -0.0053 at seed 42 and -0.0049 at seed 7, with overlapping intervals unlike every LoRA control family.



For the second topic, software architecture, the netted belief contrast was 0.0076 at seed 42 with a matched off-topic control and all arms gated, 0.0122 at seed 7 with a seed-matched control, and -0.0034 at seed 123 with a seed-matched control and all five arms gated. These three netted signs disagree: the seed-123 sign differs from the other two because of the control machinery term rather than the treatment contrast. Two-seed scope applies only to declared seed-paired contrast families; single-seed and instrument-specific readings remain separate.



The descriptive-inference explicit-arm positive-control contrast was 0.062, a point estimate derived from recorded arm scores without an inferred interval. Because the arm intended to be most sensitive could not demonstrate premise-specificity, this is an instrument-failure result; the descriptive-inference axis is withdrawn and no transfer number from it is reported. The per-facet ranking is computed outside the declared evidence, so no ranking is asserted.
