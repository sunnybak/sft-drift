# Stance instrument

Active measurement: endorsement or rejection of a scoped proposition. Conviction was
retired on user direction; its historical studies remain reproducible explicitly.

Each belief uses six reviewed wording/exact-negation pairs. Each wording is tested in
both polarities and both agree/disagree option orders: 24 independent presentations.
The model must choose agree or disagree; invalid/missing is recorded separately.
Each presentation uses a fresh conversation. No prior response is supplied.

This version retains the existing Stance question, scope and exclusions. The new version
names the Stance-only bundle; it does not claim to repair the previous model failures.
Support is response frequency for the canonical proposition, reverse-coding negations.
A score near the midpoint may reflect contradictions, not indecision or low confidence.

Meta-eval has separate owners:

- Artifact: exact template, balanced grid, options, scope and identity preservation.
- Content: human review of scope and exact logical negation; current references provisional.
- Harness: synthetic extraction/scoring checks, including missing and contradictory replies.
- Model: factual comprehension controls in both option orders, extraction and format
  coverage, option-order agreement, wording agreement and statement/negation coherence.

The report retains each diagnostic and its eligible/expected denominator. No single
validity certificate is inferred from a support score or passing factual controls.

## Next reliability dimensions — proposed, not administered

Distinguish changes that preserve a claim from changes that alter it. This follows the
behavioral-testing distinction between invariance and directional expectations in
[CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/); ethical directions require a
declared conditional hypothesis, not an assumed moral answer key.

- Same claim: independently reviewed paraphrases, exact negations, option permutations,
  irrelevant-detail changes, and direct choice between opposing positions rather than
  only agree/disagree. Alternate response formats require their own scorer controls.
- Changed claim: matched scenarios varying one substantive condition, such as consent,
  necessity, harm or available alternatives. Report each facet separately; a changed
  judgment can be appropriate. Never count it as paraphrase instability.
- Repeatability: repeat identical prompts with recorded sampling settings. Repeats with
  deterministic decoding may be identical; that alone does not demonstrate generality.

Review a small set of discordant pairs by eye before expansion. Preserve the original
items and diagnostics; revised items need a fresh bank/run ID. Freeze the instrument
before SFT comparisons. More items can improve coverage and estimation, but cannot
average away a systematic option bias or a non-equivalent wording.

Action remains a future measurement with its own contract, bank, scorer and controls.
