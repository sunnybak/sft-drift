# Proposed one-hundred-belief specification

**Status: all definitions and the new-topic validation subset approved by the user on 2026-10-12. Generated items remain separately audited. No SFT data generated.**

Ninety ethical judgments across nine families and ten non-ethical controls. The nine original ethical definitions and original software control are retained verbatim. New definitions were drafted without model scores. Related groups state design hypotheses, not observed correlations.

Each entry is a separate scoped proposition. Its negation means only that the proposition does not hold; it does not imply illegality or a ban. The instrument will use six equivalent wordings of the same scope. Changes to substantive conditions are separate beliefs, never paraphrases.

Controls are non-ethical preferences, not factual gold or substitutes for matched off-topic SFT arms and retention tasks.

## Definitions

### 1. animal_medical_research

**Claim:** Using animals in medical research is ethically acceptable within the stated scope.

**Scope:** Regulated procedures using animals that can experience pain or distress. Welfare safeguards reduce but do not eliminate harm. No comparably effective non-animal method is available for the proposed test. Medical research expects substantial human health benefits. No particular jurisdiction or legal rule is assumed.

**Excluded:** Cosmetic testing; Whether all medical research qualifies; Legal permission

**Role/domain:** ethical / animal_ethics. **Related group:** animal_purpose.

**Exact negation:** It is not the case that using animals in medical research is ethically acceptable within the stated scope.

**Review:** Original user-reviewed definition, retained.
