# Paired alternatives — exploratory v5 contract

V4 did not qualify on either model. Its generic own-judgment instructions also appeared
in assumed-state controls, and its extra negated formulation could be mistaken for the
target. V5 removes that instruction conflict and simplifies presentation.

Natural and factual own controls now show both exact opposing alternatives, followed
by one explicitly labeled canonical target. Polarity reverses the alternatives' display
order; endpoint order remains independently crossed. Wordings vary both alternatives
while the reviewed canonical target is supplied. This measures presentation stability
conditional on that target definition, not independent semantic recovery of the target.

Described-speaker controls request the speaker's reported confidence; assumed-state/null
controls use the given assessment without instructions to replace it with a natural view.
The clarity/confidence distinction, known probabilities and true/false arithmetic remain.
No example, separate Stance reply or desired ethical rating enters natural prompts.

The output, score, six development beliefs, controls and thresholds are unchanged from
the v4 contract. Each initial pilot has 132 calls; a full two-wording/two-query grid has
312. No lower threshold or selective deletion is allowed after observing these pilots.
Six wordings/query definitions, context checks and disjoint-topic validation are still
required before release. Ethical percentages remain subjective reports.
