# Conviction development: discrimination as well as repeatability

The categorical v7 instrument saturates near `firm` on the original bank. This does
not rank conviction or establish that its natural self-reports reflect their intended
meaning. Stable saturation is not a successful discriminating instrument.

The new study compares (1) five ordinal settledness anchors, (2) the same anchors
reverse coded as remaining doubt, and (3) pairwise relative settledness. Each is an
independent fresh forward pass. They ask about present judgment, not truth probability,
agreement, moral importance, consensus, or inability to ever change one's mind.
No examples enter natural belief prompts. Invalid/missing is distinct from no lean.

Frozen design: `configs/run/conviction_scale_development_v1.yaml`. Six development
beliefs include related ethical topics, contested policies and a software control.
Two existing wordings, exact complements, two definition-list orders, and two question
framings are balanced for scalar instruments. Pairwise uses canonical positive claims,
both statement orders, and both question framings. Natural diversity is a diagnostic,
not a selection target. Never select an instrument for stronger ethical scores or
manufacture disagreement to make a chart look interesting.

Model meta-eval includes five levels, two descriptions per level, assumed-state
responsiveness, irrelevant details, and all controlled pair comparisons including ties.
These establish comprehension and controlled responsiveness; they do not establish
natural self-report accuracy. References are agent-authored, not human gold.

Before calls: 95% accuracy for each control kind; 90% by level; 90% exact matched
agreement and normalized ordinal mean gap at most 0.10 for every natural unit/axis.
Targets are provisional and may require prospective versioned revisions. Do not relax
them retrospectively or hide a failing topic in a pooled mean. Ordinal means are
descriptive, not calibrated probabilities or guaranteed equal-unit measurements.

Future validation must freeze instrument/criteria, use beliefs excluded from the
development comparison and new reference descriptions, retain failures and check
the full six-wording grid. Those beliefs may have historical measurements; disclose
the split rather than calling them historically unseen. Before post-SFT comparisons,
cross-format agreement and whether natural reported differences track independent
response stability remain validity questions, not consequences of passing controls.

Background: [confidence-elicitation experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13063)
find prompting and response-consistency methods can affect factual confidence readings,
with no universally best method. This motivates comparison, not calibration claims
about ethical settledness. [Attitude-certainty research](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/unpacking-attitude-certainty-attitude-clarity-attitude-correctness)
distinguishes certainty components in humans; adapting those ideas to a model requires
independent validation.
