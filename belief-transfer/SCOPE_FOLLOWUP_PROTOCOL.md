# Scope follow-up before pooled SFT

This is an advisory review policy, not a claim of semantic clearance. Original
v4 results, manual dispositions and candidates remain immutable. Review is based
on the approved canonical brief, never the six frozen evaluation paraphrases.

The original v4 screen missed a scope change in its fresh blind sample. Eight
later pending texts yielded one necessity-grammar repair (v6). Ten further full
hiring-appeal texts yielded one omitted recourse safeguard (v7). Both repairs are
minimal and preserve the assigned position. Candidate v2 applies all seven
hash-matched overlays: 54 replacements among 20,000 records; no filtering.

## Follow-up and blind check

Freeze rubric v5 after the three-case development canary. It retains the exact
13 v4 questions and adds material_scope_omission, with source conditions listed
explicitly. Canary original omission=1, repaired=0, intact negative control=0;
these are development model judgments, not calibrated defect probabilities.
Review/high thresholds remain .2/.7. No further tuning on the blind sample.

Apply v5 to the 1,180 original disposition-queue records, using their current v2
texts, plus a fresh 80-document flag-blind sample. The sample excludes every
original queued ID, every known previous agent review/selection ID and all
repair IDs. Seed20261019 selects four positive/four negative documents per
category across the ten categories. Selection and source hashes are saved before
inference. Sampling seed controls selection, not deterministic API prose/scores.

The implementing agent reads all 80 complete blind texts against their briefs
without their model scores. Record and freeze those judgments before joining the
new Decisions outputs. Separately read complete texts for every unresolved
provider response and every serious score >=.2 in the follow-up queue. Confirmed
material defects receive traceable repairs, complete revised-text review and
separate model rechecks; disagreements remain explicit. No canonical overlap,
normative weighting, immaterial exclusion omission or model ethical preference
is itself a defect. A critical omission matters when a standalone document no
longer identifies the same scoped case; e.g. availability of hiring review.

The original queue is not relabeled as manually reviewed. New dispositions state
whether they come from full-text agent review or an advisory model with no serious
flag. Low scores do not prove absence of defects. The final data-use policy must
retain uncertainty and report the blind results; it cannot silently drop or
regenerate disagreeing records. An exposed missed defect must be repaired even
when its model score is low. If the blind check finds an unaddressed systematic
error family, address that family and freeze a fresh confirmation sample before
training; do not tune a detector and call the same sample held out.

## Permitted interpretation

Synthetic ethical text is not an authority on moral correctness. Training tests
whether opposing, matched document assignments alter expressed stance. Every
valid answer and contradictory pair remains in evaluation. Remaining unverified
content risk limits causal interpretation; no 'training-ready' flag follows from
model scores. Experimental use requires an explicit hashed arm/dose/analysis plan
and completed technical training checks. No treatment run starts from this policy
alone.
